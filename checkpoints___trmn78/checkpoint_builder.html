How the six questions are chosen

Portfolio decides what needs evidence; the approved Unit Bank supplies the question architecture. The run uses the current body of evidence at generation time, chooses Bank families that fit each student's next evidence need, and writes fresh question instances. It does not invent a new question structure when an appropriate approved Bank family exists.
