Multiple Representations of Relationships

Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct a table, graph, equation, or context from another representation of the same relationship.
  2. I can use features in one representation to work with a related table, graph, equation, or context.
  3. I can determine whether two representations belong to the same relationship using evidence from values, rates, or patterns.

Work independently. Show enough mathematical evidence for your thinking to be judged.

1
The table shows x: 0,1,2,3 and y: 4,7,10,13. Write an equation and give the next ordered pair.
2
A graph of a relationship contains (0,-2) and rises 5 for every 2 units right. Build a three-row table that belongs to the graph and state the rate.
3
Do y=2x+5 and the table (0,5),(2,9),(4,13),(6,18) represent the same relationship? Use specific evidence and correct any mismatch.
