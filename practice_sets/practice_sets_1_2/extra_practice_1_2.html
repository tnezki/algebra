17. The temperature conversion rule is F = 2C + 30 for this exercise. Find F when C = -8 and show the substitution line with the negative input grouped.

18. A student evaluates x² - 5 at x = -3 as -3² - 5 = -14. Diagnose the substitution error and give the correct value.

19. A student evaluates 30 - 4(3) + 2² by subtracting 4 from 30 first. Identify the first invalid step and compute the correct value.

20. Evaluate (2(7 - 3) + 4) / (3² - 5). Show the numerator value, denominator value, and final quotient.

21. Evaluate 3a - 2b when a = -5 and b = -4. Show how the signs enter the substituted expression before simplifying.

22. Compare -4² and (-4)². Evaluate each expression and explain why the parentheses change the value.

23. A concert hall has 52 rows, each with about 38 seats. A student claims the total is 1,976 seats. Use a quick estimate to decide whether that exact answer is reasonable.
