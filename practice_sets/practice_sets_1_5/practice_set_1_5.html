Algebra 1 · 1.5 Equations as Models · Practice 1

Intro

1. A concert ticket costs $10 plus a $5 service fee. Define a variable for the number of tickets and write an equation for a total of $35.

2. A tank starts with 45 liters and drains 5 liters each minute. Define variables and write an equation for the amount after m minutes.

3. Write an equation for: “Seven more than 5 times a number is 38.” Define the variable.

4. A student writes 5 + 6x = 31 for “six more than 5 times x is 31.” Diagnose the translation error and write the correct equation.

5. Solve 5x + 4 = 34 and show equivalent steps.

6. Solve 5(x - 2) = 35 and check your solution in the original equation.

7. A school club needs $100. It already has $20 and earns $7 per item sold. Write and solve an equation for the number of items needed. Interpret the solution.

8. A ride covers 28 miles. After traveling 9 miles, the remaining distance is modeled by d + 9 = 28. Solve and explain what d means.
