Algebra Check Your Understanding 3.2

1. Solve the system by substitution. $\begin{cases}y=2x-10\\2x+y=6\end{cases}$

2. Solve the system by substitution. $\begin{cases}y=-2x+8\\x+y=5\end{cases}$

3. For the system $y=x+2$ and $2x+y=-7$, identify the most efficient first substitution step before solving.

4. Which method is more efficient for $x+y=5$ and $y=2x-1$: substitution or elimination? Explain the structural cue.

5. A student substitutes $3x+3$ for $y$ in $2x+2y=6$ but writes $2x+2(3x)+3=6$. Identify and correct the substitution error.

6. Check whether $(-1,3)$ satisfies both $y=x+4$ and $x+y=2$.
