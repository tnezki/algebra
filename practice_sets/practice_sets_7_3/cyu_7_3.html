Algebra Check Your Understanding 7.3

1. For $f(x)=4(2)^x$, complete a key-point table for $x=-1,0,1,2$. Use the points to describe the graph as growth or decay.

2. Without graphing every point, identify the $y$-intercept of $f(x)=7(\frac{1}{2})^x$ and explain how you know.

3. State the domain and range of $f(x)=5(3)^x$.

4. A student graphs $y=6(0.7)^x$ with $y$-intercept $(0,6)$ and the curve decreasing toward 0 as $x$ increases. Decide whether the graph is reasonable.

5. Describe what happens to $f(x)=3(\frac{5}{4})^x$ as $x$ becomes very large and as $x$ becomes very negative.

6. For $f(x)=8(\frac{1}{2})^x$, complete a key-point table for $x=-1,0,1,2$ and classify the graph as growth or decay.
