15. For $x^2+15x+54$, build a product-sum table of integer factor pairs of 54 until you find a pair with sum 15. Use that pair to factor the trinomial.

17. One factor of $x^{2} + 14x + 48$ is $(x+6)$. Determine the missing factor.

21. Factor $x^{2} - 15x + 50$.

22. Factor $x^{2} + 2x - 48$.

23. Compare $(x+6)(x+8)$ and $(x+5)(x+9)$. Do they have the same constant term? Do they have the same middle coefficient?

24. A factor pair for a trinomial must have product -42 and sum 1. Can both integers be positive? Explain.
