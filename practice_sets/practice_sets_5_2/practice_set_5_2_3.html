13. Factor $3x^{2} + 13x - 10$.

14. Factor $4x^{2} + 10x + 4$.

15. For $x^2+13x+40$, build a product-sum table of integer factor pairs of 40 until you find a pair with sum 13. Use that pair to factor the trinomial.

16. Use product-sum reasoning to factor $x^2+2x-24$.

17. One factor of $x^{2} + 12x + 35$ is $(x+5)$. Determine the missing factor.

18. Find $m$ so $x^2+13x+m=(x+6)(x+7)$.

19. A student factors $x^2+13x+40$ as $(x+5)(x+8)$. Multiply the factors and decide whether the claim is correct.

20. A rectangle has side lengths $(x+5)$ and $(x+8)$. Write the area in standard form and factored form.

21. Factor $x^{2} - 13x + 36$.

22. Factor $x^{2} + 2x - 35$.

23. Compare $(x+5)(x+7)$ and $(x+4)(x+8)$. Do they have the same constant term? Do they have the same middle coefficient?

24. A factor pair for a trinomial must have product -30 and sum 1. Can both integers be positive? Explain.
