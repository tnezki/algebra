Algebra 1 • Quick Check

Comparing Quadratic Forms

Section 5.5
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct equivalent quadratic forms to reveal different key features.
  2. I can interpret standard, vertex, and factored forms to read key features of quadratic functions.
  3. I can critique which quadratic form best supports a given question or context.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Choose which form best reveals the vertex for this quadratic: standard $y=x^2-2x-3$, vertex $y=(x-1)^2-4$, or factored $y=(x+1)(x-3)$. State the vertex.

2

Which form best reveals the zeros? State the zeros and explain why that form is efficient.

3

Which form best reveals the y-intercept? Use the appropriate form to state it, then explain what each of the other forms makes visible immediately.
