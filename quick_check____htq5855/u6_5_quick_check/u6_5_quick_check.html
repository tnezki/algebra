Quadratic Modeling and Optimization

Section 6.5
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct quadratic models from real-world constraints, data, or key features.
  2. I can extend quadratic models to predict maximums, minimums, intercepts, and meaningful solution values.
  3. I can assess whether a quadratic model and its solutions are reasonable for the situation.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

A rectangle has perimeter 40 m. If one side is $x$, write a quadratic area model in terms of $x$.

2

Use the model to determine the dimensions that maximize area and state the maximum area.

3

State a reasonable domain for the model and explain why some algebraic outputs are not feasible rectangle dimensions.
