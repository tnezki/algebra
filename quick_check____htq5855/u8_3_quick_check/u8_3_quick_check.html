Algebra 1 • Quick Check

Absolute Value Functions and Piecewise Thinking

Section 8.3
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can design an absolute value or piecewise representation for situations with distance, change in direction, or two-part behavior.
  2. I can apply key features of absolute value graphs to interpret vertex, intercepts, and rate of change.
  3. I can predict outputs and intervals from absolute value and piecewise rules in context.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Write an absolute-value rule for a quantity whose output is 2 times the distance of $x$ from the target 5.

2

Graph your rule and identify the vertex, intercept information you can determine, and the rates on the two sides.

3

Use the rule to predict outputs for $x=2$ and $x=8$. Explain why the equal-distance inputs are connected.
