Quadratic Formula

Section 6.3
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can design a coefficient map that organizes $a$, $b$, and $c$ before using the quadratic formula.
  2. I can apply the quadratic formula to solve equations with rational, irrational, or no real solutions.
  3. I can assess how the discriminant predicts the number and type of real solutions.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Create a coefficient map for $x^2+(3)x+(-4)=0$ and substitute correctly into the quadratic formula.

2

Compute the discriminant $b^2-4ac$ first and use it to predict the number/type of real solutions.

3

Finish solving with the quadratic formula. Give exact solutions when possible and decimal approximations when useful.
