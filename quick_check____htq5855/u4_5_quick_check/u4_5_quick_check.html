Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct connections between polynomial structure, quadratic graphs, and key features.
  2. I can predict graph features from equivalent quadratic expressions.
  3. I can critique whether a polynomial expression supports a claimed vertex, intercept, axis of symmetry, or direction of opening.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

The quadratic is $y=1(x+2)^2+0$. State the vertex, axis of symmetry, and direction of opening.

2

Rewrite or expand enough of the expression to predict the y-intercept, then check by substitution.

3

A student claims the graph has x-intercepts because the vertex is below the x-axis. Assess whether the claim is always valid for this function and support your answer with the equation structure.
