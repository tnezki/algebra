Evidence Focus
  1. I can formulate a substitution plan when one equation is already written in an efficient form.
  2. I can apply substitution to solve systems and interpret the ordered-pair solution.
  3. I can assess which algebraic method is more efficient for a given system.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

2

Verify your ordered pair in both original equations.

3

For the system $3x+2y=18$, $y=x+1$, decide whether substitution or elimination is more efficient and explain the structural reason for your choice.
