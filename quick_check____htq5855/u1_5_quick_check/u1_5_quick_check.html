Algebra 1 • Quick Check

Equations as Models

Section 1.5
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct an equation that models a situation using meaningful quantities and operations.
  2. I can use solution steps to solve an equation and connect the result to the situation.
  3. I can predict whether a solution is reasonable by interpreting it in the original context.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

A club charges a one-time fee of \$12 plus \$6 per event. Construct an equation for total cost $C$ after $e$ events.

2

Use your equation to determine how many events give a total cost of \$48. Show solution steps and interpret the result.

3

Before solving a new total of \$54, estimate whether the answer should be greater or less than your previous event count and explain why.
