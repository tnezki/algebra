If two lines have the same slope, must they be the same line?

Graphing Linear Functions

Why This Fits

This graph or data reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will graph linear functions and interpret key features.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • Line A is $y=2x+1$; Line B is $y=2x-4$.
  • The lines are parallel because slopes match and intercepts differ.
  • Any traded graph must respect the grid scale and signed rise/run.

Watch For

  • Plotting the intercept on the wrong axis.
  • Using slope steps without respecting graph scale or sign.

Strong Synthesis

A point and a slope are sufficient; in slope-intercept form, the y-intercept supplies the point and m supplies the slope.
