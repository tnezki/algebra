Predict

What must be added to x²+6x to make one perfect square?

Make a visible prediction, sketch, or physical arrangement before the reveal.
Evidence

Use what you can observe

Students arrange six x-tiles as three along each side of an x² tile, leaving a 3-by-3 corner.

Record the side lengths of the missing corner.
Reveal

Compare with the mathematical model

Nine unit squares complete the square, giving (x+3)².

Discussion

What should we carry forward?

1. Why is the 6 split into two groups of 3?

2. Where does the 9 come from?

3. What feature becomes visible after rewriting in vertex form?
