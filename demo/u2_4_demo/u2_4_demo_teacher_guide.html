Which plan grows faster: the visually steeper line or the line with the larger rate?

Comparing Linear Representations

Why This Fits

This error or contradiction reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will analyze and compare linear relationships using multiple representations.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • Plan A starts at 12 and grows $3/month.\lt /li\gt \lt li\gt Plan B starts at 4 and grows $4/month, so $B=4+4m$.
  • Break-even: 12+3m=4+4m gives m=8; B is cheaper before 8 months, equal at 8, A cheaper after 8.

Watch For

  • Comparing lines only by current output values instead of rate and starting value.
  • Assuming the steeper-looking graph has greater slope without checking axis scales.

Strong Synthesis

Graph scale can distort appearance. Compare numerical slopes/rates and starting values, then use the context/question to decide what matters.
Choose

Which plan grows faster: the visually steeper line or the line with the larger rate?

Two small graphs use different vertical scales; the steeper-looking graph has rate 1.5 while the flatter-looking graph has rate 4.
