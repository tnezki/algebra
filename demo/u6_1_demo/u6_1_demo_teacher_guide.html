Algebra 1 · Section 6.1 Demo Teacher Guide

If x²-5x+6=0, what should the graph of y=x²-5x+6 show?

Solving Quadratics by Factoring

Why This Fits

This graph or data reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will solve quadratic equations using factored form and interpret solutions.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • A factors (x-2)(x-3)=0 → x=2,3.
  • B factors (x+4)(x-2)=0 → x=-4,2.
  • Repeated root (x-3)²=0 → x=3; graph is tangent at the x-axis.

Watch For

  • Applying the zero product property before the equation is set equal to zero.
  • Keeping factor values instead of solving each factor equation for the variable.

Strong Synthesis

Solutions of f(x)=0 should be exactly the x-coordinates where y=f(x) meets the x-axis. A mismatch signals algebra, graph, or equation setup error.
Predict

If x²-5x+6=0, what should the graph of y=x²-5x+6 show?

Make a visible prediction, sketch, or physical arrangement before the reveal.

Teacher notes

Purpose: create a quick commitment before formal instruction. Ask for reasons, not just answers.

Expected/acceptable evidence: A factors (x-2)(x-3)=0 → x=2,3.

Move on when students can state a reason connected to a value, feature, structure, or representation—not merely a guess.

Evidence

Use what you can observe

Students factor to (x-2)(x-3)=0 and predict two x-intercepts.

Record both predicted solutions before the graph appears.

Teacher notes

If later slides depend on this evidence, verify students have recorded the needed information before advancing.

Reveal

Compare with the mathematical model

The graph crosses at x=2 and x=3.

Discussion

What should we carry forward?

1. Why must the equation equal zero before the factor method works?

2. How do solutions relate to x-intercepts?

3. What would one repeated solution look like?
