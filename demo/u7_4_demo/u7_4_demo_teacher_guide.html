A linear model starts higher than an exponential model. Does that mean it stays higher forever?

Comparing Function Families

Why This Fits

This graph or data reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will compare linear, quadratic, and exponential functions using multiple representations.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • L has constant first difference 8.
  • Q has constant second difference 2.
  • E has constant ratio 1.5.

Watch For

  • Using only graph shape instead of differences, ratios, and equation structure.
  • Confusing constant second differences with constant ratios.

Strong Synthesis

A small set of outputs can be close even when the underlying change structures differ. Extending inputs and checking differences/ratios reveals the family behavior.
Predict

A linear model starts higher than an exponential model. Does that mean it stays higher forever?

Make a visible prediction, sketch, or physical arrangement before the reveal.

Teacher notes

Purpose: create a quick commitment before formal instruction. Ask for reasons, not just answers.

Expected/acceptable evidence: L has constant first difference 8.

Move on when students can state a reason connected to a value, feature, structure, or representation—not merely a guess.

Evidence

Use what you can observe

Compare L(x)=20+8x and E(x)=5(1.5)^x for x=0 through 4.

Record the first five outputs for both functions.

Discussion

What should we carry forward?

3. How do first/second differences and ratios distinguish families?
