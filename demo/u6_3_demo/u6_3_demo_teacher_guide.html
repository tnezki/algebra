Quadratic Formula

Why This Fits

This error or contradiction reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will solve quadratic equations using the quadratic formula and interpret solutions.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • Case A: discriminant 25, roots 1/2 and -2.
  • Case B: discriminant 16-28=-12, so no real solutions.
  • Denominator must be the entire 2a, not divide by 2 then multiply by a.

Watch For

  • Substituting signs incorrectly for $a$, $b$, or $c$.
  • Ignoring the discriminant when interpreting the number or type of real solutions.

Strong Synthesis

Positive discriminant → two distinct real roots; zero → one repeated real root; negative → no real roots (complex roots if studied).

Reveal

Test the claim

The numerator begins with -b, so -3 is required; the correct solutions are 1/2 and -2.
