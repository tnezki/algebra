Function Families

Why This Fits

This quick class vote or prediction creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will recognize common function families from graphs, tables, equations, and contexts.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • A is linear: first differences are +3.
  • B is quadratic: first differences 3,5,7 and second differences are constant.
  • C is exponential: ratios are ×2.

Watch For

  • Classifying a family from graph appearance alone without checking differences, ratios, or equation structure.
  • Confusing quadratic growth with exponential growth.

Strong Synthesis

Several families are curved. Strong evidence comes from stable difference/ratio structure, equation form, and family-specific features across more than one representation.
