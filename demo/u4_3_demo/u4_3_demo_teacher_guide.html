Algebra 1 · Unit 4 · Section 4.3 · Demo Teacher Guide

Same Family, Different Shape

Projection hook · designed for 5–8 minutes

Time5–8 minutes
FormatPrediction + evidence debate
Core ideaTransformations

Why This Demo Fits Section 4.3

The current learning target is: Students will analyze and graph transformations involving stretches, compressions, and reflections. The demo creates a short argument around the same representation or decision before formal notes.

Materials

  • Projector; optional mini-whiteboards for predictions

Before Class

  1. Open the projected demo and confirm keyboard/Back/Next navigation works.
  2. Do not pre-teach the formal rule; the purpose is prediction and evidence.

Suggested Flow

MinutesProjected PhaseTeacher Focus
0–1HookRead the challenge and require an initial prediction.
1–3EvidenceAsk students what they notice before naming vocabulary.
3–5Competing claimsPress for evidence rather than votes.
5–7Stress testUse the test to expose incomplete reasoning.
7–8BridgeCarry the final question directly into notes.

Expected Student Thinking

The strongest responses identify the mathematical feature emphasized by the section: Transformation equations using parameters; Graphs showing vertical stretches and compressions; Graphs reflected over an axis.

Watch for: Students deciding from surface appearance or one example. Ask, “What evidence would still work if the numbers changed?”

Transition Into Notes

How can one parameter change the shape while preserving the family?
