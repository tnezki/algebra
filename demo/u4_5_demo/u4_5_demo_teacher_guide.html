What can x²-5x+6 tell us before we graph it?

Polynomial Structure and Quadratic Graphs

Why This Fits

This graph or data reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will connect polynomial expressions to quadratic graphs and key features.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • Positive leading coefficient means opens upward.
  • Zeros are 2 and 3 from factored form.
  • Axis of symmetry is halfway at x=2.5; vertex is below the x-axis.

Watch For

  • Assuming every expression form reveals every graph feature equally well.
  • Confusing the vertex with an intercept or the axis of symmetry with a solution.

Strong Synthesis

Factored form highlights zeros, vertex form highlights vertex/transformations, and standard form highlights coefficients/y-intercept/leading-term structure. Choose based on the question.
