Predict

If f(x)=x², where will the vertex go for f(x-3)+2?

Make a visible prediction, sketch, or physical arrangement before the reveal.
Evidence

Use what you can observe

Students sketch the predicted vertex and one additional translated point on mini whiteboards.

Keep your predicted points visible for the reveal.
Reveal

Compare with the mathematical model

A generated parent/translated graph confirms a shift right 3 and up 2 for every point.

Discussion

What should we carry forward?

1. Why does x-3 move the graph right?

2. Which features stay unchanged under a translation?

3. How can matching one point be insufficient?

We will describe translations by tracking corresponding points, not memorizing isolated sign rules.
