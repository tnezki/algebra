Algebra 1 · Section 5.2 Demo Teacher Guide

Two integers multiply to 12 and add to 7. Why do those numbers factor x²+7x+12?

Factoring Trinomials

Why This Fits

This build then reveal creates a short point of tension that surfaces the section misconception without trying to teach the whole lesson.

Time5–8 minutes
GroupingWhole class / projector
Core ideaStudents will factor quadratic trinomials using patterns, products, sums, and structure.

Materials

Projector/screen; student notebook or mini whiteboard when the slide asks students to record or commit.

Before Class

Open the deck in presentation view. If a reveal figure is used, confirm the local image loads. Do not reveal later slides early.

Suggested Flow

Use the slide-specific notes below. Keep the pace brisk; the goal is noticing and a productive question, not complete instruction.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • A uses 3 and 4: (x+3)(x+4).
  • B uses -4 and 3: (x-4)(x+3), since product -12 and sum -1.
  • For a created monic trinomial, middle coefficient is p+q and constant is pq.

Watch For

  • Using factor pairs that multiply correctly but do not produce the required middle term.
  • Stopping after finding one factor without verifying the full product.

Strong Synthesis

Expanding (x+p)(x+q) gives x²+(p+q)x+pq, so their sum becomes the x-coefficient and product becomes the constant.

Discussion

What should we carry forward?

1. Which number controls the constant term?

2. Which combination creates the middle coefficient?

3. How do signs change when the constant is negative?
