Section 1.1

Multiple Representations of Relationships

Day 1

1 · Solve / Equation Fluency

  1. \(x+9=23\)
  2. \(y-7=5\)

Complete the diamond.

3 · Skill Review

Try without a calculator first. Use a calculator if you need one.

4 · Challenge

A coordinate pattern moves from \((-2,1)\) to \((1,3)\) by adding 3 to the x-coordinate and 2 to the y-coordinate. What is the next point? Explain how you know.
