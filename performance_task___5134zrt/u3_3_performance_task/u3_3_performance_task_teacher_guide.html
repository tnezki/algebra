Evidence Focus
  1. I can build a system of equations that represents two constraints in a situation.
  2. I can predict the meaning of a solution before solving from the context and variables.
  3. I can design a solution pathway that connects equations, calculations, and a contextual conclusion.

Complete the connected performance. Shared setup or exchange is allowed only where the task says so; record your own mathematical evidence and final conclusions.

Create a Consistent Evidence Set

Create a ticket/count, mixture, or cost situation with two independent constraints and one sensible integer solution. Keep the equations hidden for the trade.
Sample standard: all created representations describe the same relationship; hidden information can be reconstructed uniquely from the evidence provided.
Partner reconstruction should be independent. Final revision explains any mismatch rather than simply replacing an answer.
Accept different valid values/contexts if the mathematics remains consistent and the exact I-can evidence is visible.
Section-specific sample: Student-created systems vary. A strong example has two genuinely independent constraints, a sensible solution, and a partner reconstruction whose equations reproduce both original conditions.

Prepare the Exchange

Copy or preserve your full solution, then give a partner a deliberately incomplete version that hides exactly one important representation or relationship. Do not hide so much that reconstruction becomes guessing.
