Audit the Evidence

Read the evidence packet below. Identify the exact mismatch or unsupported claim. Record what is valid before you change anything.
For $y\lt 2x+4$, a student draws a solid boundary and shades the region containing $(0,0)$.
Look for: an explicitly named mismatch, not just “this is wrong.”
The repair must satisfy the original representation/constraint and be checked with substitution, points, or another representation.
A strong defense cites section evidence tied to: I can construct a boundary line and shaded region for a linear inequality.
Section-specific sample: For $y\lt 2x+4$ the boundary must be dashed. The point $(0,0)$ satisfies $0\lt 4$, so shading that contains the origin is correct; the solid boundary is the repair.
