Predict the overtake story

Explain why exponential growth eventually exceeds the other two here, using structure rather than one data point.

Teacher evidence / sample: Repeated multiplication by 2 compounds faster than constant additive or quadratic second-difference growth for sufficiently large x.

Critique a claim

A student says “quadratic always grows faster than linear.” State a reasonable qualification.

Teacher evidence / sample: For these positive-domain models, Q eventually exceeds L, but not necessarily at every input; behavior depends on coefficients/domain and can include decreasing portions.
