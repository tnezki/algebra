Audit the Evidence

Read the evidence packet below. Identify the exact mismatch or unsupported claim. Record what is valid before you change anything.
For $2x^2-5x-3=0$, a student maps $a=2,b=5,c=-3$ and computes the discriminant as $25-24=1$.
Look for: an explicitly named mismatch, not just “this is wrong.”
The repair must satisfy the original representation/constraint and be checked with substitution, points, or another representation.
A strong defense cites section evidence tied to: I can design a coefficient map that organizes $a$, $b$, and $c$ before using the quadratic formula.
Section-specific sample: The correct coefficient map is $a=2,b=-5,c=-3$. The discriminant is $25+24=49$, giving roots $x=3$ and $x=-1/2$.
