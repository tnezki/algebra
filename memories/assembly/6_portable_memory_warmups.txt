DIAMOND PROBLEM RULES — ALL UNITS

Problem 4 is ALWAYS a generated diamond in EVERY unit.
Every diamond uses graph_tool.make_diamond().

For every diamond:
top number = product of the left and right numbers
bottom number = sum of the left and right numbers

Two separate rotations are required:

1. V-type controls the number/sign pattern.
2. P-type controls which diamond cells are given.

Do not let V-type control placement. Placement is controlled only by P-type.

HARD RULES:

* No two consecutive warmups may use the same V-type.
* No two consecutive warmups may use the same P-type.
* No factor pair may repeat across the entire unit.
* Include all six P-types across the unit.
* Do not overuse only top/bottom, top/left, and left/bottom.
* Rotate signs: include positive/positive, positive/negative, and negative/negative.
* Zero as a factor may appear at most once in the unit.

V-types — number/sign pattern:
V1: both factors positive
V2: one factor positive and one factor negative
V3: both factors negative
V4: larger product or less-obvious factor pair
V5: special case, such as a zero factor, used at most once or twice

P-types — given-cell placement:
P1: given left + right → find top product + bottom sum
P2: given top + bottom → find left/right factors
P3: given top + left → find right factor + bottom sum
P4: given top + right → find left factor + bottom sum
P5: given left + bottom → find right factor + top product
P6: given bottom + right → find left factor + top product

Required spread across 15 warmups:
V1: 3–4 times
V2: 3–4 times
V3: 3–4 times
V4: 2–3 times
V5: 1–2 times

P1: 2–3 times
P2: 2–3 times
P3: 2–3 times
P4: 2–3 times
P5: 2–3 times
P6: 2–3 times

Number constraints:
Factors: integers –12 to 12
Products: –100 to 100
Sums: –20 to 20
No fractions

Suggested 15-warmup V-type sequence:
1-1-1:V1  1-1-2:V2  1-1-3:V3
1-2-1:V4  1-2-2:V1  1-2-3:V2
1-3-1:V3  1-3-2:V4  1-3-3:V1
1-4-1:V2  1-4-2:V3  1-4-3:V4
1-5-1:V1  1-5-2:V2  1-5-3:V5

Suggested 15-warmup P-type sequence:
1-1-1:P1  1-1-2:P2  1-1-3:P4
1-2-1:P6  1-2-2:P3  1-2-3:P5
1-3-1:P1  1-3-2:P4  1-3-3:P2
1-4-1:P6  1-4-2:P5  1-4-3:P3
1-5-1:P1  1-5-2:P4  1-5-3:P6
