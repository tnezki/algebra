COMMON POLISH FAILURE MODES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1.  Reporting passes clean without actually listing verbs
2.  Fixing I-can verbs without re-tallying the full 15
3.  Rewriting rubric boilerplate when only first sentence needs changing

5.  Changing task names or timing labels during a verb fix
6.  Fixing verb spread by clustering fixes in one DOK level

8.  Skipping the spread check on formative verbs (all-one-type DOK level)
9.  Using the mid-course spiral sentence for end-of-course units or vice versa
10. Stopping mid-pass to ask questions — fix and note in report
