THE TWO OUTCOMES — READ THIS CAREFULLY

For each flagged image there are exactly two outcomes.

OUTCOME 1 — AGREE: Fix it.

Claude agrees the item has a genuine problem.

Apply the fix using minimum change:
  — Only change what resolves the specific issue.
  — Do not rewrite the whole item.
  — Do not restructure surrounding items.

     unless the fix requires it.

Fixes may include (but are not limited to):
  — Graph title: change title= to '' or a generic descriptor

  — Question that gives the answer away: minimum reword to
    separate the ask from the embedded answer.
  — Bad DOK pattern (5a–5n): smallest fix that resolves it.

State the fix clearly:

Claude believes the item is acceptable as written.

State the outcome clearly:

HOW TO EVALUATE EACH FLAGGED ITEM

STEP 1 — IDENTIFY
  Read the item ID from the screenshot header.
  Locate that exact item in the bank HTML.
  Read the full question text, any table, any figure reference.

STEP 2 — UNDERSTAND THE CONCERN
  What does Troy appear to be flagging?
  Common concerns from past reviews:
    Graph title gives away the equation or answer.
    Table says "complete it" but cells are pre-filled.
    Answer is visible in the graph, label, or column header.
    Context numbers produce implausible real-world outputs.
    Item seems too easy for its DOK level.
    Question phrasing is ambiguous or confusing.

STEP 3 — EVALUATE INDEPENDENTLY
  Do not assume Troy is right just because he flagged it.
  Ask: Is this a genuine violation of a quality standard,
  or does the item work despite its surface appearance?

  Genuine violation — examples:
    Graph title r'$f(x)=4(0.5)^x$' on a DOK1 item asking
    "decide whether the multiplier is > 1 or between 0 and 1."
    → The title answers the question. This is 5b-graph-title.

    Item says "complete the table" but all cells are filled.
    → Student has nothing to do. This is 5m.

    Item asks student to "write the factored equation" and
    graph title shows y=(x-1)(x-6).
    → Title is the answer. This is 5b-graph-title.

  Potentially acceptable — examples:
    A DOK1 item where the graph title shows the equation but
    the item asks the student to compute a specific output
    value, not identify the equation. The title helps them
    check — it doesn't give away the task.
    → Might be acceptable depending on the exact ask.

    A context with large numbers that are still realistic
    (e.g., a city of 500,000 people with 2% annual growth).
    → Not a 5n violation even if the numbers feel large.

STEP 4 — APPLY THE OUTCOME
  AGREE → fix it immediately, document the fix.
  DISCUSS → explain and ask before touching anything.

GENERATE_GRAPHS.PY — FIX RULES

When fixing a graph title:
  Change ONLY the title= parameter in the matching graph call.
  Do not change the filename, function arguments, or data.
  Do not modify any line above the append marker.
  The figure file will be overwritten when user re-runs py.

When a figure is shared across multiple items:
  Evaluate ALL items using that graph before clearing the title.
  If any item has a title violation, clear the title.
  Removing a title from a DOK 1 "read the graph" item makes
  the task slightly more authentic — it does not break it.
