Q3 2.5/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 37 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 25 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

table (0,37),(4,25); -12/3; -4 meters per second; -4m/s (circled)

Feedback

Your work shows some correct rate or direction reasoning. Check the sign and divide the position change by 4 seconds: (25-37)/4=-3 m/s, decreasing.

Q4a 5/5

Write and interpret a linear model

A club costs $12 to join and $3 per meeting.

Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost.

a. Write an equation for C in terms of n.

Your work

table (0,12),(1,15); C=3n+12 (circled)

Feedback

Correct linear model: C=3n+12. The slope is 3 and starting value is 12.
