Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2p^{3}y^{5})(4py^{2})\)

Answer: \(8 p^{4} y^{7}\)

Multiply coefficients: 2×4=8.

Add each variable’s exponents: 3+1=4 and 5+2=7.

\(8 p^{4} y^{7}\)

4. Review & Preview variation

Use slope and y-intercept to graph the line.

\(y = - \frac{x}{2} - 4\)

Answer: Slope \(- \frac{1}{2}\); y-intercept -4

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\((p^{-6})(p^{5})\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p}\)

Combine exponents: −6+5=-1.

\(\frac{1}{p}\)

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{p^{-2}}{y^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{2}}{p^{2}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{2}}{p^{2}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-7-8-11-12

Answer: \(y=-x-9\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -9.

\(y=-x-9\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1000? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(15\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-30w=1000\)

Subtract: 30w=450.

Divide: w=15.
