Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{6a^{5}}{3a^{2}}\)

Answer: \(2 a^{3}\)

Divide the coefficients: 6/3=2.

Subtract exponents: 5−2=3.

\(2 a^{3}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3p^{7})^{3}\)

Answer: \(27 p^{21}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 3: 3^3=27.

Multiply exponents: 7×3=21.

\(27 p^{21}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

a.
\(\frac{x^{91}}{x^{10}}\Rightarrow x^{83}\)
b.
\(y^{81}\cdot y^{10}\Rightarrow y^{91}\)
c.
\((5m^{10})^2\Rightarrow 5m^{20}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^81; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 25m^20.

4. Review & Preview variation

A collector starts with 95 cards and collects 16 more cards each week. Write an expression for the collection size after w weeks.

Answer: \(95+16w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(95+16w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(\left(\frac{4a}{4}\right)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{p^{5}}{p^{10}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{5}}\)

5−10=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{p^{5}}\)
