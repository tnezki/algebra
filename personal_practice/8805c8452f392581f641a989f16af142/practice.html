\(3 x^{5}\)

\((4p^{5}y^{2})(2py)\)

Answer: \(8 p^{6} y^{3}\)

Multiply coefficients: 4×2=8.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+1=6 and 2+1=3.

\(8 p^{6} y^{3}\)
