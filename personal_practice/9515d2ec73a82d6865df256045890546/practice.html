Lesson 3.1.2

1. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(8x^{-7}y^{2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{8y^{2}}{x^{7}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{8y^{2}}{x^{7}}\)

2. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((a^{6})^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

4. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $40 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1010? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(11\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-40w=1010\)

Subtract: 40w=440.

Divide: w=11.
