3.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $35 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $890? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-35w=890\)

Subtract: 35w=560.

Divide: w=16.
