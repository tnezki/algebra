Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{4}y^{2})^{3}\)

Answer: \(27 x^{12} y^{6}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^3, x^(4×3), y^(2×3).

\(27 x^{12} y^{6}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{x^{8}}{x^{4}}\)

Answer: \(x^{4}\)

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(x^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(8a^{5})^0+(3a)^0}{(a^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\((p^{-2})(p^{4})\)

Answer: \(p^{2}\)

Combine exponents: −2+4=2.

\(p^{2}\)
