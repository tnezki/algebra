Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((-2y^{6})(3y^{4})\)

Answer: \(- 6 y^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-2)(3)=-6.

Add exponents of y: 6+4=10.

\(- 6 y^{10}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{4}y)^{2}\)

Answer: \(9 x^{8} y^{2}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^2, x^(4×2), y^(1×2).

\(9 x^{8} y^{2}\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $25 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $2100? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(10\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-25w=2100\)

Subtract: 25w=250.

Divide: w=10.
