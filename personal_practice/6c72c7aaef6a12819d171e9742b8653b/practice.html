Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{12x^{4}y^{3}}{3x^{2}y^{2}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y\)

Divide coefficients: 12/3=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(4−2) and y^(3−2).

\(4 x^{2} y\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((x^{8})(x^{4})\)

Answer: \(x^{12}\)

The base is the same; add the exponents: 8+4=12.

\(x^{12}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

An account has $213. A student withdraws $7 per week. Write an expression for the balance after w weeks, and state when the model makes sense.

Answer: \(213-7w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(213-7w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(3a^{4})^0+(2a)^0}{(2a^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{4}}{y^{9}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{5}}\)

4−9=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{y^{5}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $25 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(18\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-25w=1900\)

Subtract: 25w=450.

Divide: w=18.
