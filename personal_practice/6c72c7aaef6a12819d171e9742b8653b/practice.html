Answer: \(8 x^{24} y^{3}\)

\(8 x^{24} y^{3}\)

2.
Fill in the missing exponent.

\(y^{4}\cdot y^{6}=y^{\square}\)

Answer: \(y^{10}\)

The base is the same; add the exponents: 4+6=10.

\(y^{10}\)
