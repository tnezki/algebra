Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((-2x^{4}y^{4})(2x^{5}y^{6})\)

Answer: \(- 4 x^{9} y^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: -2×2=-4.

Add each variable’s exponents: 4+5=9 and 4+6=10.

\(- 4 x^{9} y^{10}\)

3.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Rewrite each expression using exponent properties.

a. \(\frac{h^{6}}{h^{11}}\)b. \(x^{2}\cdot x^{7}\)c. \((2k^{3})^{2}\)d. \(n^{3}\cdot n\)e. \(\frac{42x^{3}y^{6}}{7x^{3}}\)f. \(3xy^{5}\cdot 4x^{2}y^{2}\)

Answer: <p class="rp-parts"><span><strong>a.</strong> \(\frac{1}{h^{5}}\)</span><span><strong>b.</strong> \(x^{9}\)</span><span><strong>c.</strong> \(4k^{6}\)</span><span><strong>d.</strong> \(n^{4}\)</span><span><strong>e.</strong> \(6y^{6}\)</span><span><strong>f.</strong> \(12x^{3}y^{7}\)</span></p>

a: subtract the exponents in the quotient.

b and d: add exponents when multiplying like bases.

c: raise both 2 and the power on k to 2.

e: divide coefficients and cancel matching powers of x.

f: multiply coefficients and add like-base exponents.

\(y^{58}\cdot y^{17}\Rightarrow y^{75}\)

7.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-4-5-8-9

Answer: \(y=-x-6\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -6.

\(y=-x-6\)

8.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $35 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $890? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-35w=890\)

Subtract: 35w=560.

Divide: w=16.
