Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{y^{11}}{y^{5}}\)

Answer: \(y^{6}\)

Subtract exponents: 11−5=6.

\(y^{6}\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((2x^{3}y^{2})(4x^{3}y^{4})\)

Answer: \(8 x^{6} y^{6}\)

Multiply coefficients: 2×4=8.

Add each variable’s exponents: 3+3=6 and 2+4=6.

\(8 x^{6} y^{6}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Simplify each expression. Express answers as integers or improper fractions.

a.
\(-\frac{8}{9}-\frac{4}{11}\)
b.
\(-\frac{5}{6}-6\)
c.
\(\left(-\frac{11}{6}\right)(-4)\)
d.
\(-4\div\frac{5}{6}\)

Answer: a. \(-\frac{124}{99}\); b. \(-\frac{41}{6}\); c. \(\frac{22}{3}\); d. \(-\frac{24}{5}\)

Part a: use a common denominator; the result is \(-\frac{124}{99}\).

For other parts use signed fraction operations and reduce.

4. Review & Preview variation

Use slope and y-intercept to graph the line.

\(y = \frac{x}{2} - 3\)

Answer: Slope \(\frac{1}{2}\); y-intercept -3

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(p^{-7}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(5p^{6})^0+(4p)^0}{(4p^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.
