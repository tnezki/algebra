Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((-2x^{5}y^{2})(-2xy^{2})\)

Answer: \(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

Multiply coefficients: -2×-2=4.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+1=6 and 2+2=4.

\(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{8x^{6}y^{5}}{4x^{4}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 8/4=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(5−3).

\(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(7x^{7})^0+(3x)^0}{(x^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{-2}}{y^{-5}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{5}}{x^{2}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{5}}{x^{2}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-7-8-11-12

Answer: \(y=-x-9\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -9.

\(y=-x-9\)
