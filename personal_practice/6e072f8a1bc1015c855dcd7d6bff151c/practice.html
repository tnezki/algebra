Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{16x^{5}y^{3}}{4x^{3}y}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 16/4=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(5−3) and y^(3−1).

\(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)
