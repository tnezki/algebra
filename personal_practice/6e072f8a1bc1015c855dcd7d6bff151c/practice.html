Lesson 3.1.2

5.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(a^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a^{2}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{a^{2}}\)

6.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{8x^{-1}y}{2y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{4 y^{2}}{x}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{4 y^{2}}{x}\)

7.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1550 in the bank and spends $20 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1270? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(14\text{ weeks}\)

\(1550-20w=1270\)

Subtract: 20w=280.

Divide: w=14.
