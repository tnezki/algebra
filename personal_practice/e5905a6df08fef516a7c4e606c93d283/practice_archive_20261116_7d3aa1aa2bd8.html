My work (transcribed)

y^6+y^2=y^12

Teacher-approved feedback

The written result does not give y^4. For equal bases being divided, subtract the denominator exponent from the numerator exponent.

Q1c 3.5/5
Original question

Simplify each expression to one power. (z^4)^3.

My work (transcribed)

(z^4)^3=z^(4*3) [not simplified]

Teacher-approved feedback

Partial method or an unfinished simplification. Multiply the inner and outer exponents; do not add them. Expected z^12; check the written steps.

Q2a 3/5
Original question

Rewrite each expression without zero or negative exponents. n^0.

My work (transcribed)

n^1 or 1

Teacher-approved feedback

One of your alternatives, 1, is correct, but n^1 is generally not equal to 1. The single simplified answer for n^0 is 1 (n != 0).

Q2b 0/5
Original question

Rewrite each expression without zero or negative exponents. k^-4.

My work (transcribed)

1/k^-4=1; I am not sure

Teacher-approved feedback

The written result does not give 1/k^4 (assuming a nonzero base). A negative exponent means take the reciprocal and use a positive exponent; it does not make the expression negative.

Q3 1.5/5
Original question

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 39 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 55 meters. Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second. Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

My work (transcribed)

0->39,4->55, +13 [incorrect]; 4/13; It is increasing

Teacher-approved feedback

Use final minus initial position, then divide by elapsed time: (55-39)/4=4 m/s. A positive rate means increasing position.

Q4a 0/5
Original question

A club costs $5 to join and $2 per meeting. Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost. a. Write an equation for C in terms of n.

My work (transcribed)

C = 5 · 2 = 10

Teacher-approved feedback

The expression 5 times 2 is not a model for the number of meetings. The correct relationship is C = 2n + 5.

Q4b 0/5
Original question

A club costs $5 to join and $2 per meeting. Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost. b. What does the number added at the end of your equation mean?

My work (transcribed)

The number of the meetings

Teacher-approved feedback

This confuses the starting cost/value with the rate or total. The added constant represents the $5 one-time joining fee (the cost at n=0).
