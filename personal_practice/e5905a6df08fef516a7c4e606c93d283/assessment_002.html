Q3

Signed rate of change (motion)

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

0->39,4->55, +13 [incorrect]; 4/13; It is increasing

Feedback

Use final minus initial position, then divide by elapsed time: (55-39)/4=4 m/s. A positive rate means increasing position.

Q4a

A club costs $5 to join and $2 per meeting.

Write and interpret a linear model

A club costs $5 to join and $2 per meeting.

Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost.

a. Write an equation for C in terms of n.

Your work

C = 5 · 2 = 10

Feedback

The expression 5 times 2 is not a model for the number of meetings. The correct relationship is C = 2n + 5.
