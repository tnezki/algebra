Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2a^{3})(2a)\)

Answer: \(4 a^{4}\)

Multiply coefficients: (2)(2)=4.

Add exponents of a: 3+1=4.

\(4 a^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2x^{4}y^{3})^{2}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{8} y^{6}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^2, x^(4×2), y^(3×2).

\(4 x^{8} y^{6}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A trail has 179 miles left to walk. A hiker completes 20 miles each day. Write an expression for the distance remaining after d days.

Answer: \(179-20d\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(179-20d\)

4. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{42}}{x^{13}}\Rightarrow x^{31}\)
b.
\(y^{29}\cdot y^{13}\Rightarrow y^{42}\)
c.
\((7m^{13})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{26}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^29; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^26.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{-2}}{y^{-7}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{7}}{y^{2}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{7}}{y^{2}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(a^{-5}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-7-8-11-12

Answer: \(y=-x-9\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -9.

\(y=-x-9\)
