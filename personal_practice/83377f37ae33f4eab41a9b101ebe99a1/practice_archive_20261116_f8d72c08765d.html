Q3 4.5/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 45 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 29 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

45-29=16; 16/4=4; -4 every second; decreasing

Feedback

Correct signed rate and motion interpretation: (29-45)/4=-4 m/s, decreasing.

Q4a 5/5

Write and interpret a linear model

A club costs $11 to join and $3 per meeting.

Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost.

a. Write an equation for C in terms of n.

Your work

C=3n+11 [earlier scribbled attempt]

Feedback

Correct linear model: C=3n+11. The slope is 3 and starting value is 11.
