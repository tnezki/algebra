Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2y^{8})^{4}\)

Answer: \(16 y^{32}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 4: 2^4=16.

Multiply exponents: 8×4=32.

\(16 y^{32}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{x^{8}}{x^{4}}\)

Answer: \(x^{4}\)

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(x^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(a^{-4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a^{4}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{a^{4}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(7y^{7})^0+(3y)^0}{(y^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $35 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $890? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-35w=890\)

Subtract: 35w=560.

Divide: w=16.
