Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{12x^{6}y^{5}}{3x^{4}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 12/3=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(5−3).

\(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Answer: \(185+7w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(185+7w\)

5.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{6}}{x^{11}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{5}}\)

6−11=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{x^{5}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{5y^{2}}{x^{5}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{5y^{2}}{x^{5}}\)

7.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1850 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1490? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(12\text{ weeks}\)

\(1850-30w=1490\)

Subtract: 30w=360.

Divide: w=12.
