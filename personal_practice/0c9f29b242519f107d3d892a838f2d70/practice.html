1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Then select the property used.

\((2x^{2}y^{4})(3x^{7}y^{7})\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

Answer: \(6x^{9}y^{11}\); A. Product of Powers

Use the product of powers rule.

\(6x^{9}y^{11}\)

Answer choice A.

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{5}y)^{2}\)

Answer: \(9 x^{10} y^{2}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^2, x^(5×2), y^(1×2).

\(9 x^{10} y^{2}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A trail has 214 miles left to walk. A hiker completes 7 miles each day. Write an expression for the distance remaining after d days.

Answer: \(214-7d\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(214-7d\)

Answer: <p class="rp-parts"><span><strong>a.</strong> \(\frac{1}{h^{4}}\)</span><span><strong>b.</strong> \(x^{11}\)</span><span><strong>c.</strong> \(9k^{6}\)</span><span><strong>d.</strong> \(n^{3}\)</span><span><strong>e.</strong> \(5y^{6}\)</span><span><strong>f.</strong> \(4x^{6}y^{5}\)</span></p>

b and d: add exponents when multiplying like bases.

e: divide coefficients and cancel matching powers of x.

f: multiply coefficients and add like-base exponents.
