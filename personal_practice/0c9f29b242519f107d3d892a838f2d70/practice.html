Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify.

\((3a^{6})^{4}\)

Answer: \(81 a^{24}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 4: 3^4=81.

Multiply exponents: 6×4=24.

\(81 a^{24}\)

2.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify. Then select the property used.

\((2x^{2}y^{2})(3x^{7}y^{7})\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

\(6x^{9}y^{9}\)

3.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

A trail has 101 miles left to walk. A hiker completes 15 miles each day. Write an expression for the distance remaining after d days.

Answer: \(101-15d\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(101-15d\)

Simplify each expression. Express answers as integers or improper fractions.

a.
\(-\frac{2}{5}-\frac{1}{4}\)
b.
\(-\frac{4}{8}-3\)
c.
\(\left(-\frac{7}{8}\right)(-5)\)
d.
\(-5\div\frac{4}{8}\)

Answer: a. \(-\frac{13}{20}\); b. \(-\frac{7}{2}\); c. \(\frac{35}{8}\); d. \(-10\)

Part a: use a common denominator; the result is \(-\frac{13}{20}\).

For other parts use signed fraction operations and reduce.
