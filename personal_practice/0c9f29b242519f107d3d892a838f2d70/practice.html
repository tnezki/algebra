Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{2}y^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(9 x^{4} y^{4}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^2, x^(2×2), y^(2×2).

\(9 x^{4} y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{4x^{5}y^{6}}{2x^{3}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)

Divide coefficients: 4/2=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(5−3) and y^(6−3).

\(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)
