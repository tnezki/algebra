1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Multiply the expressions below.

\(5y^{4}\)
\(-2y\)

Answer: \(- 10 y^{5}\)

Multiply coefficients: (5)(-2)=-10.

Add exponents of y: 4+1=5.

\(- 10 y^{5}\)

4.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Use slope and y-intercept to graph the line.

\(y = \frac{x}{2} - 3\)

Answer: Slope \(\frac{1}{2}\); y-intercept -3

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
