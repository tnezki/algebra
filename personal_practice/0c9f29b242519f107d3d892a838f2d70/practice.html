Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{25x^{10}}{5x^{6}}\)

Answer: \(5 x^{4}\)

Divide the coefficients: 25/5=5.

Subtract exponents: 10−6=4.

\(5 x^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((4y^{5})(2y^{4})\)

Answer: \(8 y^{9}\)

Multiply coefficients: (4)(2)=8.

Add exponents of y: 5+4=9.

\(8 y^{9}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Simplify each expression. Express answers as integers or improper fractions.

a.
\(-\frac{3}{4}-\frac{2}{7}\)
b.
\(-\frac{6}{3}-4\)
c.
\(\left(-\frac{10}{3}\right)(-3)\)
d.
\(-3\div\frac{6}{3}\)

Answer: a. \(-\frac{29}{28}\); b. \(-6\); c. \(10\); d. \(-\frac{3}{2}\)

Part a: use a common denominator; the result is \(-\frac{29}{28}\).

For other parts use signed fraction operations and reduce.

4. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{80}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{73}\)
b.
\(y^{71}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{80}\)
c.
\((7m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{18}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^71; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^18.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.
