Lesson 3.1.2

1. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{a^{5}}{a^{10}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

5−10=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

2. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(\left(\frac{4x}{3}\right)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

3. Review & Preview variation

Find the slope and equation of the graphed line.

Answer: \(y = \frac{2 x}{3} + 3\)

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
