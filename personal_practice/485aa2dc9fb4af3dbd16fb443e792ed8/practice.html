Lesson 3.1.2

1. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{1}{p^{-5}}\)

Answer: \(p^{5}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(p^{5}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\((y^{-3})(y^{2})\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y}\)

Combine exponents: −3+2=-1.

\(\frac{1}{y}\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope 3.

The intercept is 4.
