Q3 4/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 41 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 57 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

41+16=57; 16/4=4; 4 meters per second [no explicit word increasing]

Feedback

Your work shows some correct rate or direction reasoning. Check the sign and divide the position change by 4 seconds: (57-41)/4=4 m/s, increasing.

Q4a 5/5

Write and interpret a linear model

A club costs $8 to join and $5 per meeting.

Let n be the number of meetings and C the total cost.

a. Write an equation for C in terms of n.

Your work

C=5n+8

Feedback

Correct linear model: C=5n+8. The slope is 5 and starting value is 8.
