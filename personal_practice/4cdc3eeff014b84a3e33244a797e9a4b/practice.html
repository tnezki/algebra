Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((3x^{5}y)(2x^{7}y^{5})\)

Answer: \(6 x^{12} y^{6}\)

Multiply coefficients: 3×2=6.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+7=12 and 1+5=6.

\(6 x^{12} y^{6}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{x^{8}}{x^{4}}\)

Answer: \(x^{4}\)

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(x^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(x^{-6}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{6}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{x^{6}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{2x^{-7}y^{2}}{2y^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{4}}{x^{7}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{y^{4}}{x^{7}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Rewrite each expression below without negative or zero exponents.

a. \(3^{-1}\)b. \(4^0\)c. \(3^{-2}\)d. \(x^{-4}\)

Answer: <p class="rp-parts"><span><strong>a.</strong> \(\frac{1}{3}\)</span><span><strong>b.</strong> \(1\)</span><span><strong>c.</strong> \(\frac{1}{9}\)</span><span><strong>d.</strong> \(\frac{1}{x^{4}}\)</span></p>

A negative exponent creates a reciprocal.

A nonzero base to the zero power is 1.

Use positive exponents in each result.
