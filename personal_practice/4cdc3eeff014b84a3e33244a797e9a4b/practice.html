Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Write as a single power.

\(p^{7}\cdot p\)

Answer: \(p^{8}\)

The base is the same; add the exponents: 7+1=8.

\(p^{8}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{12x^{4}y^{8}}{3x^{2}y^{4}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Divide coefficients: 12/3=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(4−2) and y^(8−4).

\(4 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{-3}}{y^{-6}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{6}}{x^{3}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{6}}{x^{3}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((p^{5})^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $25 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $2100? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(10\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-25w=2100\)

Subtract: 25w=250.

Divide: w=10.
