1.
Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{p^{6}}{p^{4}}\)

Answer: \(p^{2}\)

Subtract exponents: 6−4=2.

\(p^{2}\)

2.
Multiply.

\((-3p^{4})(2p)\)

Answer: \(- 6 p^{5}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-3)(2)=-6.

Add exponents of p: 4+1=5.

\(- 6 p^{5}\)

3.
Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.
a.
\(\frac{x^{73}}{x^{11}}\Rightarrow x^{64}\)
b.
\(y^{62}\cdot y^{11}\Rightarrow y^{73}\)
c.
\((7m^{11})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{22}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^62; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^22.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(214-7d\)

5.

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{2}}\)

\(\frac{1}{p^{2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{2}}{x^{6}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{2}}{x^{6}}\)

7.
Samantha has $1250 in the bank and spends $50 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(7\text{ weeks}\)

\(1250-50w=900\)

Subtract: 50w=350.

Divide: w=7.
