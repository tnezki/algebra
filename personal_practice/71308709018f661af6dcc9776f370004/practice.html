Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply the expressions below.

\(2a^{7}\)
\(-2a\)

Answer: \(- 4 a^{8}\)

Multiply coefficients: (2)(-2)=-4.

Add exponents of a: 7+1=8.

\(- 4 a^{8}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{15p^{11}}{3p^{7}}\)

Answer: \(5 p^{4}\)

Divide the coefficients: 15/3=5.

Subtract exponents: 11−7=4.

\(5 p^{4}\)

a.
\(\frac{x^{33}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{26}\)
b.
\(y^{24}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{33}\)
c.
\((5m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 5m^{18}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(6x^{-5}y^{2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{6y^{2}}{x^{5}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{6y^{2}}{x^{5}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\((a^{-4})(a^{3})\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a}\)

Combine exponents: −4+3=-1.

\(\frac{1}{a}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-7-8-11-12

Answer: \(y=-x-9\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -9.

\(y=-x-9\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1000? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(15\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-30w=1000\)

Subtract: 30w=450.

Divide: w=15.
