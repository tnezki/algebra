Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

\((2y^{4})^{3}\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

Answer: \(8y^{12}\); C. Power of a Power

Use the power of a power rule.

\(8y^{12}\)

Answer choice C.

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2p^{3}y^{5})(4py^{2})\)

Answer: \(8 p^{4} y^{7}\)

Multiply coefficients: 2×4=8.

Add each variable’s exponents: 3+1=4 and 5+2=7.

\(8 p^{4} y^{7}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(7y^{7})^0+(3y)^0}{(y^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(4a^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{4}{a^{2}}\)

The coefficient 4 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −2.

\(\frac{4}{a^{2}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $25 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $2100? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(10\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-25w=2100\)

Subtract: 25w=250.

Divide: w=10.
