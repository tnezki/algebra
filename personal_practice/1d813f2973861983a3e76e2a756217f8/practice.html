2. Direct Skills

Write as one power.

\((p^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(p^{4}\)

Multiply the exponents: 2×2=4.

\(p^{4}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A savings account starts with $186. A student deposits $8 each week. Write an expression for its balance after w weeks, defining w.

Answer: \(186+8w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(186+8w\)

4. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{33}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{26}\)
b.
\(y^{24}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{33}\)
c.
\((5m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 5m^{18}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^24; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 25m^18.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(8^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(x^{-4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{4}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{x^{4}}\)
