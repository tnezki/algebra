A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

6.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(\left(\frac{7x}{3}\right)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

7.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1250 in the bank and spends $50 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(7\text{ weeks}\)

\(1250-50w=900\)

Subtract: 50w=350.

Divide: w=7.
