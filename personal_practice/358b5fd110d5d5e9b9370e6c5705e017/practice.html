Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{20y^{8}}{4y^{4}}\)

Answer: \(5 y^{4}\)

Divide the coefficients: 20/4=5.

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(5 y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Write as one power.

\((p^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(p^{4}\)

Multiply the exponents: 2×2=4.

\(p^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(p^{-7}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((p^{2})^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-3-4-7-8

Answer: \(y=-x-5\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -5.

\(y=-x-5\)
