  5. Negative Exponent

Use the power of a power rule.

\(4y^{12}\)

Answer choice C.

2. Direct Skills

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((-2x^{5}y^{2})(-2xy^{2})\)

Answer: \(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

Multiply coefficients: -2×-2=4.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+1=6 and 2+2=4.

\(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A trail has 101 miles left to walk. A hiker completes 15 miles each day. Write an expression for the distance remaining after d days.

Answer: \(101-15d\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(101-15d\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(y^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((-8)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.
