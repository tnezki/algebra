Q3 4/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 36 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 48 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

Table: (0,36), (4,48); change +12; 12/4 = 3. Also wrote -3 near the response and "increasing".

Feedback

Your calculation 12/4 = 3 meters per second and your direction "increasing" are correct. The negative sign you also wrote is inconsistent; the signed rate is +3.
