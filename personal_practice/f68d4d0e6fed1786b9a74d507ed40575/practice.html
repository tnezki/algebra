Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((4p^{5}y^{2})(2py)\)

Answer: \(8 p^{6} y^{3}\)

Multiply coefficients: 4×2=8.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+1=6 and 2+1=3.

\(8 p^{6} y^{3}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{10a^{4}}{5a^{2}}\)

Answer: \(2 a^{2}\)

Divide the coefficients: 10/5=2.

Subtract exponents: 4−2=2.

\(2 a^{2}\)

Use slope and y-intercept to graph the line.

\(y = - \frac{x}{2} - 4\)

Answer: Slope \(- \frac{1}{2}\); y-intercept -4

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{-8}}{y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y}{y^{8}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y}{y^{8}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{4}}{y^{9}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{5}}\)

4−9=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{y^{5}}\)
