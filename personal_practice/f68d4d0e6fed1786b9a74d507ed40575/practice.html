Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2x^{7}y^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{14} y^{4}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^2, x^(7×2), y^(2×2).

\(4 x^{14} y^{4}\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent

\(16y^{8}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(x^{-8}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{8}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{1}{x^{8}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((-2)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $40 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1010? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(11\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-40w=1010\)

Subtract: 40w=440.

Divide: w=11.
