Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Then select the property used.

\((y^{3})^{2}\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

Answer: \(y^{6}\); C. Power of a Power

Use the power of a power rule.

\(y^{6}\)

Answer choice C.

2. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((-3a^{7})(-2a^{2})\)

Answer: \(6 a^{9}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-3)(-2)=6.

Add exponents of a: 7+2=9.

\(6 a^{9}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{80}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{73}\)
b.
\(y^{71}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{80}\)
c.
\((7m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{18}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^71; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^18.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

4. Review & Preview variation

An account has $117. A student withdraws $8 per week. Write an expression for the balance after w weeks, and state when the model makes sense.

Answer: \(117-8w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(117-8w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{-8}}{y^{-7}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{7}}{y^{8}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{7}}{y^{8}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{5}}{x^{8}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{3}}\)

5−8=−3; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{x^{3}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Find the slope and equation of the graphed line.

Answer: \(y = 3 x + 2\)

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
