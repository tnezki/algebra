Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{2}y^{4})^{2}\)

Answer: \(9 x^{4} y^{8}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^2, x^(2×2), y^(4×2).

\(9 x^{4} y^{8}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{6a^{5}}{3a^{2}}\)

Answer: \(2 a^{3}\)

Divide the coefficients: 6/3=2.

Subtract exponents: 5−2=3.

\(2 a^{3}\)
