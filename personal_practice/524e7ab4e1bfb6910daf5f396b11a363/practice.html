Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify.

\((2x^{5}y^{4})(-2x^{6}y^{6})\)

Answer: \(- 4 x^{11} y^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: 2×-2=-4.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+6=11 and 4+6=10.

\(- 4 x^{11} y^{10}\)

Combine exponents: −7+4=-3.

\(\frac{1}{a^{3}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{6}}{y^{4}}\)

8.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $1550 in the bank and spends $20 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1270? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(14\text{ weeks}\)

\(1550-20w=1270\)

Subtract: 20w=280.

Divide: w=14.
