Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{x^{8}}{x^{4}}\)

Answer: \(x^{4}\)

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(x^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2x^{8}y^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{16} y^{4}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^2, x^(8×2), y^(2×2).

\(4 x^{16} y^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{p^{-3}}{y^{-6}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{6}}{p^{3}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{6}}{p^{3}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{3x^{-2}y^{2}}{5y^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{3 y^{4}}{5 x^{2}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{3 y^{4}}{5 x^{2}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Find the slope and equation of the graphed line.

Answer: \(y = 2 - \frac{2 x}{3}\)

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
