Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((-2y^{6})(3y^{4})\)

Answer: \(- 6 y^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-2)(3)=-6.

Add exponents of y: 6+4=10.

\(- 6 y^{10}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2x^{7}y^{4})^{2}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{14} y^{8}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^2, x^(7×2), y^(4×2).

\(4 x^{14} y^{8}\)
