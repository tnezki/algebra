1.
Write as a single power.

\(y^{4}\cdot y\)

Answer: \(y^{5}\)

\(y^{5}\)

2.

\((2x^{7}y^{2})^{2}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{14} y^{4}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^2, x^(7×2), y^(2×2).

\(4 x^{14} y^{4}\)

7.
Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $35 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $890? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-35w=890\)

Subtract: 35w=560.

Divide: w=16.
