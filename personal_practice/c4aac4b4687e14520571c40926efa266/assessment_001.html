Q3

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 38 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 46 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Question credit: 4 / 5

Your work

table (0,38),(4,46); 2 (circled); it would be 2 because 46-38=8 and 4*2=8

Feedback

Your work shows some correct rate or direction reasoning. Check the sign and divide the position change by 4 seconds: (46-38)/4=2 m/s, increasing.
