Q3 1.5/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 40 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 24 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

circled printed increasing; 40 m,24 m,-16 m,4 m per sec; no negative rate

Feedback

Use final minus initial position, then divide by elapsed time: (24-40)/4=-4 m/s. A negative rate means decreasing position.
