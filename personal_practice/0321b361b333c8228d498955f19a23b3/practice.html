Lesson 3.1.2

1. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{2}}{x^{5}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{3}}\)

2−5=−3; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{x^{3}}\)

2. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(x^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.

3. Review & Preview variation

Which expressions are equivalent to \(64x^{4}\)? Select all correct answers.

a.
\((8x^{4})^2\)
b.
\((8x^{2})^2\)
c.
\((64x^{2})^2\)
d.
\((\frac{1}{64}x^{-4})^{-1}\)
e.
\((8x)(8x^{3})\)
f.
\((8x^{2})(8x^{2})\)

Answer: B, D, E and F

A: not equivalent

B: equivalent

C: not equivalent

D: equivalent

E: equivalent

F: equivalent

4. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1250 in the bank and spends $50 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(7\text{ weeks}\)

\(1250-50w=900\)

Subtract: 50w=350.

Divide: w=7.
