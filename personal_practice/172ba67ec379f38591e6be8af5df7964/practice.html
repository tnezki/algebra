2.
Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(4x^{-6}\)

Answer: \(\frac{4}{x^{6}}\)

The coefficient 4 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −6.

\(\frac{4}{x^{6}}\)

3.
Samantha has $1850 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1490? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(12\text{ weeks}\)

\(1850-30w=1490\)

Subtract: 30w=360.

Divide: w=12.
