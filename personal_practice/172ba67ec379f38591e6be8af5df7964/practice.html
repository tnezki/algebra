1.
Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{6x^{-5}y}{6y^{-4}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{5}}{x^{5}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{y^{5}}{x^{5}}\)

2.
Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(3y^{5})^0+(4y)^0}{(4y^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

3.
Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.
x-2-123
y-3-4-7-8

Answer: \(y=-x-5\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is -5.

\(y=-x-5\)
