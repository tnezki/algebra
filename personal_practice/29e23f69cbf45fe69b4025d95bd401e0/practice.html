2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{8x^{8}y^{5}}{4x^{6}y}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Divide coefficients: 8/4=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(8−6) and y^(5−1).

\(2 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(7x^{7})^0+(3x)^0}{(x^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.
