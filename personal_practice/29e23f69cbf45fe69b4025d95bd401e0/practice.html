Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{9x^{8}y^{2}}{3x^{6}y}\)

Answer: \(3 x^{2} y\)

Divide coefficients: 9/3=3.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(8−6) and y^(2−1).

\(3 x^{2} y\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((-3a^{7})(-2a^{2})\)

Answer: \(6 a^{9}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-3)(-2)=6.

Add exponents of a: 7+2=9.

\(6 a^{9}\)

Rewrite each expression using exponent properties.

a. \(\frac{h^{3}}{h^{8}}\)b. \(x^{5}\cdot x^{2}\)c. \((2k^{4})^{2}\)d. \(n^{4}\cdot n\)e. \(\frac{18x^{4}y^{5}}{3x^{4}}\)f. \(2xy^{2}\cdot 4x^{3}y^{5}\)

Answer: <p class="rp-parts"><span><strong>a.</strong> \(\frac{1}{h^{5}}\)</span><span><strong>b.</strong> \(x^{7}\)</span><span><strong>c.</strong> \(4k^{8}\)</span><span><strong>d.</strong> \(n^{5}\)</span><span><strong>e.</strong> \(6y^{5}\)</span><span><strong>f.</strong> \(8x^{4}y^{7}\)</span></p>

e: divide coefficients and cancel matching powers of x.

f: multiply coefficients and add like-base exponents.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{1}{y^{-6}}\)

Answer: \(y^{6}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(y^{6}\)

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(3x^{-4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{3}{x^{4}}\)

The coefficient 3 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −4.

\(\frac{3}{x^{4}}\)
