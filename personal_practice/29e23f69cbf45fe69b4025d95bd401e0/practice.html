Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(5a^{-1}\)

Answer: \(\frac{5}{a}\)

The coefficient 5 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −1.

\(\frac{5}{a}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((a^{2})^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.
