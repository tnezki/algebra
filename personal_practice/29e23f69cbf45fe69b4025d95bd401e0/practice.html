Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{20y^{8}}{4y^{4}}\)

Answer: \(5 y^{4}\)

Divide the coefficients: 20/4=5.

Subtract exponents: 8−4=4.

\(5 y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((3p^{2})(4p^{5})\)

Answer: \(12 p^{7}\)

Multiply coefficients: (3)(4)=12.

Add exponents of p: 2+5=7.

\(12 p^{7}\)

\(y^{22}\cdot y^{15}\Rightarrow y^{37}\)

4. Review & Preview variation

Use slope and y-intercept to graph the line.

\(y = x - 2\)

Answer: Slope \(1\); y-intercept -2

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(p^{-7}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{p^{7}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{p^{-4}}{y^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{2}}{p^{4}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{2}}{p^{4}}\)
