Lesson 3.1.2

1. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(3^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.

2. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(y^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Which expressions are equivalent to \(64x^{4}\)? Select all correct answers.

a.
\((8x^{4})^2\)
b.
\((8x^{2})^2\)
c.
\((64x^{2})^2\)
d.
\((\frac{1}{64}x^{-4})^{-1}\)
e.
\((8x)(8x^{3})\)
f.
\((8x^{2})(8x^{2})\)

Answer: B, D, E and F

A: not equivalent

B: equivalent

C: not equivalent

D: equivalent

E: equivalent

F: equivalent

4. Review & Preview variation

Find the slope and equation of the graphed line.

Answer: \(y = \frac{2 x}{3} + 3\)

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
