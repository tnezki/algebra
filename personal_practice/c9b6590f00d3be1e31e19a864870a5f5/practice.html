Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((-2y^{6})(3y^{4})\)

Answer: \(- 6 y^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: (-2)(3)=-6.

Add exponents of y: 6+4=10.

\(- 6 y^{10}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{8p^{13}}{4p^{8}}\)

Answer: \(2 p^{5}\)

Divide the coefficients: 8/4=2.

Subtract exponents: 13−8=5.

\(2 p^{5}\)

4. Review & Preview variation

A savings account starts with $186. A student deposits $8 each week. Write an expression for its balance after w weeks, defining w.

Answer: \(186+8w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(186+8w\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1850 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1490? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(12\text{ weeks}\)

\(1850-30w=1490\)

Subtract: 30w=360.

Divide: w=12.
