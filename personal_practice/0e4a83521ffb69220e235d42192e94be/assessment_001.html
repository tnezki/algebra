Q3

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 35 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 19 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Question credit: 1.5 / 5

Your work

table 0:35,4:19; -16; The average rate is 4 meters per second [no sign or direction]

Feedback

Use final minus initial position, then divide by elapsed time: (19-35)/4=-4 m/s. A negative rate means decreasing position.
