1.
Multiply.

\((3a^{4})(5a^{6})\)

Answer: \(15 a^{10}\)

Multiply coefficients: (3)(5)=15.

Add exponents of a: 4+6=10.

\(15 a^{10}\)

2.
Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{4x^{5}y^{6}}{2x^{3}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)

Divide coefficients: 4/2=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(5−3) and y^(6−3).

\(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)
