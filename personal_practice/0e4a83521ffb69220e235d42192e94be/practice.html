Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{25a^{12}}{5a^{8}}\)

Answer: \(5 a^{4}\)

Divide the coefficients: 25/5=5.

Subtract exponents: 12−8=4.

\(5 a^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3x^{2}y^{4})^{2}\)

Answer: \(9 x^{4} y^{8}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 3^2, x^(2×2), y^(4×2).

\(9 x^{4} y^{8}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{80}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{73}\)
b.
\(y^{71}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{80}\)
c.
\((7m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{18}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^71; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^18.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

4. Review & Preview variation

A trail has 179 miles left to walk. A hiker completes 20 miles each day. Write an expression for the distance remaining after d days.

Answer: \(179-20d\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(179-20d\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(8a^{5})^0+(3a)^0}{(a^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(8x^{-5}y^{4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{8y^{4}}{x^{5}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{8y^{4}}{x^{5}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y-211013

Answer: \(y=3x+4\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope 3.

The intercept is 4.

\(y=3x+4\)
