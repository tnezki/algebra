Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{6x^{9}y^{6}}{2x^{7}y^{2}}\)

Answer: \(3 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Divide coefficients: 6/2=3.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(9−7) and y^(6−2).

\(3 x^{2} y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply.

\((4y^{5})(2y^{4})\)

Answer: \(8 y^{9}\)

Multiply coefficients: (4)(2)=8.

Add exponents of y: 5+4=9.

\(8 y^{9}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{8x^{-5}y^{2}}{5y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{8 y^{3}}{5 x^{5}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{8 y^{3}}{5 x^{5}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(2x^{-6}y^{4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{2y^{4}}{x^{6}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{2y^{4}}{x^{6}}\)

Samantha has $1250 in the bank and spends $50 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(7\text{ weeks}\)

\(1250-50w=900\)

Subtract: 50w=350.

Divide: w=7.
