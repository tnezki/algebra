Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{8x^{4}y^{6}}{4x^{2}y^{2}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Divide coefficients: 8/4=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(4−2) and y^(6−2).

\(2 x^{2} y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2x^{2}y^{3})^{3}\)

Answer: \(8 x^{6} y^{9}\)

Apply the outer power to each factor: 2^3, x^(2×3), y^(3×3).

\(8 x^{6} y^{9}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Evaluate \(f(4)\) for each function.

a.
\(f(x)=5x+4\)
b.
\(f(x)=-|4-x|\)
c.
\(f(x)=\sqrt[3]{-2x}+4\)
d.
\(f(x)=\frac{1}{5}x+4\)

Answer: a. \(24\); b. \(0\); c. \(2\); d. \(\frac{24}{5}\)

Substitute 4 for x independently in each function and simplify; use the real cube root.

4. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{37}}{x^{15}}\Rightarrow x^{24}\)
b.
\(y^{22}\cdot y^{15}\Rightarrow y^{37}\)
c.
\((6m^{15})^2\Rightarrow 6m^{30}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^22; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 36m^30.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((-6)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(6x^{-2}y^{2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{6y^{2}}{x^{2}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{6y^{2}}{x^{2}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $40 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1010? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(11\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-40w=1010\)

Subtract: 40w=440.

Divide: w=11.
