Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Multiply.

\((2p^{6})(-2p^{5})\)

Answer: \(- 4 p^{11}\)

Multiply coefficients: (2)(-2)=-4.

Add exponents of p: 6+5=11.

\(- 4 p^{11}\)

2.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{16x^{6}y^{7}}{4x^{4}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{4}\)

Divide coefficients: 16/4=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(7−3).

\(4 x^{2} y^{4}\)

\(117-8w\)
