Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(\left(\frac{7x}{3}\right)^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(4x^{-5}\)

Answer: \(\frac{4}{x^{5}}\)

The coefficient 4 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −5.

\(\frac{4}{x^{5}}\)
