Lesson 3.1.1

\((a^{8})^{3}\)

Answer: \(a^{24}\)

\(a^{24}\)

2. Direct Skills

Multiply the expressions below.

\(2x^{8}\)
\(3x^{4}\)

Answer: \(6 x^{12}\)

Multiply coefficients: (2)(3)=6.

Add exponents of x: 8+4=12.

\(6 x^{12}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(5x^{2})^0+(4x)^0}{(5x^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{x^{-4}}{y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y}{x^{4}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y}{x^{4}}\)

7. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2100 in the bank and spends $20 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1780? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(2100-20w=1780\)

Subtract: 20w=320.

Divide: w=16.
