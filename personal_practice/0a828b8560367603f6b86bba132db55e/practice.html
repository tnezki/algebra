Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((3a^{8})^{4}\)

Answer: \(81 a^{32}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 4: 3^4=81.

Multiply exponents: 8×4=32.

\(81 a^{32}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{x^{12}}{x^{7}}\)

Answer: \(x^{5}\)

Subtract exponents: 12−7=5.

\(x^{5}\)

4. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{75}}{x^{17}}\Rightarrow x^{60}\)
b.
\(y^{58}\cdot y^{17}\Rightarrow y^{75}\)
c.
\((7m^{17})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{34}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^58; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^34.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(y^{-7}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{7}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{y^{7}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\((y^{-5})(y^{3})\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

Combine exponents: −5+3=-2.

\(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1250 in the bank and spends $50 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(7\text{ weeks}\)

\(1250-50w=900\)

Subtract: 50w=350.

Divide: w=7.
