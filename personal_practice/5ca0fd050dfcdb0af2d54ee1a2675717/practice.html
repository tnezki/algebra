Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{a^{11}}{a^{5}}\)

Answer: \(a^{6}\)

Subtract exponents: 11−5=6.

\(a^{6}\)

2. Direct Skills

Fill in the missing exponent.

\(y^{4}\cdot y^{2}=y^{\square}\)

Answer: \(y^{6}\)

The base is the same; add the exponents: 4+2=6.

\(y^{6}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A collector starts with 155 cards and collects 14 more cards each week. Write an expression for the collection size after w weeks.

Answer: \(155+14w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(155+14w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{a^{5}}{a^{10}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

5−10=−5; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{a^{5}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{y^{-4}}{y^{-6}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{6}}{y^{4}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{y^{6}}{y^{4}}\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $25 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1900? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(18\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-25w=1900\)

Subtract: 25w=450.

Divide: w=18.
