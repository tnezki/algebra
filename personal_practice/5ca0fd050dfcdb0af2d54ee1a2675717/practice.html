Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((2p^{2})^{4}\)

Answer: \(16 p^{8}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 4: 2^4=16.

Multiply exponents: 2×4=8.

\(16 p^{8}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

A savings account starts with $185. A student deposits $7 each week. Write an expression for its balance after w weeks, defining w.

Answer: \(185+7w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(185+7w\)

b.
\(y^{71}\cdot y^{15}\Rightarrow y^{86}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(2x^{-1}y^{4}\)

Answer: \(\frac{2y^{4}}{x}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{2y^{4}}{x}\)

6. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{(3a^{4})^0+(6a)^0}{(3a^2)^0}\)

Answer: \(2\)

Each nonzero expression to power 0 equals 1.

Then (1+1)/1=2.
