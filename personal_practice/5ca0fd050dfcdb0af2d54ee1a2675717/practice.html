Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{6x^{4}y^{3}}{3x^{2}y}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 6/3=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(4−2) and y^(3−1).

\(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

\(\frac{x^{6}}{x^{4}}\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

Answer: \(x^{2}\); B. Quotient of Powers

Use the quotient of powers rule.

\(x^{2}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{8x^{-1}y}{2y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{4 y^{2}}{x}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{4 y^{2}}{x}\)

6. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(y^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(\frac{1}{y^{2}}\)
