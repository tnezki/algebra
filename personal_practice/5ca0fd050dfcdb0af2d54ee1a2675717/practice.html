Lesson 3.1.1

1.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{8x^{6}y^{5}}{4x^{4}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 8/4=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(5−3).

\(2 x^{2} y^{2}\)

2.I can simplify expressions using exponent properties.

Simplify.

\((3p^{2})^{4}\)

Answer: \(81 p^{8}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 4: 3^4=81.

Multiply exponents: 2×4=8.

\(81 p^{8}\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(185+7w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{2x^{-7}y^{2}}{2y^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{y^{4}}{x^{7}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{y^{4}}{x^{7}}\)

6.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{1}{x^{-2}}\)

Answer: \(x^{2}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(x^{2}\)

7.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $2100 in the bank and spends $20 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1780? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(16\text{ weeks}\)

\(2100-20w=1780\)

Subtract: 20w=320.

Divide: w=16.
