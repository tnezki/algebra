2.
Simplify.

\((-3x^{6}y^{2})(2x^{7}y^{5})\)

Answer: \(- 6 x^{13} y^{7}\)

Multiply coefficients: -3×2=-6.

Add each variable’s exponents: 6+7=13 and 2+5=7.

\(- 6 x^{13} y^{7}\)
