Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply the expressions below.

\(y^{8}\)
\(y^{5}\)

Answer: \(y^{13}\)

The base is the same; add the exponents: 8+5=13.

\(y^{13}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{6x^{6}y^{5}}{3x^{4}y^{2}}\)

Answer: \(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)

Divide coefficients: 6/3=2.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(5−2).

\(2 x^{2} y^{3}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{1}{y^{-7}}\)

Answer: \(y^{7}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(y^{7}\)

6. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(7x^{-4}y^{5}\)

Answer: \(\frac{7y^{5}}{x^{4}}\)

Move only factors with negative powers across the fraction bar.

\(\frac{7y^{5}}{x^{4}}\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $40 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1010? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(11\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-40w=1010\)

Subtract: 40w=440.

Divide: w=11.
