Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Write as one power.

\((p^{7})\)

Answer: \(p^{7}\)

Multiply the exponents: 7×1=7.

\(p^{7}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{12x^{6}y^{5}}{3x^{4}y^{3}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Divide coefficients: 12/3=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(6−4) and y^(5−3).

\(4 x^{2} y^{2}\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{p^{6}}{p^{8}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{p^{2}}\)

6−8=−2; rewrite the reciprocal with a positive exponent.

\(\frac{1}{p^{2}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\(p^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

Any nonzero base raised to the zero power equals 1.
