Q3 4.5/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 33 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 25 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

y=0x+33 [initial attempt]; -2 m per second -> decreasing (negative 2)

Feedback

Correct signed rate and motion interpretation: (25-33)/4=-2 m/s, decreasing.
