Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Simplify. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(\frac{16x^{10}y^{5}}{4x^{8}y^{2}}\)

Answer: \(4 x^{2} y^{3}\)

Divide coefficients: 16/4=4.

Subtract exponents for each base: x^(10−8) and y^(5−2).

\(4 x^{2} y^{3}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify.

\((4p^{6})^{2}\)

Answer: \(16 p^{12}\)

Raise the coefficient to power 2: 4^2=16.

Multiply exponents: 6×2=12.

\(16 p^{12}\)

3. Review & Preview variation

Evaluate \(f(4)\) for each function.

a.
\(f(x)=5x+4\)
b.
\(f(x)=-|4-x|\)
c.
\(f(x)=\sqrt[3]{-2x}+4\)
d.
\(f(x)=\frac{1}{5}x+4\)

Answer: a. \(24\); b. \(0\); c. \(2\); d. \(\frac{24}{5}\)

Substitute 4 for x independently in each function and simplify; use the real cube root.

4. Review & Preview variation

An account has $117. A student withdraws $8 per week. Write an expression for the balance after w weeks, and state when the model makes sense.

Answer: \(117-8w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(117-8w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Write an equivalent expression with positive exponents.

\(\frac{1}{x^{-5}}\)

Answer: \(x^{5}\)

A negative exponent means reciprocal; simplify the factors in the numerator and denominator.

\(x^{5}\)

\(\frac{1}{a^{3}}\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Samantha has $1450 in the bank and spends $30 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $1000? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(15\text{ weeks}\)

\(1450-30w=1000\)

Subtract: 30w=450.

Divide: w=15.
