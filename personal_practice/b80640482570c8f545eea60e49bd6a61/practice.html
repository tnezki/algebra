Lesson 3.1.1

1. Direct Skills

Multiply and write one monomial.

\((-2x^{5}y^{2})(-2xy^{2})\)

Answer: \(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

Multiply coefficients: -2×-2=4.

Add each variable’s exponents: 5+1=6 and 2+2=4.

\(4 x^{6} y^{4}\)

2. Direct Skills

Simplify. Then select the property used.

\((2x^{2}y^{3})(3x^{5}y^{5})\)

  1. Product of Powers
  2. Quotient of Powers
  3. Power of a Power
  4. Zero Exponent
  5. Negative Exponent

Answer: \(6x^{7}y^{8}\); A. Product of Powers

Use the product of powers rule.

\(6x^{7}y^{8}\)

Answer choice A.

3. Review & Preview variation

Gerardo made these three claims. Decide which are correct, and correct the others.

a.
\(\frac{x^{80}}{x^{9}}\Rightarrow x^{73}\)
b.
\(y^{71}\cdot y^{9}\Rightarrow y^{80}\)
c.
\((7m^{9})^2\Rightarrow 7m^{18}\)

Answer: a. Incorrect, x^71; b. Correct; c. Incorrect, 49m^18.

Subtract exponents for quotients; add for products; apply outer powers to coefficients as well.

4. Review & Preview variation

A savings account starts with $157. A student deposits $14 each week. Write an expression for its balance after w weeks, defining w.

Answer: \(157+14w\)

Start with the initial amount and add or subtract the amount per time period.

\(157+14w\)

Lesson 3.1.2

5. Direct Skills

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(x^{-2}\)

Answer: \(\frac{1}{x^{2}}\)

A negative exponent indicates a reciprocal, not a negative value.

\(\frac{1}{x^{2}}\)

6. Direct Skills

Evaluate. Assume any variable is nonzero.

\((5p^{6})^0\)

Answer: \(1\)

The entire nonzero expression is the base; anything nonzero to power 0 equals 1.

7. Review & Preview variation

Determine the equation of the line containing the points in the table.

x-2-123
y121187

Answer: \(y=-x+10\)

Differences in y divided by differences in x give slope -1.

The intercept is 10.

\(y=-x+10\)

8. Review & Preview variation

Find the slope and equation of the graphed line.

Answer: \(y = 3 x + 2\)

The slope is the rise divided by the run; the y-intercept is where x=0.
