1.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Rewrite with positive exponents. Assume variables are nonzero.

\(3a^{-7}\)

Answer: \(\frac{3}{a^{7}}\)

The coefficient 3 stays in the numerator; move only the base with exponent −7.

\(\frac{3}{a^{7}}\)

2.I can explain zero and negative exponents and rewrite expressions.

Simplify using positive exponents.

\(\frac{4x^{-3}y^{4}}{2y^{-1}}\)

Answer: \(\frac{2 y^{5}}{x^{3}}\)

Combine coefficients, send x with its negative exponent to the denominator, and bring the denominator’s negative y power to the numerator.

\(\frac{2 y^{5}}{x^{3}}\)

3.Original CPM mixed/spaced homework problem (all original subparts).

Samantha has $2350 in the bank and spends $15 per week. In how many weeks will her balance be $2200? Define a variable and solve.

Answer: \(10\text{ weeks}\)

\(2350-15w=2200\)

Subtract: 15w=150.

Divide: w=10.
