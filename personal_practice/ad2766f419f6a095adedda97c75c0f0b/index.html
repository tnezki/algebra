Q3 2.5/5

Signed rate of change (motion)

At 0 seconds, a cart is at 45 meters. At 4 seconds, it is at 53 meters.

Find the signed average rate of change in meters per second.

Is the position increasing or decreasing? Explain using the sign.

Your work

table (0,45),(1,49),(2,53); +4; Average rate of change is 4 m/s; the position is increasing (its growing)

Feedback

Your work shows some correct rate or direction reasoning. Check the sign and divide the position change by 4 seconds: (53-45)/4=2 m/s, increasing.
