Expressions and Structure

Structure tells you what can combine.

Learning Target

  • Combine like terms.
  • Use the distributive property correctly.

Identify terms, coefficients, and factors.

Terms are separated by addition or subtraction. A coefficient is the numerical factor multiplying a variable part. Factors are quantities multiplied together inside a term.

Math NotesRead structure before calculating

For \(7x^2-3x+5\), the terms are \(7x^2\), \(-3x\), and \(5\). The coefficient of \(x\) in the middle term is -3.

Example

For \(7x^2-3x+5\), list the terms, identify the coefficient of the x-term, and name the factors in \(7x^2\).

You Try It

For \(-4ab+9a-2\), identify the term containing both variables, its coefficient, and its factors.

Substitute values into an expression.

Substitution means replacing a variable with a specific value. Put negative replacements in parentheses before evaluating so the sign stays attached to the value.

Math NotesNegative inputs

If \(x=-2\), then \(x^2=(-2)^2=4\). Writing the parentheses first prevents common sign errors.

Example

Combine like terms.

Like terms have exactly the same variable part. Combine their coefficients, but do not combine terms with different variable parts.

Math NotesLike-term test

\(5x\) and \(-2x\) are like terms. \(5x\) and \(5x^2\) are not.

Example

Simplify by combining like terms: \(5x+3-2x+8\).

You Try It

Simplify: \(4a-7+3b-9a+2b+5\).

Use the distributive property correctly.

The distributive property multiplies a factor outside parentheses by every term inside. Keep signs attached to the values you distribute.

Math NotesDistribute to every term

\(a(b+c)=ab+ac\). With subtraction, \(-3(2x-5)=-6x+15\).

Example

Use the distributive property to rewrite \(-3(2x-5)\).

You Try It

Simplify \(4(3y+2)-5\). Show the distribution before combining constants.

Common Mistakes to Watch

MistakeWhat to remember
Combining unlike terms\(3x+4x^2\) cannot become \(7x^3\). The variable parts differ.
Distributing to only one termA factor outside parentheses multiplies every term inside.
Losing a negative during substitutionUse parentheses around a negative replacement before applying exponents.
