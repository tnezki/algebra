Problem 6

An exponential function has $f(0)=16$ and $f(2)=4$. The factor is positive. Determine the per-unit factor and write the function.

Because two equal steps multiply by $\frac{1}{4}$, the positive per-step factor is $\frac{1}{2}$. Thus $f(x)=16(\frac{1}{2})^x$.
