Question 1

Use the starting value and multiplier to write the first four outputs.

ModelOutput at 0Output at 1Output at 2Output at 3
Start 6, multiply by 5
Start 80, multiply by 0.5

Solution: Starting at $6$ and multiplying by $5$ gives $6,30,150,750$. Starting at $80$ and multiplying by $0.5$ gives $80,40,20,10$.

Question 2

Circle each expression that is written in exponential form $ab^x$.

  1. $E(x)=5(2)^x$
  2. $f(x)=5x+2$
  3. $f(x)=2x^2+5$
  4. $f(x)=90(0.85)^x$

Solution: Exponential form $ab^x$ has a variable exponent. Circle $E(x)=5(2)^x$ and $f(x)=90(0.85)^x$.

Question 3

Gasoline increases from $\$4.10$ per gallon to $\$4.35$ per gallon over two months. Assuming a geometric pattern, find the monthly multiplier and monthly percent increase.

Solution: Let the monthly multiplier be $r$. Then $4.10r^2=4.35$, so $r=\sqrt{4.35/4.10}\approx1.0300$. The monthly percent increase is about $3.0\%$.

Question 4

A model has the form $y=240(0.8)^x$.

  1. Explain why the output can get close to 0 without becoming negative.
  2. Describe what each parameter tells you about the situation.

Solution: The starting value is $240$ and the multiplier is $0.8$, so each step keeps $80\%$ of the previous output. Multiplying positive numbers by $0.8$ keeps them positive, so the values approach $0$ but never become negative. The horizontal asymptote is $y=0$.

Question 5

Complete the diamond problem. The top is the product and the bottom is the sum.

Solution: The diamond has product $-24$ and sum $2$. The two numbers are $6$ and $-4$ because $6(-4)=-24$ and $6+(-4)=2$.

Question 6

For each pair of numbers, write the product and the sum.

  1. $3$ and $5$
  2. $-4$ and $7$
  3. $-6$ and $-2$
  4. $8$ and $-1$

Solution: $3$ and $5$: product $15$, sum $8$. $-4$ and $7$: product $-28$, sum $3$. $-6$ and $-2$: product $12$, sum $-8$. $8$ and $-1$: product $-8$, sum $7$.

Question 7

The expression $65x^2+212x-133$ is already the result of multiplying two factors. Explain how you know the product without finding the factors.

Solution: Multiplying two binomials combines area/FOIL pieces into one polynomial. The expression $65x^2+212x-133$ is the final sum of those products; it can be recognized as a product result even before recovering the original factors.

Question 8

Factor $12x^2+11x-5$ by splitting the middle term. Then verify by multiplying your binomials.

Solution: For $12x^2+11x-5$, $ac=-60$ and the needed sum is $11$, so use $15$ and $-4$. Split: $12x^2+15x-4x-5=3x(4x+5)-1(4x+5)=(3x-1)(4x+5)$. Multiplying verifies $12x^2+15x-4x-5=12x^2+11x-5$.

Question 9

Complete the table for $2x^2+5x+3$ and use it to write the factored form.

StepValue
Product $ac$$6$
Needed sum$5$
Number pair_____ and _____
Factored form_____

Solution: $ac=6$, needed sum $5$, so the pair is $2$ and $3$. Therefore $2x^2+5x+3=(2x+3)(x+1)$.

Question 10

Factor $36x^2-49$ using the difference-of-squares pattern. Then factor it again by thinking of it as a product of conjugates. Compare the two explanations.

Solution: Difference of squares: $36x^2-49=(6x)^2-7^2=(6x-7)(6x+7)$. Thinking of conjugates gives the same result because $(a-b)(a+b)=a^2-b^2$.

Question 11

A classmate claims $x^2-12x+40$ is almost a perfect square because $40$ is close to $36$. Is that reasoning sufficient? Give a reasonableness argument based on structure, not closeness.

Solution: Closeness is not enough. A perfect square with middle term $-12x$ would be $(x-6)^2=x^2-12x+36$. Since $40 e36$, $x^2-12x+40$ is not a perfect-square trinomial.

Question 12

Create a factorable trinomial and a similar-looking non-factorable trinomial. Explain how product-sum evidence distinguishes them.

Solution: Example factorable: $x^2+5x+6$ because $2\cdot3=6$ and $2+3=5$, so $(x+2)(x+3)$. Similar non-factorable over integers: $x^2+5x+7$ because no integer pair multiplies to $7$ and adds to $5$.

Question 13

Describe what a zero means on a graph of a quadratic.

Solution: A zero is an $x$-value where the graph crosses or touches the $x$-axis, so the output is $0$.

Question 14

Given zeros $2$ and $5$, write two different equivalent factored equations.

Solution: Any nonzero leading coefficient works with factors $(x-2)(x-5)$. Examples: $y=(x-2)(x-5)$ and $y=2(x-2)(x-5)$.

Question 15

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Solve $(2x-5)(x+4)=0$.

Solution: Set each factor equal to zero. $2x-5=0$ gives $x=\frac{5}{2}$, and $x+4=0$ gives $x=-4$.

Question 16

Strategy Construction. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Completing the Square. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Design a step-by-step solving strategy for $2x^2+9x-5=0$ that avoids unnecessary work. Then solve it.

Solution: Use the quadratic formula because $2x^2+9x-5$ is not a quick simple factor. $x=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{9^2-4(2)(-5)}}{2(2)}=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{121}}{4}=\frac{-9\pm11}{4}$. Thus $x=\frac12$ or $x=-5$.
