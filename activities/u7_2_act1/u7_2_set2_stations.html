Station 2 Solutions

An exponential function has $f(0)=6$ and $f(3)=162$. The factor is positive. Determine the per-unit factor and write the function.

Solution:

Across 3 equal steps the outputs multiply by $27$, so the positive per-step factor is $3$. Thus $f(x)=6(3)^x$.

The graph below is consistent with a model whose $y$-intercept is 1.5. Compare the candidates $y=1.5(1.75)^x$ and $y=1.75(1.5)^x$. Which equation fits the graph more directly? Support your choice with one intercept feature and one growth feature.

Solution:

$y=1.5(1.75)^x$ fits: its initial value is 1.5, matching the $y$-intercept, and its base is greater than 1 so the graph grows.
