Teacher & Student Moves Guide

Use across the Activity options
I-Can Moves
I Can 1: I can build exponential equations using an initial value and a growth or decay factor.

Teacher Moves

  • Ask students to identify the mathematical evidence that supports the response.
  • Press for a brief check, comparison, or justification before moving on.

Student Moves

  • Show the mathematics clearly and name the feature or pattern used.
  • Compare reasoning with a partner and revise when evidence suggests it.
I Can 2: I can use exponential equations to predict values from tables, graphs, and situations.

Teacher Moves

  • Ask students to identify the mathematical evidence that supports the response.
  • Press for a brief check, comparison, or justification before moving on.

Student Moves

  • Show the mathematics clearly and name the feature or pattern used.
  • Compare reasoning with a partner and revise when evidence suggests it.
I Can 3: I can formulate exponential equations that represent repeated percent increase or decrease.

Teacher Moves

  • Ask students to identify the mathematical evidence that supports the response.
  • Press for a brief check, comparison, or justification before moving on.

Student Moves

  • Show the mathematics clearly and name the feature or pattern used.
  • Compare reasoning with a partner and revise when evidence suggests it.
Participation-Structure Moves
Stations

Teacher Moves

  • Monitor for different strategies and recurring misconceptions.
  • Select and sequence useful work for a later connection.

Student Moves

  • Leave a readable strategy at each station.
  • Compare methods instead of only checking answers.
Find Someone Who

Teacher Moves

  • Require explanation/checking before a signature.
  • Listen for copied answers without mathematical reasoning.

Student Moves

  • Explain or check one problem with each partner.
  • Record reasoning you understand, not just a final answer.
