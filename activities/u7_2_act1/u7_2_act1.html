Writing Exponential Equations
Source: practice_set_7_2_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Partners
Materials: One whiteboard and marker per student
Structure Partner explanation + alternating roles
Setup Sit with your partner. Each person needs one whiteboard and one marker.
Directions
  1. Work with a partner
  2. Partner A explains, Partner B writes
  3. Switch roles
  4. Check answers with teacher
  5. Rotate partners
Goal: Verbal reasoning + procedural accuracy
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Partner A explains while Partner B writes, then roles switch on the next problem.
  • The writer asks clarifying questions instead of silently copying.
  • Partners check the displayed solution and name any procedural error before rotating.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • One partner completes every problem while the other watches.
  • The explainer only says the answer and skips the reasoning.
  • Partners rotate without checking or correcting their work.

Unit 7.2 · Activity 1 · Rally Coach Solution 3
Solution 3
Current · DOK 2

Gasoline increases from $\$4.10$ per gallon to $\$4.35$ per gallon over two months. Assuming a geometric pattern, find the monthly multiplier and monthly percent increase.

Solution: Let the monthly multiplier be $r$. Then $4.10r^2=4.35$, so $r=\sqrt{4.35/4.10}\approx1.0300$. The monthly percent increase is about $3.0\%$.

Solution 4
Current · DOK 3

A model has the form $y=240(0.8)^x$.

  1. Explain why the output can get close to 0 without becoming negative.
  2. Describe what each parameter tells you about the situation.

Solution: The starting value is $240$ and the multiplier is $0.8$, so each step keeps $80\%$ of the previous output. Multiplying positive numbers by $0.8$ keeps them positive, so the values approach $0$ but never become negative. The horizontal asymptote is $y=0$.

Mastery · DOK 2

Factor $12x^2+11x-5$ by splitting the middle term. Then verify by multiplying your binomials.

Solution: For $12x^2+11x-5$, $ac=-60$ and the needed sum is $11$, so use $15$ and $-4$. Split: $12x^2+15x-4x-5=3x(4x+5)-1(4x+5)=(3x-1)(4x+5)$. Multiplying verifies $12x^2+15x-4x-5=12x^2+11x-5$.

Solution 10
Mastery · DOK 3

Factor $36x^2-49$ using the difference-of-squares pattern. Then factor it again by thinking of it as a product of conjugates. Compare the two explanations.

Solution: Difference of squares: $36x^2-49=(6x)^2-7^2=(6x-7)(6x+7)$. Thinking of conjugates gives the same result because $(a-b)(a+b)=a^2-b^2$.

Solution 11

Solution: Closeness is not enough. A perfect square with middle term $-12x$ would be $(x-6)^2=x^2-12x+36$. Since $40 e36$, $x^2-12x+40$ is not a perfect-square trinomial.

Strategy Construction. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Completing the Square. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Design a step-by-step solving strategy for $2x^2+9x-5=0$ that avoids unnecessary work. Then solve it.

Solution: Use the quadratic formula because $2x^2+9x-5$ is not a quick simple factor. $x=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{9^2-4(2)(-5)}}{2(2)}=\frac{-9\pm\sqrt{121}}{4}=\frac{-9\pm11}{4}$. Thus $x=\frac12$ or $x=-5$.
