6

The A bacteria count is modeled by $M(t)=120(1.2)^t$ for a planned observation window of 24 hours. State a meaningful contextual domain for $t$ and distinguish it from the mathematical domain of the exponential function.

Answer:

A meaningful contextual domain is $0\le t\le 24$ hours. Mathematically, the exponential expression is defined for all real $t$, but negative times and times beyond the stated observation window are outside this model’s intended context.

7

A real-world quantity is modeled by $A(t)=150(\frac{5}{4})^t$. Interpret both 150 and the factor $\frac{5}{4}$ in context-independent language.

Answer:

150 is the initial value at $t=0$. The factor $\frac{5}{4}$ means a 25% increase for each one-unit increase in $t$.

21

A quadratic model has factored form $Q(t)=-(t-0)(t-8)$ and opens downward. Identify its two zeros and the time-coordinate of its vertex. Explain how those features help describe the model.

Answer:

The zeros are $t=0$ and $t=8$. Their midpoint is $t=4$, the vertex time. The zeros mark where the output is 0, and the vertex marks the model’s maximum.

Factor $x^2+7x+12$ completely.

$(x+3)(x+4)$.

23

Factor $\displaystyle 3x^2+8x+4$ completely.

Answer:

$(3x+2)(x+2)$.

24

Find two integers whose product is $\displaystyle 18$ and whose sum is $-9$. Explain how this pair would help factor a quadratic trinomial.

Answer:

$-3$ and $-6$. The pair supplies the two numbers needed to split or construct the middle term in product-sum factoring.
