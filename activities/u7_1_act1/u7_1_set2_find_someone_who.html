A quantity decreases by 9% each period. Write the multiplicative factor used from one period to the next.

Answer:

The factor is $\frac{91}{100}$ (0.91).

2

An exponential relationship uses a multiplier of $\frac{5}{4}$ for each increase of 1 in $x$. Describe the percent change and tell whether it is growth or decay.

Answer:

25% increase (growth).

3

Consider $y=8(\frac{3}{2})^x$. Decide whether the relationship represents exponential growth or decay. Identify the initial value and the growth or decay factor.

Answer:

Growth; initial value $\displaystyle 8$ and factor $\frac{3}{2}$.

4

Use the graph to decide whether the relationship shows exponential growth or decay. Give one graph feature that supports your decision.

Answer:

Exponential decay. The curve decreases toward the horizontal axis as x increases; the change is multiplicative rather than a constant vertical difference.

21

A height model leads to the equation $\displaystyle 5t^2-12t-9=0$ for possible times when an object is at ground level, where $t$ is measured in seconds and $t\ge 0$. Use the quadratic formula and interpret the meaningful solution.
