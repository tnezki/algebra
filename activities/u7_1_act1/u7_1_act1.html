Recognizing Growth and Decay
Source: practice_set_7_1_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Rotating partners
Materials: Record sheet or worksheet + pencil
Structure Movement + peer interaction
Setup You will need your record sheet or worksheet and a pencil. Keep it with you as you move around the room.
Directions
  1. Work Alone (5 min): Skim every problem you can, then start solving any problem you can. Leave anything you’re stuck on blank for now.
  2. Find a Partner: Walk around and find someone who is ready to share. Compare your completed answers — discuss any that don’t match.
  3. Work Together (5 min): Choose one problem that either of you left blank. Work through it together. Both partners record the solution on their own sheet.
  4. Find Someone New: Thank your partner and repeat steps 2–3 with a different person each time.
  5. Return to your seat with every problem completed and understood — not just filled in.
Goal: Exposure to multiple explanations
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students begin alone and mark which items they can solve before moving.
  • Partners compare completed answers and talk through any mismatch before recording.
  • Each new partner helps complete a previously blank item, and both students write the solution.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students simply collect answers from classmates without explaining them.
  • A pair stays together the whole time instead of finding new explanations.
  • Students return with filled-in blanks they cannot explain.

Solution 3
Current · DOK 2

For the sequence 2, 8, 3y+5, find the value of y that would make the sequence arithmetic and the value of y that would make it geometric. Show the comparison you used.

Solution: Arithmetic needs equal differences: $8-2=6$, so $(3y+5)-8=6$. Then $3y+5=14$ and $y=3$. Geometric needs equal ratios: $8/2=4$, so $(3y+5)/8=4$. Then $3y+5=32$ and $y=9$.

A rectangle has area $54$ and side lengths $x$ and $x+3$. Find possible positive $x$.

Solution: Area means $x(x+3)=54$, so $x^2+3x-54=0$. Factor: $(x+9)(x-6)=0$, giving $x=-9$ or $x=6$. A side length must be positive, so $x=6$.

Two-Method Analysis. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Solve $x^2-8x+7=0$ two ways. Then explain which method is more efficient and why.

Solution: Factoring gives $x^2-8x+7=(x-1)(x-7)=0$, so $x=1$ or $x=7$. Using the quadratic formula gives $x=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{64-28}}{2}=\frac{8\pm6}{2}$, so $x=1$ or $x=7$. Factoring is more efficient because the trinomial factors quickly.
