1

The sequence is $\displaystyle 9$, $\displaystyle \frac{45}{4}$, $\displaystyle \frac{225}{16}$, $\displaystyle \frac{1125}{64}$, $\ldots$ . Find the next two terms and explain whether the pattern shows exponential growth or decay.

Answer:

Next terms: $\frac{5625}{256}$, $\frac{28125}{1024}$. The constant multiplier is $\frac{5}{4}$, so the pattern shows exponential growth.

2

A quantity decreases by 25% each period. Write the multiplicative factor used from one period to the next.

Answer:

The factor is $\frac{3}{4}$ (0.75).

3

An exponential relationship uses a multiplier of $\frac{5}{6}$ for each increase of 1 in $x$. Describe the percent change and tell whether it is growth or decay.

Answer:

16.6667% decrease (decay).

4

Consider $y=6(\frac{4}{3})^x$. Decide whether the relationship represents exponential growth or decay. Identify the initial value and the growth or decay factor.

Answer:

Growth; initial value $\displaystyle 6$ and factor $\frac{4}{3}$.
