Question 1

The table gives the height of a bouncing ball after each bounce. Identify whether the pattern is closer to arithmetic, geometric, quadratic, or neither. Use at least one ratio or difference.

Bounce01234
Height (cm)800480288173104

Solution: The ratios are approximately $480/800=0.6$, $288/480=0.6$, $173/288\approx0.60$, and $104/173\approx0.60$. The pattern is closest to geometric/exponential decay with multiplier about $0.6$.

Question 2

For each situation, label it as growth, decay, or not exponential.

  1. A bacteria sample doubles every hour.
  2. A candle loses 3 centimeters of height every hour.
  3. A car keeps 82% of its value each year.
  4. A club gains exactly 4 members each week.

Solution: a) growth because doubling means multiplier $2$. b) not exponential because subtracting $3$ cm is a constant difference. c) decay because keeping $82\%$ means multiplier $0.82$. d) not exponential because adding $4$ members is a constant difference.

Question 3

For the sequence 2, 8, 3y+5, find the value of y that would make the sequence arithmetic and the value of y that would make it geometric. Show the comparison you used.

Solution: Arithmetic needs equal differences: $8-2=6$, so $(3y+5)-8=6$. Then $3y+5=14$ and $y=3$. Geometric needs equal ratios: $8/2=4$, so $(3y+5)/8=4$. Then $3y+5=32$ and $y=9$.

Question 6

For each pair, decide whether the expressions are equivalent by naming the common factor that connects them.

  1. $8x+12$ and $4(2x+3)$
  2. $5y^2-15y$ and $5y(y-3)$
  3. $3a+12b$ and $6(a+2b)$

Solution: a) Equivalent because $4(2x+3)=8x+12$; the common factor is $4$. b) Equivalent because $5y(y-3)=5y^2-15y$; the common factor is $5y$. c) Not equivalent because $6(a+2b)=6a+12b$, not $3a+12b$.

Question 7

Use the table to factor the polynomial shown by the terms.

Term$16x^3$$24x^2$$-40x$
Common numeric factor$8$$8$$8$
Common variable factor$x$$x$$x$

Write the polynomial as a product of the greatest common factor and a remaining polynomial.

Solution: The greatest common factor is $8x$. Dividing each term gives $16x^3/(8x)=2x^2$, $24x^2/(8x)=3x$, and $-40x/(8x)=-5$. So the product is $8x(2x^2+3x-5)$.

Solution: Student A is factored completely: $6p(2p-5)$. Student B has $3p(4p-10)$, and $4p-10$ still has a common factor of $2$, so it can become $6p(2p-5)$.

Question 9

Rewrite $9x^2y+12xy^2+3xy$ as a product. Then identify the number of terms remaining inside the parentheses.

Solution: The GCF is $3xy$. Dividing gives $3x$, $4y$, and $1$, so $9x^2y+12xy^2+3xy=3xy(3x+4y+1)$. There are $3$ terms inside the parentheses.

Question 10

Factor $12x^2+30x$ in two different first steps: one using a common factor of $6x$ and one using a common factor of $3x$. Compare the results and explain why one final answer is considered completely factored.

Solution: Using $6x$ gives $12x^2+30x=6x(2x+5)$, which is complete. Using $3x$ gives $3x(4x+10)$; the parentheses still have common factor $2$, so $3x\cdot2(2x+5)=6x(2x+5)$. The completely factored answer uses the GCF $6x$.

Question 11

Without fully expanding every term, argue whether $8n(3n-5)+12(3n-5)$ can reasonably be rewritten as $4(3n-5)(2n+3)$. Use structure to support your conclusion.

Solution: Both terms share $(3n-5)$, so factor it first: $(3n-5)(8n+12)$. Then $8n+12=4(2n+3)$. Therefore the expression equals $4(3n-5)(2n+3)$.

Question 12

Create a three-term polynomial with greatest common factor $6x$ such that one term is negative and the polynomial has degree $3$. Then factor it completely and explain how your example satisfies all constraints.

Solution: One valid example is $12x^3-18x^2+30x$. The GCF is $6x$, one term is negative, and the degree is $3$. Factoring gives $6x(2x^2-3x+5)$, and the terms inside have no common factor greater than $1$.

Station 4 Answers

Question 13

For $f(x)=(x-2)(x+8)$, name the $x$-intercepts.

Solution: Set each factor equal to zero: $x-2=0$ gives $x=2$, and $x+8=0$ gives $x=-8$. The $x$-intercepts are $(2,0)$ and $(-8,0)$.

Question 14

Factor $x^2+9x+20$.

Solution: Find numbers that multiply to $20$ and add to $9$: $4$ and $5$. Therefore $x^2+9x+20=(x+4)(x+5)$.

Question 15

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

A rectangle has area $54$ and side lengths $x$ and $x+3$. Find possible positive $x$.

Solution: Area means $x(x+3)=54$, so $x^2+3x-54=0$. Factor: $(x+9)(x-6)=0$, giving $x=-9$ or $x=6$. A side length must be positive, so $x=6$.

Question 16

Two-Method Analysis. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Solve $x^2-8x+7=0$ two ways. Then explain which method is more efficient and why.

Solution: Factoring gives $x^2-8x+7=(x-1)(x-7)=0$, so $x=1$ or $x=7$. Using the quadratic formula gives $x=\frac{8\pm\sqrt{64-28}}{2}=\frac{8\pm6}{2}$, so $x=1$ or $x=7$. Factoring is more efficient because the trinomial factors quickly.
