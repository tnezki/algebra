Solution 1

For the expression \(9x-4+3y\), identify the terms and the coefficients of the variable terms. Give one factor from each variable term.

Solution:

The terms are \(9x,-4,3y\). The variable coefficients are 9 and 3. Factors include 9 and \(x\) in \(9x\), and 3 and \(y\) in \(3y\).
