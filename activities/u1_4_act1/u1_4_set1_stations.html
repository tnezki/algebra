Unit 1 • Section 1.4: Expressions and StructureProblems 21–24
Classroom Copy 1
Station 6 Answers • Set 1

A museum sells $x$ tickets at 6 dollars each and receives a fixed 30-dollar sponsorship. Write an expression for the total dollars received.

Answer:

$6x+30$.

A rectangle has length $x+5$ and width 3. Write its area in expanded form.

Answer:

$3x+15$.

Four identical bags each contain $x$ pencils and 2 erasers. Write the total number of items in two equivalent forms.

Answer:

$4(x+2)=4x+8$.

A team packs 3 water bottles for each of $x$ players plus 8 extra bottles. Write an expression for the total number of bottles.

Answer:

$3x+8$.
