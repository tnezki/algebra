Classroom Copy 1
Station 6 Answers • Set 2

A fundraiser sells $x$ meals at 9 dollars each and receives a fixed 20-dollar donation. Write an expression for the total dollars collected.

Answer:

$9x+20$.

A rectangle has length $x+2$ and width 6. Write its area in expanded form.

Answer:

$6x+12$.

Five identical shelves each hold $x$ books and 3 binders. Write the total number of items in two equivalent forms.

Answer:

$5(x+3)=5x+15$.

A club orders 4 badges for each of $x$ volunteers plus 5 extra badges. Write an expression for the total number of badges.

Answer:

$4x+5$.
