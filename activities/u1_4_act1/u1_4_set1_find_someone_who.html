Find Someone Who — Set 1 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
1. In \(7x-5y+12\), list the terms, identify the coefficient of x, and identify the constant term.
Solution: Terms: 7x, -5y, 12; coefficient of x is 7; constant is 12.
2. In \(4(3x+2)\), identify the two factors in the outer product, then distribute.
Solution: Factors: 4 and (3x+2). Distributed form: 12x+8.
3. Evaluate \(5a+2b-6\) when a=-3 and b=4. Show substitution first.
Solution: 5(-3)+2(4)-6=-15+8-6=-13.
4. Evaluate \(3x^2+1\) when x=-5 and explain why grouping the negative input matters.
Solution: 3(-5)²+1=3(25)+1=76; parentheses make -5 the squared base.
5. Combine like terms: \(6x-4+3x+11-2x\).
Solution: 7x+7.
6. A student combines \(5x+2x^2\) as \(7x^2\). Explain why the terms cannot be combined.
Solution: The variable parts differ: x and x² are unlike terms.
7. Use the distributive property to rewrite \(-3(4x+7)\).
Solution: -12x-21.
8. Are \(5(x-2)+3\) and \(5x-7\) equivalent? Justify by rewriting one expression.
Solution: Yes. 5(x-2)+3=5x-10+3=5x-7.

Find Someone Who — Set 1 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
9. Solve \(x+9\gt 16\) and describe the endpoint/shading on a number line.
Solution: x>7; open point at 7, shade right.
10. Solve \(x-8\le -3\) and describe the number-line graph.
Solution: x≤5; closed point at 5, shade left.
11. Solve \(6x\lt 42\). State whether the inequality direction changes and why.
Solution: x<7; no change because division is by positive 6.
12. Solve \(-5x\ge 20\) and explain the sign reversal.
Solution: x≤-4; dividing by a negative reverses the inequality.
13. Solve \(x/3\gt 8\) and check one value that belongs to the solution set.
Solution: x>24; for example x=27 gives 9>8.
14. Solve \(x/(-6)\lt 2\) and explain why the direction reverses.
Solution: x>-12; multiplying by -6 reverses the inequality.
15. A student solves \(-4x\gt 28\) and writes x> -7. Identify and correct the error.
Solution: Dividing by -4 requires reversing the sign: x< -7.
16. Write a one-step inequality whose solution is \(x\ge 5\). Then describe the endpoint and shading.
Solution: Example x-2≥3. Closed point at 5, shade right.

Find Someone Who — Set 1 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
17. Start at the origin and move 8 left and 3 up. Write the ordered pair and quadrant.
Solution: (-8,3), Quadrant II.
18. A point is 5 units right and 7 units down. Write the ordered pair and identify the sign of each coordinate.
Solution: (5,-7); x positive, y negative.
19. A graph compares hours studied (input) with quiz score (output). Assign the quantities to x- and y-axes and explain why.
Solution: Hours studied on x-axis, quiz score on y-axis because input is first/horizontal and output second/vertical.
20. A student intends the point (-4,9) but writes (9,-4). Name the error and state the correct coordinate order.
Solution: The coordinates were reversed; correct point is (-4,9), x first then y.
21. A y-axis has labeled values -18, -12, -6, 0 with two equal small intervals between each labeled value. What is one small interval worth?
Solution: 3 units.
22. Consecutive labeled x-values are 0 and 15 with five equal small intervals. What value is three intervals to the right of 0?
Solution: Each interval is 3, so the value is 9.
23. A line crosses the x-axis at 6 and the y-axis at -5. Write both intercepts as ordered pairs.
Solution: x-intercept (6,0); y-intercept (0,-5).
24. A bank-balance graph has y-intercept (0,60). Interpret the intercept and explain how it differs from an x-intercept.
Solution: It means $60 is present at time 0. An x-intercept would be a time/input when the balance reaches 0.
