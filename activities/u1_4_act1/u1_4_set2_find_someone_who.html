Find Someone Who — Set 2 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
1. Simplify \(8x+6+5x-14\) and explain which terms were combined.
Solution: 13x-8; variable terms combine with variable terms and constants with constants.
2. A student simplifies \(4x^2+3x^2-2x\) as \(5x^2\). Diagnose and simplify correctly.
Solution: 4x²+3x²=7x²; -2x is unlike and stays. Result 7x²-2x.
3. Expand \(-5(2x-4)+3\) and simplify, showing the sign of each distributed term.
Solution: -10x+20+3=-10x+23.
4. Expand and simplify \(2(x+6)+3(x-2)\), then check at x=1.
Solution: 2x+12+3x-6=5x+6. At x=1 both forms equal 11.
5. In \(-11x+9\), identify the coefficient, variable term, and constant term. Explain what the negative coefficient means structurally.
Solution: Coefficient -11; variable term -11x; constant 9. The variable term is -11 times x.
6. Create an expression with an x-term, an x²-term, and a constant. Explain which terms cannot be combined.
Solution: Example 3x²-2x+7. The x² and x terms are unlike because exponents differ.
7. Use distribution or substitution to decide whether \(6(x+2)-5\) and \(6x+7\) are equivalent.
Solution: 6x+12-5=6x+7, so yes.
8. Evaluate \(3x^2+2x-4\) at x=-3. Write the substituted expression first.
Solution: 3(-3)²+2(-3)-4=27-6-4=17.

Find Someone Who — Set 2 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
9. A number decreased by 9 is at least 4. Write and solve a one-step inequality, then state the solution in words.
Solution: x-9≥4, so x≥13: the number is at least 13.
10. Solve \(-7x\lt 35\) and explain the inequality reversal.
Solution: x>-5; dividing by -7 reverses < to >.
11. The solution is \(x\lt -1\). Describe the endpoint and shading, then explain why the endpoint is open.
Solution: Open point at -1, shade left; -1 is excluded because the inequality is strict.
12. A student solves \(x/(-5)\ge 4\) as x≥-20. Diagnose and correct.
Solution: Multiplying by -5 reverses the sign: x≤-20.
13. Write a one-step inequality whose solution is \(x\le 9\) and solve it to verify.
Solution: Example x+4≤13 → x≤9.
14. Solve \(x-13\gt -6\) and test one value that works and one that does not.
Solution: x>7. Example 8 works; 7 does not.
15. A team can spend at most $72 on identical shirts costing $9 each. Write and solve an inequality for number s of shirts.
Solution: 9s≤72, so s≤8; at most 8 shirts.
16. Compare \(x\gt 2\) and \(x\ge 2\). Explain the only point where the solution sets differ.
Solution: They differ at x=2: excluded for >, included for ≥.
Answer Key 2

Find Someone Who — Set 2 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.4
17. A student says moving 6 down and 2 right gives (-6,2). Diagnose the coordinate-sign/order error.
Solution: Right gives x=2; down gives y=-6. Correct pair (2,-6).
18. From the origin, move 7 left and 4 up. Write the ordered pair and quadrant.
Solution: (-7,4), Quadrant II.
19. In a graph of gallons used versus minutes, minutes is the input. Assign axes and justify.
Solution: Minutes on x-axis; gallons used on y-axis.
20. A point should represent x=-8 and y=-2, but a student records (-2,-8). Name the error.
Solution: Coordinate reversal; correct point is (-8,-2).
21. The y-axis labels -20, -10, 0, 10 have five equal small intervals between labels. What is one small interval?
Solution: 2 units.
22. A student assumes each grid step is 1, but labeled ticks 0 and 12 have four equal intervals. Correct the scale.
Solution: Each interval is 3 units, not 1.
23. A line crosses the x-axis at -7 and the y-axis at 5. Write the intercepts and explain which is the x-intercept.
Solution: (-7,0) and (0,5). The x-intercept is (-7,0) because y=0 there.
24. A savings graph has y-intercept (0,38). Interpret it using “weeks” and “dollars saved.”
Solution: At week 0, $38 has already been saved.
