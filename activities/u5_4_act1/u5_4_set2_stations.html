Problem 1

For $y=(x+2)(x-7)$, determine the zeros and $x$-intercepts.

Answer:

Zeros $x=-2,7$; intercepts $(-2,0)$ and $(7,0)$.

Problem 2

For $y=(x+5)(x-6)$, determine the zeros and $x$-intercepts.

Answer:

Zeros $x=-5,6$; intercepts $(-5,0)$ and $(6,0)$.

Problem 3

For $y=(x+6)(x-4)$, determine the zeros and $x$-intercepts.

Answer:

Zeros $x=-6,4$; intercepts $(-6,0)$ and $(4,0)$.

Problem 4

For $y=(x-3)(x-7)$, determine the zeros and $x$-intercepts.

Answer:

Zeros $x=3,7$; intercepts $(3,0)$ and $(7,0)$.

Problem 18

A profit model is $P(t)=-(t-5)(t-11)$, where $t$ is weeks after launch. When does the model predict zero profit?

Problem 19

A height model is $h(t)=-4(t+1)(t-6)$. One algebraic zero is $t=-1$. Which zero is meaningful for time after launch, and why?

Answer:

$t=6$; negative time is outside the stated context.

Problem 20

A student says the zeros of $y=(x+5)(x-7)$ are 5 and -7. Diagnose the sign error.

Answer:

Set each factor equal to zero: $x=-5$ and $x=7$.

Problem 21

Without expanding, state where $y=2(x+4)(x-8)$ crosses the $x$-axis.

Answer:

$x=-4$ and $x=8$.

Problem 22

Write a factored-form quadratic with zeros -6 and 6 and leading coefficient 2.

Answer:

$y=2(x+6)(x-6)$.

Problem 23

Compare $y=(x+4)(x-7)$ and $y=3(x+4)(x-7)$. What key intercept feature stays the same?

Answer:

The zeros and $x$-intercepts stay the same; the leading factor changes vertical scale/direction, not the factor zeros.

Problem 24

The table shows two zeros. What factor must correspond to each zero in a factored form?

$x$$y$
$-5$$0$
$4$$0$
Answer:

Factors $(x+5)$ and $(x-4)$.
