Question 1

Does each quadratic function open upward or downward?

  1. $y=(x-2)(x+5)$
  2. $y=-3(x+1)(x-4)$
  3. $y=\frac{1}{2}(x-6)(x+6)$
Solution:
  1. Upward because the leading coefficient is positive.
  2. Downward because the leading coefficient is negative.
  3. Upward because the leading coefficient is positive.

Question 2

Find the y-intercept of each function by evaluating at $x=0$.

  1. $y=(x-4)(x+1)$
  2. $y=2(x-3)(x-5)$
  3. $y=-(x+2)(x-6)$
Solution:
  1. At $x=0$, $y=(-4)(1)=-4$, so the y-intercept is $(0,-4)$.
  2. At $x=0$, $y=2(-3)(-5)=30$, so $(0,30)$.
  3. At $x=0$, $y=-(2)(-6)=12$, so $(0,12)$.

Question 3

A ball's height is modeled by $h(t)=-t^2+5t+6$. Use the graph to estimate when the height is $0$ and explain what that zero means.

Solution:

Factor $h(t)=-t^2+5t+6=-(t-6)(t+1)$.

The zeros are $t=6$ and $t=-1$. In context, the meaningful zero is $t=6$ seconds, when the ball reaches the ground.

Question 4

Compare Graph A and Graph B. Which graph could represent a function with factors $(x+3)$ and $(x-1)$? Support your choice with intercept evidence.

Solution:

Factors $(x+3)$ and $(x-1)$ give zeros $x=-3$ and $x=1$.

Graph A has x-intercepts at $-3$ and $1$, so Graph A matches the factors.

Question 5

If Plan A costs 15 + 4m dollars and Plan B costs 9 + 6m dollars, what does solving $(15+4m)=(9+6m)$ find?

Solution:

It finds the mileage $m$ where the two plans cost the same amount.

Question 6

In a mixture problem with $x$ pounds of nuts and $y$ pounds of dried fruit, what would $x+y=25$ represent?

Solution:

$x+y=25$ represents the total weight: nuts plus dried fruit equals $25$ pounds.

Question 7

For $y\gt -1$, explain why the boundary is horizontal, then graph and describe the solution region in words.

Solution:

The inequality $y\gt -1$ has boundary line $y=-1$, which is horizontal because every point on it has the same $y$-value.

The line is dashed and the solution region is above the line.

Question 8

Use the test point $(0,0)$ to decide whether it is a solution to $y\le 2x-5$. Then state which side should be shaded.

Solution:

Test $(0,0)$: $0\le2(0)-5$ becomes $0\le-5$, which is false.

So shade the side that does not contain $(0,0)$.

Question 12

Question 13

In $g(x)=-f(x)$, name the reflection and describe the output change.

Solution:

$g(x)=-f(x)$ reflects the graph across the $x$-axis. Each output changes to its opposite.

Question 14

In $y=-2|x|$, identify the reflection and stretch.

Solution:

The negative sign reflects $y=|x|$ across the $x$-axis, and the factor $2$ is a vertical stretch by $2$.

Question 15

The table comes from a translated quadratic. Identify the vertex and write a possible equation.

$x$-3-2-101
$y$014916
Solution:

The table matches $y=(x+3)^2$, so the vertex is $(-3,0)$.

A possible equation is $y=(x+3)^2$.

Question 16

Compare the area-model representation and the symbolic expansion for $(x+3)(x+5)$. Explain how the four cells connect to the four products in the distributive method.

Solution:

The area model for $(x+3)(x+5)$ has cells $x^2$, $5x$, $3x$, and $15$.

These are the four distributive products: $x\cdot x$, $x\cdot5$, $3\cdot x$, and $3\cdot5$. Combining the middle terms gives $x^2+8x+15$.
