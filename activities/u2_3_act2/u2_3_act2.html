Unit 2 · Section 3 · Activity 2
Think, Trade, Agree
Writing Linear Equations
Source: practice_set_2_3_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: One whiteboard per person
Structure Indy → Group comparison → Consensus
Setup Groups of 4. Each person has a whiteboard
Directions
  1. Teacher presents problem
  2. Each student solves the problem individually
  3. When all, students done, exchange boards
  4. Discuss differences and justify reasoning
  5. Come to an agreement or ask teacher to help
  6. Repeat exchange with someone different
Goal: Accountability + justification
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Every student has an independent attempt before boards are traded.
  • Students read a partner’s board and name one agreement or disagreement.
  • The group reaches consensus or asks for help with a specific conflict.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Boards are traded before everyone has an attempt.
  • Students copy a stronger board without discussing it.
  • The group asks for help without identifying the disagreement.

Key:
  1. $y=3x+2$.
  2. $y=-6x+9$.
  3. Slope $=\frac{12-3}{5-2}=3$.
  4. $2^4-6=16-6=10$.
