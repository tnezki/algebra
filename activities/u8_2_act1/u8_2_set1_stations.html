Station 1 Answers

Question 1

For $g(x)=-(x+2)^2$, identify the horizontal shift and the reflection.

Solution:

The graph is shifted left 2 units because of $x+2$, and reflected over the $x$-axis because of the negative sign outside the square.

Question 2

If $f(x)=|x|$ has vertex $(0,0)$, what is the vertex of $g(x)=|x-5|-3$?

Solution:

The parent vertex $(0,0)$ moves right 5 and down 3, so the new vertex is $(5,-3)$.

Question 3

Graph $g(x)=(x+2)^2-3$ on the blank coordinate plane. Label the vertex and two symmetric points.

Solution:

The vertex is $(-2,-3)$. Symmetric points include $(-1,-2)$ and $(-3,-2)$, or $(0,1)$ and $(-4,1)$.

Question 4

Decide whether the transformation can be written both as a shift of the parent and as a movement of key points. Build a strategy for checking your answer using $f(x)=|x|$ and $g(x)=|x+4|-1$.

Solution:

Yes. The equation shows a shift left 4 and down 1.

Check by moving the parent vertex $(0,0)$ to $(-4,-1)$ and moving key points such as $(1,1)$ to $(-3,0)$.

Question 5

Find the vertex of $y=(x-4)^2-7$.

Solution:

Vertex form $y=(x-h)^2+k$ gives vertex $(h,k)$. Here $h=4$ and $k=-7$, so the vertex is $(4,-7)$.

Question 6

Solve $(x+3)^2=25$.

Solution:

Take square roots: $x+3=\pm5$. Therefore $x=2$ or $x=-8$.

Question 7

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Completing the Square. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Complete the square to solve $x^2-4x-12=0$.

Solution:

Move the constant: $x^2-4x=12$. Add 4 to both sides: $(x-2)^2=16$.

Then $x-2=\pm4$, so $x=6$ or $x=-2$.

Question 8

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Completing the Square. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Explain how the added constant changes when completing $x^2+bx$ for $b=-14$.

Solution:

The added constant is $(b/2)^2$. For $b=-14$, $(-14/2)^2=(-7)^2=49$.

In a time context, negative time usually occurs before the modeled start and is not meaningful. The meaningful solution is $t=3.4$ seconds, assuming it represents the event after launch/start.

Question 13

Classify each sequence as arithmetic, geometric, or neither.

  1. -7, -3, 1, 5, 9, ...
  2. -64, -16, -4, -1, ...
  3. 1, 0, 1, 4, 9, ...
  4. 0, 2, 4, 8, 16, ...
Solution:

The sequences are: arithmetic with difference 4; geometric with ratio $\frac{1}{4}$; neither for $1,0,1,4,9$ because neither differences nor ratios are constant; neither for $0,2,4,8,16$ as written because ratios are not defined from 0 and differences are not constant.

Question 14

Match each verbal statement to its multiplier.

StatementMultiplier
Increase by 12%
Decrease by 12%
Keep 35% of the amount
Triple each stage
Solution:

Increase by 12% matches 1.12; decrease by 12% matches 0.88; keep 35% matches 0.35; triple each stage matches 3.

Question 15

Each situation has a repeated percent change. Write the multiplier, decide whether the relationship is exponential growth or exponential decay, and calculate the value after 3 time steps if the starting value is 200.

  1. A video gains 30% more views each day.
  2. A medicine level decreases by 18% each hour.
  3. A savings account grows by 2.5% each year.
Solution:

For 30% growth: multiplier 1.30 and $200(1.30)^3=439.4$. For 18% decay: multiplier 0.82 and $200(0.82)^3\approx110.27$.

For 2.5% growth: multiplier 1.025 and $200(1.025)^3\approx215.38$.

Question 16

A model has the form $y=240(0.8)^x$.

  1. Explain why the output can get close to 0 without becoming negative.
  2. Describe what each parameter tells you about the situation.
Solution:

The multiplier 0.8 keeps taking 80% of the previous output, so values stay positive but shrink toward 0. The 240 is the initial value and 0.8 is the decay multiplier.
