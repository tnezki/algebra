Station 1 Answers

Question 1

If $f(x)=2^x$ has $y$-intercept $1$, what is the $y$-intercept of $g(x)=4\cdot 2^x$?

Solution:

At $x=0$, $2^0=1$. Multiplying by 4 makes $g(0)=4\cdot1=4$, so the $y$-intercept is 4.

Question 2

Graph $g(x)=|x-3|+2$ on the blank coordinate plane. Label the vertex and the slope on each side.

Solution:

The vertex is $(3,2)$. The left branch has slope $-1$ and the right branch has slope $1$.

Question 3

Compare the two graphs. Which key features are preserved, and which are changed by the transformation? Use at least two representations in your explanation.

Solution:

A strong comparison should identify the parent feature and the transformed feature: the basic shape is preserved, while the location of key points, intercepts, or asymptote/vertex changes according to the transformation shown.

Question 4

Recommend whether a shifted quadratic or shifted absolute value model is better for a path with a single minimum at $(4,2)$ and equal slopes on both sides. Justify using key features and context.

Solution:

A shifted absolute value model is better because it has a sharp single minimum and equal constant slopes on both sides. A quadratic has a smooth curve and changing slopes.

Question 5

Complete the square: $x^2+12x+20=(x+6)^2+\Box$.

Solution:

Since $(x+6)^2=x^2+12x+36$, $x^2+12x+20=(x+6)^2-16$. The box is $-16$.

Question 6

Identify the axis of symmetry of $y=(x+2)^2-5$.

Solution:

The vertex is $(-2,-5)$, so the axis of symmetry is $x=-2$.

Question 7

State why adding $25$ completes $x^2-10x$.

Solution:

Half of $-10$ is $-5$, and $(-5)^2=25$. Therefore $x^2-10x+25=(x-5)^2$.

Question 8

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Completing the Square. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Solve $x^2+12x+20=0$ by completing the square.

Solution:

Complete the square: $x^2+12x=-20$, so $(x+6)^2=16$. Then $x+6=\pm4$, giving $x=-2$ or $x=-10$.

Use vertex form to decide whether $y=x^2-6x+12$ has real zeros.

Solution:

Rewrite as $y=(x-3)^2+3$. Since $(x-3)^2\ge0$, the minimum value is 3, so the graph has no real zeros.

Question 10

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Quadratic Formula. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Use the quadratic formula to solve $x^2-3x-5=0$ exactly.

Solution:

Use $a=1,b=-3,c=-5$. Then $x=\frac{3\pm\sqrt{(-3)^2-4(1)(-5)}}{2}=\frac{3\pm\sqrt{29}}{2}$.

Question 12

Solution:

$x^2-10x+13=(x-5)^2-12$, so the vertex is $(5,-12)$. A table around $x=5$ such as $x=4,5,6$ gives $-11,-12,-11$, which shows symmetry and the minimum.

Solution:

Ratios are $9/6=1.5$, $13.5/9=1.5$, $20.25/13.5=1.5$, and $30.375/20.25=1.5$. The constant ratio supports an exponential relationship.

Question 14

A relationship has outputs 12, 18, 27, 40.5 at inputs 0, 1, 2, 3. Explain why a constant difference test is not enough, then identify the constant ratio.

Solution:

First differences are not enough because exponential patterns do not have constant differences. The ratios are $18/12=27/18=40.5/27=1.5$, so the constant ratio is 1.5.
