Describe the horizontal and vertical translation from $f(x)=|x|$ to $g(x)=|x-5|+3$.

Answer:

Shift 5 units right and 3 units up.

Answer:

$g(x)=(0.5)^{x+1}$.

Answer:

Answer:

Reflect across the x-axis, vertically compress by factor 0.5, shift left 2, and shift up 5.

The point $(-2,6)$ lies on $y=f(x)$. Under $g(x)=0.5f(x)$, where does that key point move?

Answer:

$(-2,3)$. The x-coordinate stays fixed and the y-value is multiplied by 0.5.

Answer:

$g(t)=R(t)+150$; every point moves up 150 units.

$g(x)=(x-1)^2-2$
