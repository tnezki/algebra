Solution 1

A print shop charges a setup amount of 18 dollars plus 7 dollars per poster. The total is 74 dollars. Define a variable, write an equation, solve it, and interpret the solution.

Solution:

Let \(p\) be the number of posters. \(18+7p=74\), so \(7p=56\) and \(p=8\). The order contains 8 posters.

Solution:
