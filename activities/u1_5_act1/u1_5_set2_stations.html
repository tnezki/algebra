Problem 1

A kayak rental costs 6 dollars plus 3 dollars per hour. The total is 24 dollars. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $6+3h=24$.

Problem 2

A lab has 8 materials already and adds 5 per group until there are 33 materials. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $8+5g=33$.

Problem 3

A cyclist is 3 km from home and travels 4 km each hour until 27 km away. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $3+4h=27$.

Problem 4

A container has 60 liters and drains 7 liters each minute until 25 liters remain. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $60-7m=25$.
