Find Someone Who — Set 1 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.5
1. A museum charges $12 per ticket plus a one-time $6 processing fee. Define a variable for ticket count and write an equation for a total of $54.
Solution: Let t be tickets. 12t+6=54.
2. A tank begins with 70 liters and drains 4 liters per minute. Define variables and write a rule for the amount after m minutes.
Solution: Let m be minutes and V be liters remaining. V=70-4m.
3. Write an equation for “five more than 6 times a number is 47.” Define the variable.
Solution: Let x be the number. 6x+5=47.
4. A student writes \(7+4x=39\) for “four more than 7 times x is 39.” Diagnose the translation error and write the correct equation.
Solution: “7 times x” is 7x and “four more” adds 4: 7x+4=39.
5. Solve \(6x+5=41\) using equivalent steps.
Solution: 6x=36, x=6.
6. Solve \(4(x-3)=28\) and check the result in the original equation.
Solution: x-3=7, x=10. Check: 4(10-3)=28.
7. A club wants exactly $144. It already has $36 and earns $9 per item sold. Write and solve an equation for items sold, then interpret.
Solution: 36+9i=144 → 9i=108 → i=12. The club must sell 12 items.
8. A trail is 45 miles long. After 17 miles, the remaining distance is modeled by \(d+17=45\). Solve and explain d.
Solution: d=28; 28 miles remain.

Solution: A has greater rate 6; B starts higher at 11.

Solution: A rate 6; B rate 4. A has larger constant rate.
11. Plan A costs $10 plus $4 per rental. Plan B costs $18 plus $2 per rental. Compare costs at 5 rentals.
Solution: A=30; B=28. Plan B is cheaper at 5 rentals.
12. A passes through (0,-1) and (4,19). B passes through (0,7) and (4,15). Compare rates and starting values.
Solution: A rate 5, start -1. B rate 2, start 7.
13. Two relationships have output 30 at x=6 but rates 4 and 7. Explain why the shared point does not make them the same relationship.
Solution: Different rates mean they change differently and only happen to meet at that input.
14. One relationship has rate -5 and another has rate 2. Describe how each output changes as x increases.
Solution: First decreases 5 per +1 x; second increases 2 per +1 x.
15. At x=0, A and B both equal 4. At x=3, A=13 and B=22. Use the data to compare their rates.
Solution: A rate=(13-4)/3=3; B rate=(22-4)/3=6. B grows faster.
16. Describe a fair procedure for comparing a relationship given by a context with one given by a table.
Solution: Identify common features such as starting value/rate, or evaluate both at the same inputs and compare outputs.
