Problem 1

A bike rental costs 7 dollars plus 4 dollars per hour. The total is 27 dollars. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $7+4h=27$.

Problem 2

A club has 10 supplies already and buys 3 per student until there are 31 supplies. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $10+3s=31$.

Problem 3

A walker is 5 km from home and travels 2 km each hour until 17 km away. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $5+2h=17$.

Problem 4

A tank has 44 liters and drains 4 liters each minute until 20 liters remain. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $44-4m=20$.
