Problem 1

A canoe rental costs 9 dollars plus 5 dollars per hour. The total is 34 dollars. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $9+5h=34$.

Problem 2

A class has 12 supplies already and buys 4 per student until there are 40 supplies. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $12+4s=40$.

Problem 3

A runner is 2 km from home and travels 3 km each hour until 17 km away. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $2+3h=17$.

Problem 4

A tank has 50 liters and drains 6 liters each minute until 20 liters remain. Define the variable and write an equation. Do not solve yet.

Answer:

One correct model is $50-6m=20$.
