Find Someone Who — Set 2 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.5
1. A music club charges a $30 enrollment fee plus $7 per lesson. Write a cost rule and find the cost after 4 lessons.
Solution: C=30+7l; C=58.
2. A rectangle has perimeter 62 cm and length 19 cm. Write an equation using width w, solve it, and interpret the result.
Solution: 2(19+w)=62 → 19+w=31 → w=12 cm.
3. A student models “three less than five times a number is 32” as \(3-5x=32\). Diagnose and correct the equation.
Solution: “Three less than five times x” is 5x-3, so 5x-3=32.
4. The equation \(8x+4=60\) models eight identical items plus a $4 fee. Solve x and explain what it represents.
Solution: 8x=56, x=7. The item amount/value represented by x is 7 in context.
5. A container starts with 86 L and drains 6 L per minute. Write a rule and determine when it reaches 50 L.
Solution: V=86-6t. 50=86-6t → t=6 minutes.
6. A student solves \(5(x+1)=40\) and claims x=9. Check the claim in the original equation and find the correct solution.
Solution: 5(10)=50, so 9 fails. Divide by 5: x+1=8, x=7.
7. Create a brief context that could be modeled by \(18+5x=53\). Define x and interpret the solution.
Solution: Example: $18 start fee plus $5 per ride totals $53. x=7 rides.
8. An algebraic model for number of seats gives x=-4. Explain why a mathematically valid negative solution could be rejected in context.
Solution: A count of seats cannot be negative; the domain/context makes the value unreasonable.
