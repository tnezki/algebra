Question 1

Find the maximum value of $P(x)=-(x-6)^2+40$.

Solution:

The expression is in vertex form and opens downward because the squared term has a negative coefficient.

The maximum value is the vertex $y$-value, $40$.

Question 2

Interpret the $y$-intercept of $h(t)=-16t^2+32t+6$.

Solution:

The $y$-intercept is $h(0)$.

$h(0)=-16(0)^2+32(0)+6=6$.

In context, it represents the initial height at time $0$.

Question 3

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Quadratic Modeling and Optimization. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

The graph shows height over time. Estimate the maximum height and landing time.

Solution:

From the graph, the maximum occurs near the vertex, about $(5,60)$.

The landing time is the positive time where the graph reaches height $0$, which is about $9$ seconds from the graph.

Question 4

Strategy Construction. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Quadratic Modeling and Optimization. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Design a step-by-step solving strategy for $2x^2+9x-5=0$ that avoids unnecessary work. Then solve it.

Solution:

Use factoring because $a c=2(-5)=-10$ and the numbers $10$ and $-1$ add to $9$.

$2x^2+10x-x-5=0$, so $2x(x+5)-1(x+5)=0$.

$(2x-1)(x+5)=0$, so $x=\frac12$ or $x=-5$. This avoids completing the square or the formula.

The graph becomes narrower/steeper than $y=x^2$, not wider.

Question 13

Identify why $x^2+25$ is not a difference of squares over the integers, even though both terms are squares.

Solution:

A difference of squares must subtract two square terms, such as $x^2-25$.

$x^2+25$ is a sum of squares, so it does not factor over the integers using the difference-of-squares pattern.

Question 14

Factor completely by first removing a common factor: $8x^2-72$.

Solution:

First remove the GCF $8$: $8x^2-72=8(x^2-9)$.

Then factor the difference of squares: $8(x-3)(x+3)$.

Question 15

Factor $2x^2+11x-6$ completely. Include the two binomial factors in an order that makes your check easy to follow.

Solution:

Use $ac=2(-6)=-12$ and sum $11$, so use $12$ and $-1$.

$2x^2+12x-x-6=2x(x+6)-1(x+6)=(2x-1)(x+6)$.

Question 16

A context asks for the maximum height of a quadratic model. A student chooses factored form because it shows the zeros. Is that a reasonable form choice? Give a reasoned argument and name a more useful form if needed.

Solution:

Factored form is reasonable for finding zeros, but it does not show the maximum height directly.

Vertex form is more useful because the vertex gives the maximum height and the time it occurs.
