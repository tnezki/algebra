Teacher & Student Moves Guide

Use across the Activity options
I-Can Moves
I Can 1: I can construct quadratic models from real-world constraints, data, or key features.

Teacher Moves

  • Ask students to make the mathematical evidence explicit.
  • Press for a brief check or justification before moving on.

Student Moves

  • State the claim and the evidence that supports it.
  • Compare reasoning with a partner and revise when needed.
I Can 2: I can extend quadratic models to predict maximums, minimums, intercepts, and meaningful solution values.

Teacher Moves

  • Press for specific visible evidence, not a label alone.
  • Ask students to connect expression structure to the graph feature.

Student Moves

  • Point to the graph feature or expression structure that supports the claim.
  • Check the claim in a second representation when possible.

Participation-Structure Moves
Stations

Teacher Moves

  • Monitor for different strategies and recurring misconceptions.
  • Select and sequence useful work for a later connection.

Student Moves

  • Record a readable strategy on the separate record sheet.
  • Compare methods instead of only checking answers.
Find Someone Who

Teacher Moves

  • Require explanation or checking before a signature.
  • Listen for copied answers without mathematical reasoning.

Student Moves

  • Explain or check one problem with each partner.
  • Record reasoning you understand, not just a final answer.
Rally Coach / Whiteboard Partners

Teacher Moves

  • Coach with questions rather than giving the next step.
  • Require the solver to restate the reasoning before roles switch.

Student Moves

  • Explain one step at a time.
  • Restate your partner’s reasoning before continuing.
Showdown / Mathematical Hot Seat

Teacher Moves

  • Protect individual think time before reveal.
  • Compare strategies after the reveal and surface why they work.

Student Moves

  • Commit to a solution before reveal.
  • Compare and revise after hearing another strategy.
Speed Dating / Think, Trade, Agree

Teacher Moves

  • Prompt partners to compare evidence, not just answers.
  • Ask for a revision when stronger reasoning emerges.

Student Moves

  • Explain the evidence behind your answer.
  • Revise a claim when your partner’s evidence is stronger.
