Question 5

For $y=2(x-1)^2-7$, identify the axis of symmetry.

Solution: The vertex is $(1,-7)$, so the axis of symmetry is $x=1$.

Question 6

For $y=-3(x+2)^2+5$, identify the maximum or minimum value.

Solution: The parabola opens downward because $a=-3\lt 0$. The maximum value is $5$.

Question 7

Name the form that most directly shows the zeros of a quadratic.

Solution: Factored form most directly shows the zeros because each factor can be set equal to zero.

Question 8

The equation $y=2(1.5)^x$ and a context about repeated percent growth are paired. Build a four-value table and explain why the equation and context support the same family.

Solution: A four-value table for $x=0,1,2,3$ is $2,3,4.5,6.75$. Each step multiplies by $1.5$, so the equation and repeated percent growth context both support an exponential family.

Question 13

Factor $4x^3-9x$ in two steps. Name the special pattern applied after removing the GCF.

Solution: First remove the GCF $x$: $4x^3-9x=x(4x^2-9)$. Then use difference of squares: $x(2x-3)(2x+3)$.
