Problem 1

A quadratic has zeros $0$ and $10$ and passes through $(1,-9)$. Construct a factored model.

Answer:

$y=x(x-10)$.

Problem 2

A quadratic has zeros $1$ and $9$ and passes through $(0,-9)$. Construct a factored model.

Answer:

$y=-(x-1)(x-9)$.

Problem 3

A quadratic has zeros $2$ and $8$ and passes through $(0,16)$. Construct a factored model.

Answer:

$y=(x-2)(x-8)$.

Problem 4

A quadratic has zeros $3$ and $7$ and passes through $(0,-21)$. Construct a factored model.

Answer:

$y=-(x-3)(x-7)$.

Problem 13

Use the graph to estimate the maximum point and any visible nonnegative intercepts.

Answer:

Maximum at about $(5,21)$. Intercepts occur where the curve meets the horizontal axis; solve $-(x-5)^2+21=0$ for exact values if needed.

Problem 14

Use the table to identify the vertex and write a vertex-form model.

$x$$y$
$1$$8$
$2$$11$
$3$$12$
$4$$11$
$5$$8$
Answer:

Vertex $(3,12)$; $y=-(x-3)^2+12$.

Problem 15

A rectangle has perimeter 36 m. Let one side be $x$. Write a quadratic area model and determine the maximum area.

Answer:

Other side $18-x$. Area $A=x(18-x)$; maximum occurs at a square with side $9$ m and area $81$ m².

Problem 16

A quadratic model has algebraic solutions $x=-4$ and $x=8$, but the context restricts $0\le x\le 12$. Which solution should be reported and why?

Answer:

Report $x=8$ because $x=-4$ lies outside the modeled domain.

Problem 21

Describe the transformations from $y=x^2$ to $y=-2(x-4)^2+3$.

Answer:

Right 4, vertical stretch by factor 2, reflect across the $x$-axis, and up 3.

Problem 22

Apply $(x,y)\to(x+3,-4y)$ to the point $(3,4)$.

Answer:

$(6,-16)$.

Problem 23

Do $y=4(x+3)^2-5$ and the point set obtained by shifting parent points left 3, stretching outputs by 4, then down 5 describe the same transformation? Explain.

Answer:

Yes. The equation encodes exactly those transformations.

Problem 24

Write an equation for $y=|x|$ reflected over the $x$-axis, vertically stretched by 4, shifted right 3, and shifted up 3.

Answer:

$y=-4|x-3|+3$.
