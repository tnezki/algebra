Question 13

At $x=2$, Relationship A has the larger output, so Lee says A must always grow faster. Explain why one output value cannot establish which relationship grows faster.
Answer:

A single output does not determine growth rate. Compare slopes/rates separately from starting values.

Question 14

A graph of Line A uses 1 unit per grid square; a graph of Line B uses 10 units per grid square. Line B looks steeper, so Pat says B must have the greater slope. Explain why visual steepness alone is not enough when the graph scales differ.
Answer:

The conclusion is not justified from visual steepness alone because the axis scales differ. Compare numerical rate values.

Question 15

Two lines have the same slope but different $y$-intercepts. Casey says they represent the same relationship. Decide whether equal slopes are enough to make two linear relationships identical.
Answer:

False. Equal slope means parallel change, but different intercepts give different output values.

Question 16

For Relationship A, $y=5x$, and Relationship B, $y=3x+12$, find the input where their outputs are equal and identify which relationship has the greater rate of change.
Answer:

Set the equations equal: the outputs match at $x=6$, where $y=30$. A has the greater rate because its slope is 5.
