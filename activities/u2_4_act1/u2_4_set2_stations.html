13.

Line A has slope 4 and $y$-intercept -2. Line B has slope 4 and $y$-intercept 5. Devon says the lines represent the same relationship because their slopes match. Assess the claim.

The claim is false. Equal slopes give the same rate of change, but different intercepts make the relationships different.

14.

At $x=1$, Relationship A has a greater output than Relationship B. Morgan says A must have the greater slope. Explain why that conclusion does not follow.

One output value does not determine slope. Compare rates of change, not just a single output.
