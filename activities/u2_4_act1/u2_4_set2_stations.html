Question 13

For $r(x)=|x-5|$, find $r(5)$.

Solution:
  1. Substitute $5$ for $x$.
  2. $r(5)=|5-5|=0$.

Question 14

For $f(x)=|x-3|+5$, find all input values that give an output of $10$.

Solution:
  1. Solve $|x-3|+5=10$.
  2. $|x-3|=5$.
  3. $x-3=5$ or $x-3=-5$, so $x=8$ or $x=-2$.
