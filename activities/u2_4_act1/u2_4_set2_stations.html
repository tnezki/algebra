Problem 13

Line A has slope 4 and $y$-intercept -2. Line B has slope 4 and $y$-intercept 5. Devon says the lines represent the same relationship because their slopes match. Assess the claim.

Answer:

The claim is false. Equal slopes give the same rate of change, but different intercepts make the relationships different.

Problem 14

At $x=1$, Relationship A has a greater output than Relationship B. Morgan says A must have the greater slope. Explain why that conclusion does not follow.

Answer:

One output value does not determine slope. Compare rates of change, not just a single output.

Problem 15

Two graphs use different axis scales. The line that looks steeper is declared to have the greater slope. Assess the reasoning.

Answer:

Visual steepness is not reliable when scales differ; compare numerical changes in $y$ and $x$.

Problem 16

For Relationship A, $y=4x-8$, and Relationship B, $y=x+13$, find the input where their outputs are equal and identify which relationship has the greater rate of change.

Answer:

Set the equations equal: the outputs match at $x=7$, where $y=20$. A has the greater rate because its slope is 4.
