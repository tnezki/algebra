Problem 17

Factor out the greatest common factor: $8x^3-12x^2+4x$.

Answer:

$4x(2x^2-3x+1)$.

Problem 18

Rewrite $10x^2+15x$ by factoring out a common factor that makes later work easier.

Answer:

$5x(2x+3)$.

Problem 19

A student writes $12x^2-18x=6(2x^2-3x)$. Is this fully factored by the GCF? Explain.

Answer:

No. The GCF includes $x$, so a fuller factorization is $6x(2x-3)$.

Problem 20

Factor $-15x^3+20x^2$ using the greatest common factor.

Answer:

$-5x^2(3x-4)$ is one useful factorization.
