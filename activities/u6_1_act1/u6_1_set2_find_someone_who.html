Problem 17

For $f(x)=(x+6)(x-7)$, state the zeros and x-intercepts.

Answer:

Zeros $x=-6,7$; intercepts $(-6,0),(7,0)$.

Problem 18

A quadratic graph crosses the $x$-axis at $x=-4$ and $x=7$. Write a monic factored form.

Answer:

$f(x)=(x+4)(x-7)$.

Problem 19

Explain the connection among a factor $(x-8)$, the zero $x=8$, and the graph.

Answer:

The factor equals zero at $x=8$, so the function value is zero and the graph has an $x$-intercept at $(8,0)$.

Problem 20

Write a factored form with zeros $-6$ and $3$, then verify both zeros by substitution.

Answer:

$f(x)=(x+6)(x-3)$; substituting either zero makes one factor equal to $0$.

Problem 21

For $f(x)=(x-4)(x+7)$, state the $x$-intercepts and direction of opening.

Answer:

Intercepts $(4,0),(-7,0)$; the positive leading coefficient means the parabola opens upward.

Problem 22

A student claims $g(x)=-(x+3)^2+5$ opens upward. Critique the claim.

Answer:

The leading multiplier is negative, so the parabola opens downward. The claim is false.

Problem 23

Which feature is most visible in $h(x)=-2(x-6)^2-3$? State it.

Answer:

The vertex $(6,-3)$ is visible immediately.

Problem 24

Connect $p(x)=(x+5)(x-4)$ to two graph features.

Answer:

The $x$-intercepts are $(-5,0)$ and $(4,0)$; the positive leading coefficient means the parabola opens upward.
