Station 1 Answers

Question 1

Solve $x(x-9)=0$.

Solution:

Use the zero product property: $x=0$ or $x-9=0$.

So the solutions are $x=0$ and $x=9$.

Question 2

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Solve $3x^2+12x=0$ and connect each solution to an intercept.

Solution:

Factor the left side: $3x^2+12x=3x(x+4)$.

Set each factor equal to zero: $3x=0$ or $x+4=0$.

The solutions are $x=0$ and $x=-4$; on the graph these are the $x$-intercepts.

Question 3

Reasonableness/Estimation Argument. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

A model gives solutions $t=-0.2$ and $t=3.4$ seconds. Decide which solution(s) are meaningful and justify using the context and the equation form.

Solution:

In a time context, negative time usually represents a value before the model starts, so $t=-0.2$ seconds is not meaningful for the event after launch.

The meaningful solution is $t=3.4$ seconds. In factored form, the zeros tell when the modeled quantity is zero, but context restricts the domain to $t\ge 0$.

Question 9

A ball is dropped and its height values for equal time steps are $96, 72, 48, 24, 0$. Build the difference pattern, choose a likely family for this interval, and explain why the model should stop at the last value.

Solution:

The first differences are $-24,-24,-24,-24$.

Over this interval the data are linear, but the model should stop at $0$ because negative height after landing would not fit the context.

Question 10

A bank account starts at 500 dollars and earns 4 percent interest each year. Write the multiplier, build a three-year table, and explain how the repeated percent change supports the family.

Solution:

A $4\%$ increase means multiplier $1.04$.

Three-year table: year $0:500$, year $1:520$, year $2:540.80$, year $3:562.432$.

Repeated multiplication by $1.04$ supports an exponential model.

Question 11

A classmate says $y=(x-4)^2+6$ moves $y=x^2$ left $4$ and up $6$ because the equation contains $x-4$. Explain why that translation is not reasonable and give the correct movement.

Solution:

In $y=(x-4)^2+6$, the expression $x-4$ means the input must be $4$ to make the squared part zero.

So the graph moves right $4$ and up $6$, not left $4$.

Question 12

Create a data set for inputs $-2,-1,0,1,2$ that could be confused at first glance. It must include one repeated output and a nonconstant first-difference pattern. Then add enough evidence to prove whether it is linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value.

Solution:

Sample data: $y=4,1,0,1,4$.

It has repeated outputs and nonconstant first differences $-3,-1,1,3$. The constant second differences show it is quadratic.

Question 13

Complete the coefficient-and-variable-power table for $18x^3-12x^2+6x$.

Term$18x^3$$-12x^2$$6x$
Coefficient_______________
Power of $x$_______________
Solution:

For $18x^3$, coefficient $18$ and power $3$.

For $-12x^2$, coefficient $-12$ and power $2$.

For $6x$, coefficient $6$ and power $1$.

Question 14

The expression $24t+18$ represents the total number of tiles in two repeated border designs. Write an equivalent product and explain what each factor could represent in the design.

Solution:

The GCF is $6$, so $24t+18=6(4t+3)$.

In a design, $6$ could represent six repeated groups, each with $4t+3$ tiles.

Question 15

Factor $12x^2+30x$ in two different first steps: one using a common factor of $6x$ and one using a common factor of $3x$. Compare the results and explain why one final answer is considered completely factored.

Solution:

Using $6x$: $12x^2+30x=6x(2x+5)$.

Using $3x$: $12x^2+30x=3x(4x+10)=3x\cdot 2(2x+5)=6x(2x+5)$.

The completely factored form is $6x(2x+5)$ because no common factor remains.

Question 16

A student designed the area model shown to represent a product, but the model does not support the claimed expression $5m^2-12m-6$.

Revise either the expression or one part of the model so the representations match. Then write the matching product and sum.

Solution:

Check the area model by multiplying the row and column labels and combining cells.

If the cells do not sum to $5m^2-12m-6$, revise either a cell or the claimed expression so the product and sum match. A valid final answer must have matching product and combined polynomial.
