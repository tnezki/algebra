Graphing Linear Functions
Source: practice_set_2_2_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Partners
Materials: One whiteboard and one marker between partners
Structure Fast partner practice
Setup Work with your partner. One whiteboard and one marker between you.
Directions
  1. Work with a partner.
  2. Both students stay engaged.
  3. Alternate who writes.
  4. Write clearly and large.
  5. Stay focused and collaborate.
  6. Fix mistakes and discuss.
Goal: Engagement + quick feedback
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Partners alternate who writes while both continue explaining.
  • The non-writing partner checks the reasoning before moving on.
  • Corrections are discussed and rewritten clearly on the board.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • One partner writes every problem while the other watches.
  • Work is too small or incomplete to compare.
  • Mistakes are erased without a conversation about what changed.

Unit 2.2 · Activity 2 Exit Ticket Answer Key
Exit Ticket
1.Current · DOK 1–2

For $y=-2x+8$, describe how to move from one point to the next using slope.

2.Current · DOK 1–2

Does $(0,1),(1,4),(2,7)$ look linear? Explain with differences.

3.Last · DOK 1–2

Evaluate $f(5)$ for $f(x)=3x-2$.

4.Mastery · DOK 1–2

Find $|{-9}|-4$.

Key:
  1. From one point to the next, move right $1$ and down $2$.
  2. Yes. The outputs increase by $3$ each time.
  3. $f(5)=3(5)-2=13$.
  4. $|-9|-4=9-4=5$.
