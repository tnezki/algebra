Source: practice_set_2_5_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: Copies of puzzle
Structure Matching puzzle (self-checking)
Setup Groups of 4. Copies of puzzle
Directions
  1. Students match expressions, answers, or steps.
  2. Pieces form a complete shape (triangle/square).
  3. If correct, puzzle fits perfectly.
Goal: Error detection + pattern recognition
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students match each question side to a mathematically equivalent answer side.
  • Groups use disagreements to recheck calculations before forcing pieces together.
  • The final shape self-checks because all interior matches agree.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students match pieces by shape only without solving.
  • Groups trim or overlap pieces to make an incorrect match fit.
  • One student assembles while others stop checking mathematics.

1.Current · DOK 1–2

2.Current · DOK 1–2

A model predicts 50 when $x=8$ and 65 when $x=11$. Find the average rate.

3.Last · DOK 1–2

For $y=-4x+7$, identify the slope and intercept.

4.Mastery · DOK 1–2

Simplify $\frac{3}{5}+\frac{1}{5}$.

Key:
  1. No clear association.
  2. Average rate $=\frac{65-50}{11-8}=5$.
  3. Slope $=-4$ and intercept $=7$.
  4. $\frac{3}{5}+\frac{1}{5}=\frac{4}{5}$.
