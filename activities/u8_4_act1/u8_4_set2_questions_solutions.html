Write a model for revenue with zeros at prices $0$ and $12$ and revenue $72$ at $p=6$.

Solution:

Use $R(p)=a p(p-12)$. Since $R(6)=72$, $72=a(6)(-6)=-36a$, so $a=-2$.

Synthesize a coefficient map, a discriminant statement, and a graph-sketch description for $2x^2+4x+5=0$. Explain why all three indicate no real solutions.

Solution:

For $2x^2+4x+5=0$, $a=2,b=4,c=5$. The discriminant is $4^2-4(2)(5)=16-40=-24$, which is negative.

The graph opens upward and stays above the $x$-axis, so there are no real solutions.
