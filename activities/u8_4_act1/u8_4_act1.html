Setup 8 stations around the room. Pick up a record sheet before you begin.
Directions
  1. Get record sheet (8 stations).
  2. Choose a starting station (max 4 students).
  3. Work for 3 minutes.Rotate when teacher signals.
  4. Cannot all go to the same next station.
Goal: Repetition + varied problem exposure
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students begin at different stations and rotate when signaled.
  • Each station group works on the four assigned cards and records work separately.
  • Students use the answer cards to check reasoning before the next rotation.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • All groups crowd the same station.
  • Students write on the station cards instead of their record sheet.
  • Groups skip discussion and wait for the answer page.

Question 1

Define extrapolation in a modeling situation.
Solution:

Extrapolation means using a model to predict outside the range of observed data. It is often less reliable because the pattern may not continue beyond the data set.

Question 2

A residual is positive. State whether the model overpredicted or underpredicted the actual value.
Solution:

A residual is actual minus predicted. If the residual is positive, the actual value is above the prediction, so the model underpredicted.

Question 3

Use $h(t)=-16t^2+48t+5$ to predict the height at $t=2$ seconds. Then state one domain limit for the model.
Solution:

Evaluate $h(2)=-16(4)+48(2)+5=-64+96+5=37$. One domain limit is that time should be nonnegative and should stop when the object hits the ground.

Question 4

Station 2 Answers

Question 5

Which method fits $x^2+6x+2=0$ best if exact answers are required?
Solution:

The quadratic formula or completing the square is best because $x^2+6x+2=0$ does not factor nicely and exact radical answers are expected.

Question 6

Which method fits $3x^2+10x+8=0$ best?
Solution:

Factoring fits well because $3x^2+10x+8=(3x+4)(x+2)$. Then $x=-\frac43$ or $x=-2$.

Question 7

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Choosing and Defending Solution Methods . Show the algebra that supports your answer. Solve $4x^2-25=0$ using structure, not the full formula.
Solution:

Use difference of squares: $4x^2-25=(2x-5)(2x+5)=0$. Thus $x=\frac52$ or $x=-\frac52$.

Question 8

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Quadratic Modeling and Optimization . Show the algebra that supports your answer. A ball has height $h(t)=-16t^2+40t+6$. Find when it hits the ground.
Solution:

Set height equal to 0: $-16t^2+40t+6=0$. Using the formula gives $t=\frac{-40\pm\sqrt{40^2-4(-16)(6)}}{-32}$.

The positive solution is about $2.64$ seconds.

The dimensions are $x=6$ and $24-2x=12$, which maximize the area.

Question 13

Identify whether each description gives enough information to write a unique model $y=ab^x$. Initial value 12 and multiplier 3. The output gets bigger each step. The first two outputs are 8 and 20 at x-values 0 and 1. Th
Solution:

Initial value 12 and multiplier 3 is enough. Output gets bigger is not enough.

The first two outputs 8 and 20 at $x=0,1$ are enough because $a=8$ and $b=20/8=2.5$. A curved-up graph alone is not enough.

Question 14

Find the missing multiplier from consecutive outputs. Model 0 1 2 Multiplier A 9 27 81 B 64 16 4 C 10 12 14.4
Solution:

A: $27/9=3$ and $81/27=3$. B: $16/64=\frac14$ and $4/16=\frac14$.

C: $12/10=1.2$ and $14.4/12=1.2$.

Question 15

A ball is dropped from 160 cm and rebounds to 120 cm after the first bounce. Assume the rebound height is multiplied by the same factor after each bounce. Write a rule for the height after n bounces. Predict the height a
Solution:

The multiplier is $120/160=0.75$. A rule is $h(n)=160(0.75)^n$.

After 4 bounces, $h(4)=160(0.75)^4=50.625$ cm.

Question 16

Compare the table and context to choose the best family. Week 0 1 2 3 4 Members 20 30 45 67.5 101.25 Context: The club grows by half of its current size each week. Use both representations to support a family choice.
Solution:

The context says multiply by 1.5 each week. The table also has ratios $30/20=45/30=67.5/45=101.25/67.5=1.5$, so an exponential model is best.
