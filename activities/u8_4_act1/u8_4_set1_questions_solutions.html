Last · DOK 2

A ball is dropped from 160 cm and rebounds to 120 cm after the first bounce. Assume the rebound height is multiplied by the same factor after each bounce.

  1. Write a rule for the height after n bounces.
  2. Predict the height after 4 bounces.
Solution:

The multiplier is $120/160=0.75$. A rule is $h(n)=160(0.75)^n$.

After 4 bounces, $h(4)=160(0.75)^4=50.625$ cm.
