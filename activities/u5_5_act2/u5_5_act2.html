Comparing Quadratic Forms
Source: practice_set_5_5_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: Copies of puzzle
StructureMatching puzzle (self-checking)
SetupGroups of 4. Copies of puzzle
Directions
  1. Students match expressions, answers, or steps.
  2. Pieces form a complete shape (triangle/square).
  3. If correct, puzzle fits perfectly.
Goal: Error detection + pattern recognition
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students justify each match before placing a puzzle piece.
  • The group uses mismatched edges as feedback and revises the pair.
  • Students check that the completed shape is consistent before asking for confirmation.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • One student builds the puzzle while others watch.
  • Pieces are matched by shape only without reading the math.
  • The group asks for the answer key before testing edge matches.
