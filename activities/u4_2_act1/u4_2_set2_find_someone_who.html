Problem 1

A student says $y=(x-3)^2+2$ moves $y=x^2$ left 3 units and up 2 units. Identify and correct the horizontal-shift error.

Answer:

Inside the input, the sign acts oppositely: $x-3$ shifts the graph right 3 units. The vertical shift up 2 units is correct.

Problem 2

Start with $y=x$. Translate the graph 4 units right and 1 unit up. Write an equation for the translated function.

Answer:

$y=(x-4)+1$

Problem 3

Describe the translation from $y=x^2$ to $y=(x+1)^2+5$.

Answer:

1 unit left and 5 units up.

Problem 4

Translate $y=x^2$ 2 units right and 3 units up. Complete the translated coordinates for the three key points.

parent pointtranslated point
(-1,1)?
(0,0)?
(1,1)?
Answer:

(1,4), (2,3), (3,4).

Problem 13

Predict how every key point of $y=x^2$ moves when the equation changes to a graph shifted 3 units right and 3 units down. State the new vertex and the image of $(1,1)$.

parent point
(0,0)
(1,1)
Answer:

The vertex moves to $(3,-3)$, and $(1,1)$ moves to $(4,-2)$.

Problem 14

Use the graph pair to predict the signs that must appear in the translated equation. Explain the horizontal-sign convention.

Answer:

The graph is shifted left 4 units and down 2 units, so a matching equation is $y=(x+4)^2-2$. The horizontal sign appears opposite the direction of the shift because it changes the input.

Problem 15

Use the graph pair to predict the signs that must appear in the translated equation. Explain the horizontal-sign convention.

Answer:

The graph is shifted right 2 units and up 3 units, so a matching equation is $y=|x-2|+3$. The horizontal sign appears opposite the direction of the shift because it changes the input.

Problem 16

A student says $y=(x-4)^2-1$ translates the parent function left 4 and down 1. Identify the error and give the correct translation.

Answer:

The horizontal direction is reversed. The correct translation is right 4 and down 1. The sign inside the input acts opposite the visible horizontal movement.
