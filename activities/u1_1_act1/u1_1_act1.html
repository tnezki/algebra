Participation-Structure Moves
Stations

Teacher Moves

  • Monitor for different strategies and recurring misconceptions.
  • Select and sequence useful work for a later connection.

Student Moves

  • Leave a readable strategy at each station.
  • Compare methods instead of only checking answers.
Find Someone Who

Teacher Moves

  • Require explanation/checking before a signature.
  • Listen for copied answers without mathematical reasoning.

Student Moves

  • Explain or check one problem with each partner.
  • Record reasoning you understand, not just a final answer.
Rally Coach / Whiteboard Partners

Teacher Moves

  • Coach with questions rather than giving the next step.
  • Require the solver to restate reasoning before roles switch.

Student Moves

  • Explain one step at a time.
  • Restate your partner’s reasoning before continuing.
Showdown / Mathematical Hot Seat

Teacher Moves

  • Protect individual think time before reveal or description.
  • After reveal, compare strategies and surface why they work.

Student Moves

  • Commit to a solution before comparing.
  • Use precise mathematical language to defend reasoning.
Speed Dating / Think, Trade, Agree

Teacher Moves

  • Ask pairs to compare evidence, not only final answers.
  • Prompt revision when a new partner exposes a stronger method.

Student Moves

  • Share a strategy and ask one clarifying question.
  • Revise a claim or method when the evidence warrants it.

Whiteboard Indy

Multiple Representations of Relationships
← Activity Options
Structure

Individual → Independent practice

Setup

Each student gets a whiteboard and one marker.

Directions
  1. Notes are welcome.
  2. Try something before asking for an answer.
  3. Write large and clearly.
  4. Use a partner for reasoning, not copying.
  5. Fix mistakes and discuss what changed.
Goal: Individual accountability + low-stakes problem entry
Question sets

Set 1 · Set 2

Looks Like Success
  • Students show visible mathematical reasoning.
  • Partners use evidence and precise language.
  • Errors are revised after discussion.
Doesn’t Look Like
  • Copying an answer without explanation.
  • One student doing all of the thinking.
  • Moving on while a disagreement is unresolved.

Rally Coach

Multiple Representations of Relationships
← Activity Options
Structure

Partner explanation + alternating roles

Setup

Partners share a workspace; one explains while one records.

Directions
  1. Partner A explains and Partner B records.
  2. Coach with questions, not answers.
  3. Switch roles after each problem.
  4. Both partners verify the final response.
