A water cooler contains 45 liters and loses 5 liters each minute. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ minutes. Model the situation with an equation and interpret the constant term in context.

Answer:

$y=-5x+45$. The starting value 45 is the amount when $x=0$.

A concert line has 11 people at first, and 7 people join each minute. Let $y$ be the number of people after $x$ minutes. Represent the relationship with an equation, then explain the amount present when $x=0$.

Answer:

$y=7x+11$. The starting value 11 is the number of people when $x=0$.

A phone battery begins at 96 percent and decreases by 8 percentage points each hour. Let $y$ be the battery percentage after $x$ hours. Create an equation for the situation and identify the initial amount before any change occurs.

Answer:

$y=-8x+96$. The starting value 96 is the battery percentage when $x=0$.

A runner starts 2 km beyond a checkpoint and moves away at 5 km per hour. Let $y$ be the distance from the checkpoint after $x$ hours. Give a rule for $y$ in terms of $x$ and explain the y-intercept using the situation.

Answer:

$y=5x+2$. The starting value 2 is the distance from the checkpoint when $x=0$.

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-2)$ and $(4,0)$. Build an equation through both points and identify its rate of change.

Answer:

Two points from a relationship are $(0,5)$ and $(2,7)$. Determine a linear rule that fits both points, including the rate per unit.

Answer:

Answer:

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-3)$ and $(2,3)$. Find a matching equation and justify the rate from the change between the points.

Answer:

The rate is $3$ and a possible equation is $y=3x-3$.
