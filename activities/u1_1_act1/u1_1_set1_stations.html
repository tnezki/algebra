Answer:

$y=6x+14$. The starting value 14 is the number of passengers when $x=0$.

A candle is 30 cm tall and burns 3 cm each hour. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ hours. Give a rule for $y$ in terms of $x$ and explain the y-intercept using the situation.

Answer:

$y=-3x+30$. The starting value 30 is the amount when $x=0$.

Station 6 Answers • Set 1

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-2)$ and $(2,4)$. Build an equation through both points and identify its rate of change.

Answer:

The rate is $3$ and a possible equation is $y=3x-2$.

Two points from a relationship are $(0,5)$ and $(4,7)$. Determine a linear rule that fits both points, including the rate per unit.

Answer:

The rate is $\frac{1}{2}$ and a possible equation is $y=\frac{1}{2}x+5$.

Two points from a relationship are $(0,1)$ and $(2,3)$. Use the two points to reconstruct the equation and report the slope.

Answer:

The rate is $1$ and a possible equation is $y=x+1$.

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-3)$ and $(2,-7)$. Find a matching equation and justify the rate from the change between the points.

Answer:

The rate is $-2$ and a possible equation is $y=-2x-3$.
