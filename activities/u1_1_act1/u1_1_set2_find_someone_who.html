Problem 9

A tank contains 40 liters and drains 3 liters each minute. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ minutes. Model the situation with an equation and interpret the constant term in context.

Answer:

$y=-3x+40$. The starting value 40 is the amount when $x=0$.

Problem 10

A parking lot has 18 cars at opening and 5 cars enter each minute. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ minutes. Represent the relationship with an equation, then explain the amount present when $x=0$.

Answer:

$y=5x+18$. The starting value 18 is the amount when $x=0$.

Problem 11

A candle is 24 cm tall and burns 2 cm each hour. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ hours. Create an equation for the situation and identify the initial amount before any change occurs.

Answer:

$y=-2x+24$. The starting value 24 is the amount when $x=0$.

Problem 12

A hiker starts 3 km from camp and walks away at 4 km per hour. Let $y$ be the amount after $x$ hours. Give a rule for $y$ in terms of $x$ and explain the y-intercept using the situation.

Answer:

$y=4x+3$. The starting value 3 is the amount when $x=0$.

Problem 21

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-2)$ and $(4,0)$. Build an equation through both points and identify its rate of change.

Answer:

The rate is $\frac{1}{2}$ and a possible equation is $y=\frac{1}{2}x-2$.

Problem 22

Two points from a relationship are $(0,5)$ and $(2,7)$. Determine a linear rule that fits both points, including the rate per unit.

Answer:

The rate is $1$ and a possible equation is $y=x+5$.

Problem 23

Two points from a relationship are $(0,1)$ and $(2,-3)$. Use the two points to reconstruct the equation and report the slope.

Answer:

The rate is $-2$ and a possible equation is $y=-2x+1$.

Problem 24

Two points from a relationship are $(0,-3)$ and $(2,3)$. Find a matching equation and justify the rate from the change between the points.

Answer:

The rate is $3$ and a possible equation is $y=3x-3$.
