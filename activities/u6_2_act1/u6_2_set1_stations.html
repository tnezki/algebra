Problem 19

If your goal is to find the zeros of a quadratic with the least extra work, which form is usually most useful? Explain briefly.

Answer:

Factored form, because the factors can be set equal to zero directly.

Problem 20

Which quadratic form is usually most useful for identifying a maximum or minimum quickly? Explain.

Answer:

Vertex form, because the vertex coordinates are visible in $a(x-h)^2+k$.

Problem 21

Describe the translation from $y=x^2$ to $y=(x-4)^2-3$.

Answer:

Right 4 and down 3.

Problem 22

Write an equation for $y=x^2$ shifted right 3 and up 2.

Answer:

$y=(x-3)^2+2$.

Problem 23

The parent point $(2,3)$ is shifted left 2 and up 3. Give the new point.

Answer:

$(0,6)$.

Problem 24

Explain why $y=(x-5)^2$ shifts the graph right rather than left.

Answer:

The inside expression is zero at $x=5$, so the vertex moves from $x=0$ to $x=5$, which is 5 units right.
