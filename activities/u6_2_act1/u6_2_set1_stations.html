Station 1 Answers

Question 1

Solve $(x+3)^2=25$.

Solution:

Take square roots: $x+3=\pm 5$.

So $x=2$ or $x=-8$.

Question 2

Complete the square: $x^2+12x+20=(x+6)^2+\Box$.

Solution:

Expand $(x+6)^2=x^2+12x+36$.

To match $x^2+12x+20$, the constant must be $20-36=-16$.

So $x^2+12x+20=(x+6)^2-16$.

Question 3

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Completing the Square. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

The graph shows a quadratic. Use the vertex to write a vertex-form equation.

Solution:

From the graph, the vertex is about $(4,-4)$ and the zeros appear near $2$ and $6$.

A matching vertex-form model is $y=(x-4)^2-4$.

Question 4

Strategy Construction. Analyze the quadratic evidence for Completing the Square. Write a conclusion and justify it with algebraic or graphical evidence.

Design a step-by-step solving strategy for $2x^2+9x-5=0$ that avoids unnecessary work. Then solve it.

Solution:

Use factoring because $a c=2(-5)=-10$ and the numbers $10$ and $-1$ add to $9$.

$2x^2+10x-x-5=0$, so $2x(x+5)-1(x+5)=0$.

$(2x-1)(x+5)=0$, so $x=\frac12$ or $x=-5$. This avoids completing the square or the formula.

Question 5

For $g(x)=f(x+3)-2$, describe the horizontal and vertical translations.

Solution:

The $x+3$ shifts the graph left $3$ units.

The $-2$ shifts the graph down $2$ units.

Question 6

The parent vertex $(0,0)$ moves to $(5,-1)$. Write the translation in words.

Solution:

The vertex moves right $5$ and down $1$.

Question 7

Use the table to decide whether $h$ is a horizontal translation of $f$. Explain how the input pattern shifted.

$x$-2-101
$f(x)$4101
$h(x)$1014
Solution:

The row for $h$ is $1,0,1,4$, which matches $f$ values shifted one input to the left.

So $h$ is a horizontal translation of $f$ left $1$ unit.

Station 3 Answers

Question 9

Write an equation for a translation of $y=|x|$ with vertex $(-4,6)$. Then explain how the equation shows the move.

Solution:

Use vertex form $y=|x-h|+k$.

With vertex $(-4,6)$, one equation is $y=|x+4|+6$.

Question 10

Two students graph $y=-\frac{1}{2}(x-2)^2+4$. One transforms the vertex first; the other transforms a table of parent points first. Explain both methods and why they should produce the same graph.

Solution:

Vertex-first method: move the parent vertex to $(2,4)$, reflect over the $x$-axis because of the negative coefficient, and vertically compress by $\frac12$.

Table method: start with parent points, shift right $2$, multiply outputs by $-\frac12$, then shift up $4$.

Both methods apply the same transformations, so they produce the same graph.

Question 11

A student predicts that $y=4(x-1)^2-2$ is wider than $y=x^2$ because it is multiplied by $4$. Make a reasonableness argument to support or reject the prediction.

Solution:

Reject the prediction. A factor of $4$ outside the square multiplies all vertical distances from the vertex by $4$.

The graph becomes narrower/steeper than $y=x^2$, not wider.

Question 12

Create a translated absolute value function that has vertex $(3, -2)$ and passes through a point with output $5$. Include the equation, two supporting points, and a short context where the vertex has meaning.

Solution:

One example is $y=|x-3|-2$. It has vertex $(3,-2)$.

To pass through output $5$, use points where $|x-3|-2=5$, so $|x-3|=7$ and $x=10$ or $x=-4$.

Context: a penalty is smallest at target value $3$ and increases as distance from $3$ grows.

Question 13

Find the missing outside factor in each product.

  1. $\_\_\_(x+6)=5x+30$
  2. $\_\_\_(2m-7)=6m^2-21m$
  3. $\_\_\_(4p+1)=16p^2+4p$
Solution:

a. $5(x+6)=5x+30$.

b. $3m(2m-7)=6m^2-21m$.

c. $4p(4p+1)=16p^2+4p$.

Question 14

Which term prevents all three terms from sharing a factor of $4x$?

$12x^2+20x+16$

State the term and the greatest common factor the three terms do share.

Solution:

The term $16$ prevents all three terms from sharing $4x$ because it has no factor of $x$.

The three terms share a greatest common factor of $4$.

Question 15

Build a factored expression that expands to $15q^2-10q+20$. Show the common factor and the remaining polynomial.

Solution:

The GCF is $5$.

$15q^2-10q+20=5(3q^2-2q+4)$.

Question 16

Analyze the structure of $kx^2+7x-6$. Find two possible integer values of $k$ that make the trinomial factorable over the integers, and explain how the product-sum structure changes.

Solution:

For $k=1$, $x^2+7x-6$ is not integer-factorable, but choose $k=2$: $2x^2+7x-6=(2x-?)(x+?)$ is not quick. Two working examples are $k=-6$ and $k=3$.

If $k=3$, product $ac=-18$ and sum $7$ uses $9$ and $-2$, so $3x^2+7x-6=(3x-2)(x+3)$.

If $k=-6$, product $ac=36$ and sum $7$ uses $3$ and $12?$ not $7$; instead use $k=-2$: product $12$ and sum $7$ uses $3$ and $4$, so $-2x^2+7x-6=-(2x^2-7x+6)=-(2x-3)(x-2)$.
