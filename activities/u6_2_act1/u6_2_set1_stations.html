If your goal is to find the zeros of a quadratic with the least extra work, which form is usually most useful? Explain briefly.

Vertex form, because the vertex coordinates are visible in $a(x-h)^2+k$.

Problem 24

Explain why $y=(x-5)^2$ shifts the graph right rather than left.

Solution:

The inside expression is zero at $x=5$, so the vertex moves from $x=0$ to $x=5$, which is 5 units right.
