Station 1 Answers

Question 1

In $y=-2|x|$, identify the reflection and stretch.

Solution: The negative sign reflects the graph across the $x$-axis, and the factor $2$ is a vertical stretch by $2$.

Question 2

In $y=\frac{1}{3} x^2$, identify whether the graph is wider or narrower than $y=x^2$.

Solution: Since $0\lt\frac{1}{3}\lt1$, the graph is vertically compressed, so it is wider than $y=x^2$.

Question 3

Use the table to identify the vertical scale factor from $f$ to $g$.

$x$-2-1012
$f(x)$41014
$g(x)$82028

Solution: Compare outputs: $8=2(4)$, $2=2(1)$, $0=2(0)$, $2=2(1)$, and $8=2(4)$. The vertical scale factor from $f$ to $g$ is $2$.

Question 4

Two students graph $y=-\frac{1}{2}(x-2)^2+4$. One transforms the vertex first; the other transforms a table of parent points first. Explain both methods and why they should produce the same graph.

Solution: Vertex-first method: start with the parent vertex $(0,0)$, shift it right $2$ and up $4$ to $(2,4)$, reflect over the $x$-axis and compress by $\frac{1}{2}$ around that vertex. Table method: start with parent points such as $(-1,1),(0,0),(1,1)$, move each right $2$, multiply the vertical distance from the vertex by $-\frac{1}{2}$, then move up $4$. Both methods apply the same transformations, so they produce the same graph.

Station 2 Answers

Question 5

State whether $x=4$ graphs as a horizontal line or a vertical line.

Solution: $x=4$ is a vertical line because every point has $x$-coordinate $4$.

Question 6

Describe the graph of $y=-2$, including which axis it is parallel to.

Solution: $y=-2$ is a horizontal line through $-2$ on the $y$-axis. It is parallel to the $x$-axis.

Question 7

A temperature starts at $68$ degrees and drops $2$ degrees each hour. Write a linear model and use it to predict the temperature after $9$ hours.

Solution: The model is $T=68-2h$, where $h$ is hours. After $9$ hours, $T=68-2(9)=50$, so the temperature is $50$ degrees.

Question 8

Write an equation for a line that passes through $(2,1)$ and $(5,10)$. Then check your equation with one of the points.

Solution: Slope $m=\frac{10-1}{5-2}=3$. Use $y=3x+b$ and point $(2,1)$: $1=6+b$, so $b=-5$. The equation is $y=3x-5$. Check $(5,10)$: $3(5)-5=10$.

Question 9

A table has slope $-4$ and includes the point $(3,2)$. Write the equation without making a full table.

Solution: Use point-slope reasoning with slope $-4$ and point $(3,2)$. $y-2=-4(x-3)$, so $y=-4x+14$.

Question 10

You need to predict a value near $x=9$ from a data set collected for $x=1$ through $8$. Construct a strategy for choosing a line of fit and checking whether the prediction is reasonable.

Solution: Choose a line of fit that follows the overall trend of the data from $x=1$ through $8$, not one extreme point. Use two representative points on the trend line to write a model, then evaluate at $x=9$. Check reasonableness by seeing whether $x=9$ is just beyond the data range and whether the predicted value continues the established pattern.

Question 11

A model has equation $y=12x+5$. Analyze the structure of the model: what does the slope assume about the data pattern, and what would make that assumption weak?

Solution: The model $y=12x+5$ assumes the output increases by a constant $12$ for every $1$ unit increase in $x$. That assumption is weak if the data curve, has outliers, or changes rate in different intervals.

Question 12

Two approaches are available for writing an equation from a graph: read the intercept first, or choose two points first. Recommend the better approach for this graph and support your recommendation with a correct equation.

Solution: For this graph, choosing two clear points is better than estimating an intercept. Two visible points are $(-2,9)$ and $(4,-3)$, so $m=\frac{-3-9}{4-(-2)}=-2$. Substitute: $9=-2(-2)+b$, so $b=5$. The equation is $y=-2x+5$.

Question 13

Which equation is already solved for a variable: $4x+y=9$ or $y=-2x+7$?

Solution: $y=-2x+7$ is already solved for a variable.

Question 14

After substituting $y=x+6$ into $2x+y=15$, what one-variable equation should be solved?

Solution: Substitute to get $2x+(x+6)=15$.

Question 15

Solve the system and label the solution type.

$$\begin{aligned}3x+4y=2\\6x+8y=5\end{aligned}$$

Solution: Double the first equation: $6x+8y=4$. The second equation is $6x+8y=5$. Since the same left side cannot equal both $4$ and $5$, the system has no solution.

Question 16

For the taxi plans \$5 plus \$2 per mile and \$11 plus \$1.25 per mile, compare solving by graphing, substitution, and elimination. Which method gives the cleanest paper-only solution, and why?

Solution: Let $m$ be miles and $C$ be cost. The plans are $C=5+2m$ and $C=11+1.25m$. Substitution is the cleanest paper-only method because both are already solved for $C$: set $5+2m=11+1.25m$, so $0.75m=6$ and $m=8$. Graphing may be less exact, and elimination would require rewriting both equations.
