Station 1 Answers

Question 1

In $y=-x^2+4$, identify the reflection and vertical translation.

Solution: The negative sign reflects $y=x^2$ across the $x$-axis. The $+4$ translates the graph up $4$ units.

Question 2

Use the table to identify the reflection and stretch from $f$ to $h$.

$x$-2-1012
$f(x)$21012
$h(x)$-4-20-2-4

Solution: Compare $h$ to $f$: each output is $-2$ times the corresponding $f$ value. That means a reflection across the $x$-axis and a vertical stretch by factor $2$.

Question 3

A student predicts that $y=4(x-1)^2-2$ is wider than $y=x^2$ because it is multiplied by $4$. Make a reasonableness argument to support or reject the prediction.

Solution: Reject the prediction. Multiplying by $4$ makes the $y$-values grow four times as fast from the vertex, so the graph is vertically stretched and appears narrower, not wider, than $y=x^2$.

Question 4

Synthesize an equation, key-point table, and graph description for a function made from $y=|x|$ by reflecting over the $x$-axis, stretching vertically by factor $2$, and translating right $1$ and up $3$. Explain how each representation shows every transformation.

Performance notes: Look for correct parameter reasoning, transformed key points, and a clear explanation of why the chosen representation is useful.

Solution: Start with $y=|x|$. Reflect over the $x$-axis and stretch by $2$ to get $-2|x|$. Translate right $1$ and up $3$: $y=-2|x-1|+3$. Key points: parent $(0,0)$ becomes $(1,3)$, $(1,1)$ becomes $(2,1)$, and $(-1,1)$ becomes $(0,1)$. The graph is an upside-down V with vertex $(1,3)$ and arms with slopes $2$ and $-2$.

Question 5

Identify $m$ and $b$ in $y=-4x+9$.

Solution: In $y=-4x+9$, $m=-4$ and $b=9$.

Question 6

Write the slope-intercept form template and name what each letter represents.

Solution: The template is $y=mx+b$. Here $m$ represents slope, and $b$ represents the $y$-intercept or starting value.

Question 8

Plan A costs \$20 plus \$5 per use. Plan B is shown by $y=8x+5$. Compare the starting values and slopes, then decide which plan is cheaper for $2$ uses.

Solution: Plan A has starting value $20$ and slope $5$. Plan B has starting value $5$ and slope $8$. For $2$ uses, Plan A costs $20+5(2)=30$, and Plan B costs $8(2)+5=21$, so Plan B is cheaper.

Compare the table to $y=2x+11$. Which has the greater slope? Which has the greater starting value?

Solution: The table slope is $\frac{15-9}{2-0}=3$, and the starting value is $9$. The equation $y=2x+11$ has slope $2$ and starting value $11$. The table has the greater slope; the equation has the greater starting value.

Question 10

Use the graph and equation $y=-x+7$ to compare slope and intercept. Explain whether they could represent the same relationship.

Solution: The graph shown has equation $y=-1.25x+6$, so its slope is about $-1.25$ and intercept is $6$. The equation $y=-x+7$ has slope $-1$ and intercept $7$. Since both the slope and intercept differ, they do not represent the same relationship.

Question 11

Compare the scatter plot with the table. Decide whether one linear model could reasonably represent both and justify with trend and rate evidence.

$x$2468
$y$16273950

Solution: The table increases by about $11$ or $12$ for every $2$ in $x$, so its rate is about $5.5$ to $6$ per input. If the scatter plot has a similar positive trend and rate, one model could reasonably represent both; if its trend is much steeper/flatter or has a different intercept, one model would not fit both well. A strong justification must compare both trend and rate evidence.

Question 12

Two school supply companies offer printing plans: Company A charges \$40 plus \$0.06 per page; Company B charges \$15 plus \$0.11 per page. Recommend a company for a $500$-page job and a $1000$-page job. Justify with equations or a table.

Solution: Company A: $A=40+0.06p$. Company B: $B=15+0.11p$. For $500$ pages, $A=40+30=70$ and $B=15+55=70$, so they cost the same. For $1000$ pages, $A=40+60=100$ and $B=15+110=125$, so Company A is cheaper.

Question 13

Does $(2,7)$ satisfy the equation $y=3x+1$?

Solution: Check $y=3x+1$: $3(2)+1=7$, so $(2,7)$ satisfies the equation.

Question 14

Solve by substitution.

$$\begin{aligned}y=2x+1\x+y=13\end{aligned}$$

Solution: Substitute $y=2x+1$ into $x+y=13$: $x+(2x+1)=13$, so $3x+1=13$, $3x=12$, and $x=4$. Then $y=2(4)+1=9$. The solution is $(4,9)$.

Question 15

A student models a coin problem with $n+q=44$ and $5n+25q=6.80$. The student says the system is ready to solve because the money equation uses cents and dollars together.

Assess the reasonableness of the model, revise it if needed, and explain how the units affect the solution.

Solution: The model mixes cents and dollars: nickels and quarters are in cents, but $6.80$ is dollars. Revise the money equation to $5n+25q=680$ or $0.05n+0.25q=6.80$. Consistent units are required so the solution gives the correct numbers of coins.

Question 16

A club is choosing between two bus companies. Company A charges \$180 plus \$6 per student. Company B charges \$90 plus \$9 per student. A draft graph labels the intersection as $(30,270)$ but the written conclusion says “Company A is always cheaper.”

  1. Revise the written conclusion so it matches the model.
  2. Explain what each coordinate of the intersection means.
  3. State which company is cheaper for 20 students and for 40 students.
Performance notes: Look for correction of the flawed conclusion using both the intersection and values on each side.

Solution: The intersection $(30,270)$ means both companies cost \$270 for $30$ students. Company A is not always cheaper. For $20$ students, A costs $180+6(20)=300$ and B costs $90+9(20)=270$, so B is cheaper. For $40$ students, A costs $420$ and B costs $450$, so A is cheaper.
