Problem 1

Construct the graph of $y \le -3x-4$ with the correct boundary style and shaded half-plane.

Answer:

Use a solid boundary $y=-3x-4$ and shade below the line.

Problem 2

Write an inequality represented by the shaded graph. Include the correct inequality symbol.

Answer:

One correct inequality is $y \le x+1$.

Problem 3

A student graphs $y \lt 2x+4$ with a solid boundary and shades below. Critique the graph and state the needed correction.

Answer:

The shading is on the correct side, but the boundary must be dashed because the inequality is strict.

Problem 4

Write an inequality represented by the shaded graph. Include the correct inequality symbol.

Answer:

One correct inequality is $y \ge x-2$.
