Solution 16
Last · DOK 3

A student claims Plan B is always cheaper because it starts lower: Plan A is $A=20+5x$ and Plan B is $B=5+8x$. Critique the claim and include a value of $x$ that supports your reasoning.

Solution:

Plan B starts lower, but it has a larger slope. Set $20+5x=5+8x$ to get $15=3x$, so $x=5$. Before $5$ uses, B is cheaper; after $5$ uses, A is cheaper. For example at $x=10$, A costs $70$ and B costs $85$.
