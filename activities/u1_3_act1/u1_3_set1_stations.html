Station 1 Solutions • Set 1
Answer Key
1. For consecutive integer inputs, the outputs are 11, 16, 21, 26. Identify the most likely function family and cite the numerical evidence.

Solution: Linear; the first differences are constantly +5.

2. A table has first differences 4, 8, 12, 16 for equal x-steps. Which family is suggested, and what secondary pattern supports the claim?

Solution: Quadratic; the second differences are constantly 4.

3. For each +1 in x, the output is multiplied by 4. Which family is most likely? Explain why a constant additive-rate model does not fit.

Solution: Exponential; repeated multiplication by 4 is not constant additive change.

4. A relationship decreases to one minimum value and then increases symmetrically with a sharp turn. Which family is suggested and what feature is decisive?

Solution: Absolute value; the sharp V-shaped turn/symmetry is characteristic.

Answer Key
5. Classify \(y=-6x+9\) by family and cite equation structure as evidence.

Solution: Linear; x appears to the first power with a constant coefficient.

6. Classify \(y=5(3^x)\) by family and explain what the variable position tells you.

Solution: Exponential; the variable appears in the exponent.

7. Classify \(y=(x+2)^2-7\) and name one table or shape feature you would expect as supporting evidence.

Solution: Quadratic; expect a U-shaped parabola or constant second differences for equal x-steps.

8. For \(y=|x-4|+2\), identify the family and state where the turning point occurs in x.

Solution: Absolute value; the vertex/turn occurs at x=4.

Station 4 Solutions • Set 1
Answer Key
13. Two linear relationships both have rate -3, but their outputs at x=0 are 5 and 12. Explain how their graphs/outputs remain related.

Solution: They are parallel/same rate and remain 7 units apart for every input.

14. One relationship adds 6 each step; another multiplies by 2 each step. Explain why “both increase” is not enough to call them the same family.

Solution: Adding a constant amount is linear; multiplying by a constant factor is exponential.

15. A has starting value 4 and rate -2. B has starting value -5 and rate 3. Describe two differences in how the relationships behave.

Solution: A starts higher but decreases; B starts lower but increases.

16. When one relationship is given by a table and another by an equation, name two fair quantities you can compare.

Solution: Examples: rate, starting value/y-intercept, or outputs at the same inputs.

Answer Key
21. Identify the slope of \(y=5-4x\) and explain how you located it despite the term order.

Solution: Slope is -4; rewrite as y=-4x+5 or identify coefficient of x.

22. Rewrite \(6x+3y=21\) only enough to identify the slope.

Solution: 3y=-6x+21, y=-2x+7, so slope -2.

23. A reservoir loses 18 liters every 6 minutes at a constant rate. State the rate per minute with sign and units.

Solution: -3 liters per minute.

24. A service model is \(C=4.25m+9\). Identify the rate and initial value, including their meanings.

Solution: Rate is $4.25 per unit m; initial value is $9 at m=0.
