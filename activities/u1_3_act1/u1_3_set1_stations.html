Classroom Copy 1
Station 6 Answers • Set 1

Problem 21

x y 0 1 1 4 2 9 3 16 A student says the relationship is exponential because the outputs grow quickly. Critique the claim.
Answer:

It is quadratic: second differences are constant; ratios are not.

Problem 22

x y 0 5 1 10 2 20 3 40 A student says the relationship is linear because each row increases. Critique the claim.
Answer:

It is exponential: outputs multiply by 2; first differences are not constant.

Problem 23

A graph is V-shaped. A student immediately calls it quadratic. What additional evidence should be checked, and which family is more likely?
Answer:

Check whether the graph has two straight arms or curved parabolic sides and inspect equation/table structure. Straight arms indicate absolute value.

Problem 24

An equation is $y=7(1.2)^x$. A student calls it quadratic because the graph curves upward. Critique the classification.
Answer:

It is exponential because the variable is in the exponent and a constant factor is repeatedly multiplied.
