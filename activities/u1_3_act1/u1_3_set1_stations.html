Problem 1

Classify $y=-2x+5$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Linear; x is first degree and is not inside an exponent or absolute value.

Problem 2

Classify $y=(x-1)^2+3$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Quadratic; the variable is squared.

Problem 3

Classify $y=4(2^x)$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Exponential; the variable x is in the exponent.

Problem 4

Classify $y=|x+3|-2$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Absolute value; the variable appears inside absolute-value bars.

Answer:

Problem 10

The outputs follow a quadratic pattern: 4, 9, 16, 25. Predict the next two outputs and describe the square-number structure you used.

Answer:

36, 49; these are consecutive square numbers $2^2,3^2,4^2,5^2,6^2,7^2$.

Problem 11

The outputs follow an exponential pattern: 2, 6, 18, 54. Generate the next two outputs and name the repeated multiplicative factor.

Answer:

162, 486; multiply by 3 each step.

Problem 12

The outputs follow an absolute value pattern: 4, 3, 2, 1, 2. Continue the sequence through two more outputs and describe its movement away from the turning point.

Answer:

3, 4; after reaching 1, the outputs increase by 1 as they move away from the turning point.

Problem 17

Quadratic; area is proportional to the square of the radius.

Answer:

Absolute value; distance from a target uses the magnitude of a signed difference.
