Mathematical Hot Seat
Function Families
Source: practice_set_1_3_2(1).html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Teams of 3–4
Materials: Whiteboards + markers
StructureTeam vocabulary and reasoning challenge with rotating speaker.
SetupTeams of 3–4; one student at a time is the speaker while teammates coach with approved prompts.
Directions
  1. Teacher displays the problem page.
  2. The Hot Seat student explains the first idea or classification.
  3. Teammates may ask questions or give hints without taking over.
  4. The team writes a final answer after the Hot Seat explanation.
  5. Teacher advances to the solution page and the team rotates the Hot Seat role.
Goal: Students practice precise mathematical language while teammates support reasoning.
✓ Looks Like Success
  • The Hot Seat student uses precise words such as family, rate, input, or feature.
  • Teammates coach with questions instead of taking over.
  • The speaking role rotates after each solution page.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • The same student explains every problem.
  • Teammates give the answer without asking questions.
  • Students use vague labels without connecting to evidence.

Mastery · DOK 3

Solve $4-2x\lt 10$ using two methods: first by inverse operations and then by testing values around the boundary. Explain how the methods support the same solution set.

Solution: Inverse operations: $4-2x\lt10$ gives $-2x\lt6$, so $x\gt-3$ after dividing by $-2$ and reversing the sign. Testing $x=-4$ gives false and $x=0$ gives true, supporting $x\gt-3$.
