Station 1 Answers • Set 2

Classify $y=5x+1$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Linear; x is first degree and is not inside an exponent or absolute value.

Classify $y=2x^2-3$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Quadratic; the highest power of x is 2.

Classify $y=3(0.5^x)$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Exponential; the variable x is in the exponent.

Classify $y=2|x-1|+4$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value and identify the equation feature that supports your choice.

Answer:

Absolute value; the variable appears inside absolute-value bars.

The outputs follow a linear pattern: -2, 2, 6, 10. Continue with two more outputs and identify the constant-change evidence.

The outputs follow an absolute value pattern: 6, 4, 2, 0, 2. Continue the sequence through two more outputs and describe its movement away from the turning point.

Answer:

4, 6; after reaching 0, the outputs increase by 2 as they move away from the turning point.

An elevator rises 3 floors each minute at a constant rate. Select the best function family and connect your choice to the constant additive change.

Answer:

Linear; the floor number changes by a constant amount per minute.

A stopping-distance model is proportional to the square of speed. Select the best function family and connect your choice to the squared quantity.

Answer:

Quadratic; the output depends on the square of the input.

A culture grows by a factor of 1.5 each hour. Select the best function family and connect your choice to the repeated growth factor.

Answer:

Exponential; the amount is multiplied by the same factor each hour.

Distance from the coordinate $x=-3$ is modeled from a point's signed x-coordinate. Select the best function family and connect your choice to distance from a fixed location.

Answer:

Absolute value; distance from a fixed coordinate uses magnitude.
