Find Someone Who — Set 2 — Answer Key

Unit 1 · Section 1.3
1. The table has outputs -2, 1, 4, 7 for consecutive integer inputs. Classify the family and give the first-difference evidence.
Solution: Linear; constant first difference +3.
2. Equal x-steps produce first differences 5, 9, 13, 17. Which family is most likely and why?
Solution: Quadratic; second differences are constantly 4.
3. A bacteria count starts at 40 and triples each hour. Identify the family and the multiplicative factor.
Solution: Exponential; factor 3.
4. A distance-from-center rule is \(y=|x+1|-3\). Identify the family and the x-coordinate of the vertex.
Solution: Absolute value; vertex x=-1.
5. For consecutive inputs, outputs are 7, 14, 28, 56. A student calls the pattern linear because it keeps increasing. Correct the classification with stronger evidence.
Solution: Exponential; each output is multiplied by 2, while first differences are not constant.
6. The rule \(y=(x-5)^2+2\) is given. Name the family and one expected graph/table signature.
Solution: Quadratic; U-shaped parabola or constant second differences.
7. Create a four-row table that could represent an absolute-value pattern with its minimum output at x=-2. Explain the symmetry you built.
Solution: Example x=-4,-3,-2,-1 with y=5,4,3,4; outputs mirror around x=-2.
8. Describe a short numerical test that distinguishes a linear table from an exponential table when x increases by equal steps.
Solution: Check constant differences for linear and constant ratios for exponential.

Solution: A starts 12 with rate 3. B starts -1 with rate 5.
10. Plan A starts at $32 and adds $3 monthly. Plan B starts at $8 and adds $6 monthly. Which starts higher and which grows faster?
Solution: A starts higher; B grows faster.
11. Two linear relationships start at 10 and -4 and both have slope -2. Will they ever meet? Explain.
Solution: No. Same slope means the 14-unit difference remains constant.
12. A passes through (-2,5) and (4,23). B has rate 2.5. Which has the larger rate?
Solution: A rate=(23-5)/6=3, so A has larger rate.
13. A table drops 14 in y while x increases 7. Another relationship has slope -3. Compare their rates.
Solution: Table rate -2; the slope -3 relationship decreases faster.
14. Relationship A starts lower but has a larger positive rate than B. Explain what must be known or calculated to decide which output is larger.
Solution: A specific input (or intersection point/rules) is needed; the answer can change with x.
15. A and B have the same y-intercept. A has slope 2 and B has slope -4. Compare their outputs for positive x.
Solution: After x=0, A increases and B decreases, so A is greater for positive x.
16. List two common inputs you could use to compare a context-based linear relationship with an equation-based one, and state what outputs you would compare.
Solution: Example x=0 and x=5; compute/read both outputs at those same inputs.

17. A line contains points (-5,2) and (1,11). A student uses the numerator in one order and denominator in the opposite order. Explain why that changes the sign and give the correct slope.
Solution: Use consistent order: (11-2)/(1-(-5))=9/6=3/2. Reversing only one difference wrongly changes the sign.
18. Find the slope through (2,9) and (8,0), then describe the change in y for every +2 in x.
Solution: m=-9/6=-3/2. For +2 in x, y changes -3.

Solution: From -2 to 3: -10/5=-2. From 3 to 7: -8/4=-2.
21. Identify the slope of \(5x+2y=14\).
Solution: 2y=-5x+14, so y=(-5/2)x+7 and slope=-5/2.
22. A student says the slope in \(y=8-6x\) is 8. Diagnose the confusion and identify slope and y-intercept.
Solution: Slope is -6; y-intercept is 8. The standalone constant is the intercept.
23. A freezer temperature drops 15 degrees over 5 hours at a constant rate. State the signed rate with units.
Solution: -3 degrees per hour.
24. A rental model is \(P=1.75d+12\). A student says the rate is 12 dollars per day. Correct the statement and interpret both numbers.
Solution: Rate is $1.75 per day; $12 is the initial amount when d=0.
