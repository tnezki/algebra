Problem 4

A student simplifies $(5x^2 + 4x - 6)-(3x^2 - 4x + 3)$ as $2x^2 - 3$. Identify the subtraction error and give the correct result.

Answer:

The negative sign must distribute to every term in the second polynomial. The correct result is $2x^2 + 8x - 9$.
