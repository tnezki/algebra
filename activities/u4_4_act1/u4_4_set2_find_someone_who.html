Problem 3

A student simplifies $(4x^2-x+3)-(x^2+2x-5)$ as $3x^2+x-2$. Identify the subtraction error and give the correct result.

Answer:

The negative sign must distribute to every term of the second polynomial. The correct result is $3x^2-3x+8$.

Use the partial area model to explain why $(x+2)(x-6)$ and $x^2-4x-12$ are equivalent forms.
