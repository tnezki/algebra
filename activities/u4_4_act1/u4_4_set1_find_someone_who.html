x$(x-6)(x+2)$$x^2 - 4x - 12$

0-12-12

Answer:

The values agree at every listed input, supporting that the expressions are equivalent. Multiplying the binomials confirms $(x-6)(x+2)=x^2 - 4x - 12$.

Problem 4

A student simplifies $(5x^2 + 4x - 6)-(3x^2 - 4x + 3)$ as $2x^2 - 3$. Identify the subtraction error and give the correct result.

Answer:

The negative sign must distribute to every term in the second polynomial. The correct result is $2x^2 + 8x - 9$.

Problem 11

A student combines $3x^2+7x+2x^2$ as $12x^2$. Explain the error and simplify correctly.

Answer:

Only like terms combine: $3x^2+2x^2=5x^2$, while $7x$ stays separate. The result is $5x^2+7x$.
