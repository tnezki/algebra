Recognizing Growth and Decay
Source: practice_set_7_1_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: One whiteboard per student
Structure Indy → Group comparison → Consensus
Setup Groups of 4. Each person has a whiteboard. Rotate the Captain each round.
Directions
  1. Each student solves the problem individually
  2. When prompted, students Showdown (reveal answers at the same time)
  3. Discuss differences and justify reasoning
  4. Group agrees on a correct final answer
  5. Repeat
Goal: Accountability + justification
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Every student writes an answer before the reveal.
  • The captain prompts a simultaneous board reveal for each round.
  • Groups discuss different answers and agree on one justified final response.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • One student announces early and others copy before the reveal.
  • Boards are revealed at different times, removing individual accountability.
  • The group accepts the loudest answer without checking the reasoning.

Solution 11
Mastery · DOK 3

You need to factor $6x^2-x-2$. Construct an efficient strategy before starting: identify $ac$, the needed sum, how you will split the middle term, and how you will check the final product.

Solution: For $6x^2-x-2$, $ac=6(-2)=-12$. We need two numbers with product $-12$ and sum $-1$: $3$ and $-4$. Split the middle term: $6x^2+3x-4x-2$, group to get $3x(2x+1)-2(2x+1)$, so $(3x-2)(2x+1)$. Check by multiplying back.
