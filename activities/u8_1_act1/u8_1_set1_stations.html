For $x^2+6x-7=0$, explain why completing the square is a reasonable method, then solve.

Answer:

Completing the square is direct because the leading coefficient is 1: $(x+3)^2=16$, so $x=1$ or $x=-7$.

For this equation, explain why the quadratic formula is a reliable choice and give the exact solutions: $2x^2+x-5=0$ with the quadratic formula.

Answer:

$x=\frac{-1\pm\sqrt{41}}{4}$.
