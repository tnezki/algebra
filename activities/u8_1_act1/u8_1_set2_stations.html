Station 1 Answers

Question 1

A context says a population is multiplied by $1.07$ each year. Name the likely model family and interpret $1.07$.

Solution:

This is exponential growth. The multiplier $1.07$ means the population is 107% of the previous year, or increases by 7% each year.

Question 2

A ball is thrown upward from $4$ feet with initial velocity $32$ feet per second. Explain why a quadratic model is more reasonable than a linear model for height over time.

Solution:

A quadratic model is more reasonable because gravity causes height to change with a constant second difference and the path has a maximum. A linear model would imply a constant velocity forever, which does not match an object rising then falling.

Question 3

Two students model the same data. Student A uses $y=7x+10$. Student B uses $y=10(1.7)^x$. Use two different pieces of evidence from the table to decide which model is more reasonable for the given domain.

$x$$0$$1$$2$$3$
$y$$10$$17$$29$$49$
Solution:

The table starts at 10. Student A gives outputs 10,17,24,31, which misses 29 and 49.

Student B gives $10,17,28.9,49.13$, matching the table closely, so the exponential model is more reasonable on the given domain.

Question 4

A garden club records plant height for five weeks: $12,18,27,41,62$ cm. Design a short modeling response that chooses a function family, states an assumption, and predicts week $6$.

Solution:

The ratios are about $18/12=1.5$, $27/18=1.5$, $41/27\approx1.52$, and $62/41\approx1.51$, so exponential is reasonable. Assuming the same growth rate continues, a week 6 estimate is about $62(1.5)=93$ cm.

Question 5

Factor $2x^2-5x-12$.

Solution:

$2x^2-5x-12=(2x+3)(x-4)$ because the products give $2x^2$, $-12$, and the middle terms add to $-5x$.

Question 6

Solve $x(x-9)=0$.

Solution:

Use the zero product property: $x=0$ or $x-9=0$. The solutions are $x=0$ and $x=9$.

Question 7

State whether $x=3$ is a solution of $(x-3)(x+2)=0$ and explain with substitution.

Solution:

Substitute $x=3$: $(3-3)(3+2)=0\cdot5=0$. Yes, $x=3$ is a solution.

Question 8

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Solve $(2x-5)(x+4)=0$.

Solution:

Set each factor equal to zero: $2x-5=0$ gives $x=\frac{5}{2}$, and $x+4=0$ gives $x=-4$.

Question 9

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Factor and solve $x^2+x-20=0$.

Solution:

Factor: $x^2+x-20=(x+5)(x-4)$. Thus $x=-5$ or $x=4$.

Question 10

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Solving Quadratics by Factoring. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Determine whether a negative solution should be rejected for a length model $x(x+5)=84$.

Solution:

Solve $x^2+5x-84=0=(x+12)(x-7)$. The solutions are $x=-12$ and $x=7$; reject $-12$ because a length cannot be negative, so $x=7$.

Question 13

For each situation, label it as growth, decay, or not exponential.

  1. A bacteria sample doubles every hour.
  2. A candle loses 3 centimeters of height every hour.
  3. A car keeps 82% of its value each year.
  4. A club gains exactly 4 members each week.
Solution:

Bacteria doubling is exponential growth; losing exactly 3 cm per hour is not exponential; keeping 82% yearly is exponential decay; gaining exactly 4 members weekly is not exponential.

Question 14

Two students are sorting the same pattern.

For the sequence 40, 20, 10, 5, ..., identify the correct student and justify the choice.

Solution:

The sequence has constant ratio $\frac{1}{2}$, so the correct student is the one who identifies a geometric/exponential pattern. The evidence is $20/40=10/20=5/10=\frac{1}{2}$.

Question 15

Two methods are proposed for deciding whether the outputs 10, 15, 22.5, 33.75, 50.625 are exponential.

  1. Method 1 checks first differences.
  2. Method 2 checks ratios.

Use both methods, then explain which method gives stronger evidence for this pattern and why.

Solution:

First differences are not constant, but ratios are constant: $15/10=22.5/15=33.75/22.5=50.625/33.75=1.5$. The ratio test gives stronger evidence for an exponential pattern.

Question 16

Create two different short situations that both begin with 80 but use different exponential multipliers. One situation must be growth and one must be decay.

  1. Give a four-row table for each situation.
  2. Explain how the multiplier in each table proves growth or decay.
Solution:

One sample growth situation: start with 80 followers and multiply by 1.25 each week, giving $80,100,125,156.25$. One sample decay situation: start with 80 mg and multiply by 0.75 each hour, giving $80,60,45,33.75$.

A multiplier greater than 1 proves growth; between 0 and 1 proves decay.
