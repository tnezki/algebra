Three equations are $x^2-9=0$, $x^2+8x+3=0$, and $3x^2+2x-5=0$. Name an efficient method for each without solving all three.

Answer:

Factoring for $x^2-9=0$; completing the square is natural for $x^2+8x+3=0$; the quadratic formula is reliable for $3x^2+2x-5=0$ (factoring also works if recognized).

For $x^2-7x+10=0$, choose the most efficient method from factoring, completing the square, or the quadratic formula, then solve.

Answer:

Factoring is efficient: $(x-2)(x-5)=0$, so $x=2,5$.
