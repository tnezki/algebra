Unit 8 · Section 1 · Activity 1
Find Someone Who
Selecting Appropriate Models
Source: practice_set_8_1_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Independent start, then rotating partners
Materials: Record sheet or worksheet and pencil
Structure Movement + peer interaction
Setup You will need your record sheet or worksheet and a pencil. Keep it with you as you move around the room.
Directions
  1. Work Alone (5 min): Skim every problem you can, then start solving any problem you can. Leave anything you’re stuck on blank for now.
  2. Find a Partner: Walk around and find someone who is ready to share. Compare your completed answers — discuss any that don’t match.
  3. Work Together (5 min): Choose one problem that either of you left blank. Work through it together. Both partners record the solution on their own sheet.
  4. Find Someone New: Thank your partner and repeat steps 2–3 with a different person each time.
  5. Return to your seat with every problem completed and understood — not just filled in.
Goal: Exposure to multiple explanations
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students mark which problems they solved alone before moving.
  • Partners compare completed answers and discuss any mismatch before recording.
  • Each new pairing focuses on a blank or stuck problem and both partners record the reasoning.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students collect signatures without discussing the math.
  • A student fills in an answer they cannot explain.
  • The same pair stays together instead of finding new explanations.

Solution 12
Mastery · DOK 4

Recommend Between Two Approaches. Create or revise a quadratic task for Solving Quadratics by Factoring using the constraints below.

  • Include at least two representations among equation, table, graph, or context.
  • Make the solution method visible from structure, not from a hint.
  • Interpret the solution in words.

Student A wants to expand $(x-4)(x+9)=0$ first because the final report also needs standard form. Student B wants to use the zero product property first, then expand only after solving. Recommend an order of work, solve, write the standard form, and defend your choice for efficiency and accuracy.

Solution:

Use the zero product property first: $x-4=0$ or $x+9=0$, so $x=4$ or $x=-9$. Then expand for the report: $(x-4)(x+9)=x^2+5x-36$, so the standard form is $x^2+5x-36=0$.

This order is efficient because the factored form already shows the solutions.
