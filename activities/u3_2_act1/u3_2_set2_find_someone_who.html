Problem 1

A student substitutes $2x+3$ for $y$ in $3x-2y=10$ but writes $3x-2(2x)+3=10$. Identify and correct the substitution error.

Answer:

The entire expression must replace $y$: $3x-2(2x+3)=10$.

Problem 2

Check whether $(-2,5)$ satisfies both $y=x+7$ and $x+y=3$.

Answer:

Yes. Substitution satisfies both original equations.

Problem 3

Solve the system by substitution. $\begin{cases}y=2x+2\\x+y=5\end{cases}$

Answer:

Substitute the expression for $y$ into the second equation. The solution is $(1,4)$.

Problem 4

Solve the system by substitution. $\begin{cases}y=x-1\\x+y=5\end{cases}$

Answer:

Substitute the expression for $y$ into the second equation. The solution is $(3,2)$.

Problem 5

For the system $y=2x+1$ and $2x+y=5$, identify the most efficient substitution step before solving.

Answer:

Substitute $2x+1$ for $y$ in the second equation because $y$ is already isolated.

Problem 6

Which method is more efficient for $2x+y=11$ and $y=2x+4$: substitution or elimination? Explain the structural cue.

Answer:

Substitution is more efficient because one equation is already solved for $y$.

Problem 7

A student substitutes $2x-2$ for $y$ in $4x+3y=-36$ but writes $4x+3(2x)-2=-36$. Identify and correct the substitution error.

Answer:

The entire expression must replace $y$: $4x+3(2x-2)=-36$.

Problem 8

Check whether $(0,3)$ satisfies both $y=-x+3$ and $x+y=3$.

Answer:

Yes. Substitution satisfies both original equations.
