Unit 8.3 · Activity 1 · Whiteboard Partners Solution 4
Solution 4
Current · DOK 3

Two students solve $|3x-6|=12$. One splits into $3x-6=12$ or $3x-6=-12$; the other divides by $3$ first and solves $|x-2|=4$. Compare the methods and explain why they should give the same solutions.

Solution:

Both methods are equivalent because $|3x-6|=|3(x-2)|=3|x-2|$. Splitting first gives $x=6$ or $x=-2$; dividing first gives $|x-2|=4$, also $x=6$ or $x=-2$.

Solution:

Solution 9

Solve or interpret the quadratic situation for Quadratic Formula. Show the algebra that supports your answer.

Apply the quadratic formula to $-16t^2+32t+5=0$ and interpret the positive time.

Solution:

Using the formula gives $t=\frac{-32\pm\sqrt{32^2-4(-16)(5)}}{2(-16)}=\frac{-32\pm\sqrt{1344}}{-32}$. The positive solution is about $2.15$ seconds, which is the meaningful time.

Create With Constraints. Create or revise a quadratic task for Completing the Square using the constraints below.

  • Include at least two representations among equation, table, graph, or context.
  • Make the solution method visible from structure, not from a hint.
  • Interpret the solution in words.

Create a quadratic expression whose completed-square form is $(x-4)^2-9$. Show the standard form, the vertex, and the real zeros, and explain how the constraints are satisfied.

Solution:

Expand: $(x-4)^2-9=x^2-8x+16-9=x^2-8x+7$. The vertex is $(4,-9)$.

Zeros solve $(x-4)^2=9$, so $x=1$ or $x=7$.
