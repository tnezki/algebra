Problem 1

Graph $y=\frac{3}{2}x+2$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,2)$ and has slope $\frac{3}{2}$. One additional point is $(2,5)$.

Problem 2

Graph $y=2x-5$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,-5)$ and has slope $2$. One additional point is $(2,-1)$.

Problem 3

Graph $y=-\frac{3}{2}x-2$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,-2)$ and has slope $-\frac{3}{2}$. One additional point is $(2,-5)$.

Problem 4

Use the graph to identify the $y$-intercept and describe whether the function is increasing or decreasing.

Answer:

The $y$-intercept is $(0,-4)$. The function is decreasing because the slope is $-3$.

Problem 10

A parking garage charges 6 dollars to enter plus 3 dollars per hour. Sketch a graph with hours on the horizontal axis and total cost on the vertical axis. Identify what the intercept means.

Answer:

The graph is a line with slope $3$ and vertical intercept $6$. The intercept represents the entry fee.

Answer:

Problem 17

The equation is $y=-\frac{1}{2}x+6$. A student moves right 1 and down 2 from the intercept. Identify the error and correct it.

Answer:

The slope $-1/2$ means right 2 and down 1, or an equivalent ratio.

Problem 18

On a graph, each horizontal grid step is 4 units and each vertical grid step is 2 units. A student moves right 1 square and up 1 square and calls the slope 1. Correct the slope.

Answer:

The slope is $2/4=1/2$ because the graph scales are different.
