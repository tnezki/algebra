Problem 1

Graph $y=3x$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,0)$ and has slope $3$. One additional point is $(1,3)$.

Problem 2

Graph $y=x-4$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,-4)$ and has slope $1$. One additional point is $(2,-2)$.

Problem 3

Graph $y=-3x-1$. Mark the $y$-intercept and at least one additional point that shows the slope.

Answer:

The graph crosses the $y$-axis at $(0,-1)$ and has slope $-3$. One additional point is $(1,-4)$.

Problem 4

Use the graph to identify the $y$-intercept and describe whether the function is increasing or decreasing.

Answer:

The $y$-intercept is $(0,-4)$. The function is decreasing because the slope is $-\frac{4}{3}$.

Problem 16

On a graph, each horizontal grid step represents 1 units and each vertical grid step represents 7 units. A student moves right 2 grid step(s) and up 1 grid step(s) and calls the slope $1/2$. Identify the scale error and give the numerical slope.

Answer:

Grid steps must be converted to units. The slope is $\dfrac{7}{2}=\frac{7}{2}$.
