Stations
Graphing Linear Functions
Source: practice_set_2_2_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: 4 unique stations, printed twice
Materials: Printed station cards + station answer pages
Structure Timed rotations
Setup4 unique stations, printed twice. Each station page has four questions.
Directions
  1. Start at one available station copy.
  2. Work through the four questions on that station page.
  3. Use the station answers page when your group is ready to check.
  4. Rotate to a different station when the teacher signals.
Goal: Repetition + varied problem exposure
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students work on one four-question station page before rotating.
  • Duplicate station copies keep group sizes manageable.
  • Groups use answer pages to check before moving on.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • A group skips questions on the station page.
  • Students crowd one copy when the duplicate is open.
  • A group checks answers before attempting the station.
