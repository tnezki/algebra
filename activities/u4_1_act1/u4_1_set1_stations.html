Question 1

An equation has the form $y=m x+b$ with $m\ne 0$. Name the family and one graph feature to expect.

Solution: The family is linear. In $y=mx+b$, $m$ is a nonzero constant slope, so the graph is a straight line with constant rate of change.

Question 2

An equation has the form $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ with $a\ne 0$. Name the family and the key point shown by the form.

Solution: The family is quadratic. The form $y=a(x-h)^2+k$ shows the vertex at $(h,k)$.

Question 9

A water tank has $48$ gallons and drains at $6$ gallons per minute. Write the slope with units and explain what the negative rate means in the situation.

Solution: The slope is $-6$ gallons per minute. The negative sign means the amount of water in the tank decreases by $6$ gallons each minute.

Question 10

A student claims a walking rate of $42$ miles per hour is reasonable because the slope calculation $84\div 2=42$ is correct. Decide whether the slope is reasonable for the context and support your answer with units and a more realistic comparison.

Solution: The calculation $84\div 2=42$ is arithmetically correct, but $42$ miles per hour is not a reasonable walking rate. A typical walking rate is closer to $3$ miles per hour, so the units show the context is unreasonable even though the division was done correctly.

Question 11

A table has $x$-values $2,5,8,11$ and only one known output: when $x=5$, $y=14$. The slope is $-2$. Construct a strategy to fill the rest of the table without writing an equation first. Then use your strategy for all four rows.

Solution: A slope of $-2$ means for every increase of $1$ in $x$, $y$ decreases by $2$. The $x$-values change by $3$, so each step changes $y$ by $-6$. Starting from $(5,14)$: at $x=2$, move back $3$ in $x$, so add $6$ to get $20$; at $x=8$, subtract $6$ to get $8$; at $x=11$, subtract another $6$ to get $2$.

Question 12

A relationship has slope $\frac{3}{5}$ and contains the point $(10,11)$. Build three matching representations: a verbal rate statement, a table with at least four rows, and a slope-intercept equation.

Solution: A slope of $\frac{3}{5}$ means the output increases $3$ for every $5$ input units. Using $(10,11)$, $11=\frac{3}{5}(10)+b=6+b$, so $b=5$. The equation is $y=\frac{3}{5}x+5$.

Station 4 Answers

Question 13

The equations $y=4x-3$ and $y=4x+2$ have the same slope and different $y$-intercepts. Classify the solution type.

Solution: Same slope and different $y$-intercepts means the lines are parallel. The system has no solution.

Question 14

The equations $y=-2x+5$ and $2y+4x=10$ represent the same line. Classify the solution type.

Solution: Rewrite $2y+4x=10$ as $2y=-4x+10$, so $y=-2x+5$. It is the same line as the first equation, so the system has infinitely many solutions.

Question 15

Set the two expressions equal to predict the intersection, then graph the lines on the same axes to check your ordered pair.

$$\begin{aligned}y=-4x+3\y=x-7\end{aligned}$$

Solution: Set the expressions equal: $-4x+3=x-7$. Then $10=5x$, so $x=2$. Substitute into either equation: $y=2-7=-5$. The intersection is $(2,-5)$.

Question 16

Analyze the system without graphing it first.

$$\begin{aligned}4x-6y=12\\-2x+3y=-6\end{aligned}$$

Use equation structure to decide the solution type, then explain what the graph would show.

Solution: Multiply the second equation by $-2$ to get $4x-6y=12$, which matches the first equation. They represent the same line, so the system has infinitely many solutions. The graph would show one line lying exactly on top of the other.
