Problem 1

A table has a constant ratio of 2, but the first few plotted points look almost collinear in a small graph window. Classify the family using the stronger evidence.

Answer:

A constant ratio supports an exponential relationship; the small graph window is less decisive than the exact multiplicative table pattern.

Exponential; the equation structure is characteristic of an exponential function.
