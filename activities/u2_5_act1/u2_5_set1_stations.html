Question 9

A model for water use is $G=1.8d+12$, where $d$ is days and $G$ is gallons. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.
Answer:

The slope 1.8 is predicted gallons added per day; 12 is the predicted starting value.

Question 10

A scatter plot curves upward more and more as $x$ increases. A student says a straight line fits perfectly. Assess the reasoning.
Answer:

The claim is unreasonable because the changing steepness shows a nonconstant rate; a single line will miss the systematic curve.

Question 11

Data were collected for ages 10 through 16. A student uses the linear model to predict a value at age 90. Assess the reasoning.
Answer:

The prediction is a very distant extrapolation and may be unreliable because it is far outside the observed range.

Question 12

Observed values are 20, 25, 30, 35 while a model predicts 19, 26, 29, 36. A student rejects the model because no prediction is exact. Assess the reasoning.
Answer:

That is not a sound reason. A model can be useful when small errors are acceptable for the question; exact matches are not required.
