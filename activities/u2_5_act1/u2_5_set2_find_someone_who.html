Problem 3

Describe the trend in the scatter plot and decide whether a linear model is reasonable for the displayed data. Support your decision with the pattern of points.

Answer:

The pattern is curved, not well modeled by a line over the full range. A single linear model is not a good fit for the full range because the rate of change is visibly changing.

Answer:

The model predicts $y=29$ when $x=9$. Actual observed data may differ from the model prediction.

Problem 7

A model for a phone battery is $B=-4t+96$, where $t$ is hours and $B$ is percent charge. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

Answer:

The slope -4 means the model predicts a 4-percentage-point decrease per hour; 96 is the predicted charge at hour 0.

Problem 8

A model for plant height is $H=1.6w+7$, where $w$ is weeks and $H$ is centimeters. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

Answer:

The slope 1.6 means predicted growth of 1.6 cm/week; 7 is the predicted height at week 0.

Problem 9

A model for water in a tank is $V=-6t+120$, where $t$ is minutes and $V$ is liters. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

Answer:

The slope -6 means predicted loss of 6 liters/minute; 120 is the predicted starting volume.

Problem 10

A scatter plot bends downward as $x$ increases. A student claims one straight line fits the entire pattern perfectly. Assess the reasoning.

Answer:

The claim is unreasonable because the systematic curve indicates a changing rate; one line will miss the pattern.

Problem 11

Data were collected for months 1 through 8. A student uses the model to predict month 60. Assess the reasoning.

Answer:

The prediction is a distant extrapolation and may be unreliable far outside the observed range.

Problem 12

Observed points lie close to a straight line with small scatter. A student rejects a linear model because two points are not exactly on the line. Assess the reasoning.

Answer:

The rejection is too strict. A useful model need not pass through every observed point when deviations are small.

Problem 13

Measurements were collected for $x=5$ through $x=20$. Predicting at $x=14$: classify the prediction as interpolation or extrapolation and explain which is generally safer when the trend is only approximately linear.

Answer:

This is interpolation because 14 is inside the observed range. Interpolation is generally safer because it stays within the range where the pattern was observed.

Problem 14

Measurements were collected for $x=3$ through $x=15$. Predicting at $x=22$: classify the prediction as interpolation or extrapolation and explain which is generally safer when the trend is only approximately linear.

Answer:

This is extrapolation because 22 is outside the observed range. Interpolation is generally safer.

Problem 15

Temperatures were recorded from 9 A.M. through 3 P.M. Predicting noon: classify the prediction as interpolation or extrapolation.

Answer:

This is interpolation because noon lies inside the observed time range.
