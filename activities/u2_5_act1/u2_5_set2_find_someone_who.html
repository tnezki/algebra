A model for a phone battery is $B=-4t+96$, where $t$ is hours and $B$ is percent charge. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

The slope -4 means the model predicts a 4-percentage-point decrease per hour; 96 is the predicted charge at hour 0.

8

A model for plant height is $H=1.6w+7$, where $w$ is weeks and $H$ is centimeters. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

The slope 1.6 means predicted growth of 1.6 cm/week; 7 is the predicted height at week 0.

9

A model for water in a tank is $V=-6t+120$, where $t$ is minutes and $V$ is liters. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

The slope -6 means predicted loss of 6 liters/minute; 120 is the predicted starting volume.

10

A scatter plot bends downward as $x$ increases. A student claims one straight line fits the entire pattern perfectly. Assess the reasoning.

The claim is unreasonable because the systematic curve indicates a changing rate; one line will miss the pattern.

11

Data were collected for months 1 through 8. A student uses the model to predict month 60. Assess the reasoning.

The prediction is a distant extrapolation and may be unreliable far outside the observed range.

12

Observed points lie close to a straight line with small scatter. A student rejects a linear model because two points are not exactly on the line. Assess the reasoning.

The rejection is too strict. A useful model need not pass through every observed point when deviations are small.
