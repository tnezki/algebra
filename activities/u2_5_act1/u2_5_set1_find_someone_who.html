Problem 7

A model for a subscription total is $C=-12m+293$, where $m$ is months and $C$ is dollars. Interpret both the slope and intercept in context.

Answer:

The slope $-12$ represents the change in cost per month; the intercept $293$ represents the starting cost.

Problem 8

Answer:

Problem 10

A data set is shown below. A student says a straight line fits the pattern perfectly. Assess the reasoning using the changes in the outputs.

$x$$y$
$0$$7$
$1$$11$
$2$$18$
$3$$27$
Answer:

The claim is unreasonable because equal input steps produce changing output differences (4, 7, then 9), so the data do not have a constant rate of change and cannot lie perfectly on one line.
