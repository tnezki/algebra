Station 1 Answers

Question 1

A model has slope $-2.5$ pounds per week. State whether the predicted quantity increases or decreases each week.

Solution: It decreases by $2.5$ pounds per week.

Question 2

Use the cost scatter plot to estimate a linear model. Then decide whether predicting at $20$ minutes is interpolation or extrapolation.

Solution: A reasonable model is about $y=7x+1$; predicting at $20$ minutes is extrapolation.

Question 3

Compare the scatter plot with the table. Decide whether one linear model could reasonably represent both and justify with trend and rate evidence.

$x$2468
$y$16273950
Solution: Yes, both are positive and roughly linear; the early scatter points match the table trend closely.

Question 4

A first line of fit for the plant data is $h=2.5w+42$. Revise the model after noticing it underpredicts weeks $6$ through $8$. Propose a better slope or intercept and justify using the scatter plot.

Solution: One better model is about $h=3w+41$, which raises later predictions.

Question 9

Two patterns are described: Pattern A has Figure ${0}=3$ and grows by ${6}$. Pattern B has Figure ${0}=12$ and grows by ${2}$. Build a table through Figure ${4}$ and compare.

Solution: A: $3,9,15,21,27$; B: $12,14,16,18,20$; A becomes greater by Figure $3$.

Question 10

Compare three relationships from the graph. Rank them by rate of change and explain the evidence you used.

Solution: By rate: A has slope $2$, C has slope $0.5$, and B has slope $-1$.

Question 11

Explain why the equation $y={7}$ is linear even though there is no visible $x$-term. Include a table and a graph description in your explanation.

Solution: It is linear with slope $0$; every output is $7$.

Question 12

A school club’s table shows inputs ${0}$, ${1}$, ${2}$, ${3}$ with outputs ${10}$, ${14}$, ${17}$, ${22}$. Revise the flawed model by deciding whether one output is likely an error, replacing it, and writing a rule that fits the corrected table.

Solution: Replace $17$ with $18$; a corrected rule is $y=4x+10$.

Question 13

For exponential growth, is the multiplier usually greater than $1$ or between $0$ and $1$?

Solution: Greater than $1$.

Question 14

Create the next two rows of the table and choose the likely family.

$x$012345
$y$13713
Solution: Next outputs are $21$ and $31$; likely quadratic.

Question 15

Construct a strategy for translating a context into an equation before solving. Apply your strategy to: a phone plan charges \$12 plus \$5 per gigabyte and the total bill is \$47.

Solution: $12+5g=47\Rightarrow g=7$.

Question 16

Design a one-variable equation model for a realistic school or club situation where the solution is $8$. Include the context, define the variable, write the equation, solve it, and interpret why $8$ makes sense.

Solution: Example: $5x+10=50$ gives $x=8$, so $8$ is the meaningful solution.
