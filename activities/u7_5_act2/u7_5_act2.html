Exponential Modeling with Data
Source: practice_set_7_5_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: Copies of puzzle
Structure Matching puzzle (self-checking)
Setup Groups of 4. Copies of puzzle
Directions
  1. Students match expressions, answers, or steps.
  2. Pieces form a complete shape (triangle/square).
  3. If correct, puzzle fits perfectly.
Goal: Error detection + pattern recognition
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Groups justify each match before placing the piece into the puzzle.
  • Students use mismatched edges as feedback and revise the placement.
  • The final shape is checked against both puzzle fit and mathematical meaning.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students match pieces by shape or guessing without reading the math.
  • One student controls all pieces while the group watches.
  • A nearly fitting puzzle is accepted without checking the mismatched answers.
