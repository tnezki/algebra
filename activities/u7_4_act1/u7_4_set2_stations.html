Station 5 Solutions

Choose an efficient method—factoring, completing the square, or quadratic formula—to solve $x^2+6x+2=0$. Solve and briefly justify your method choice.

Solution:

An efficient choice is completing the square. The solution is $x=-3\pm\sqrt7$.

For $\displaystyle x^{2} - 8 x + 11=0$, compare completing the square with the quadratic formula. Which is more efficient here, and why? Then solve.

Solution:

Completing the square is efficient because the quadratic is one square away from vertex form: $(x-4)^2=5$. Thus $x=4+\sqrt{5}$ or $x=4-\sqrt{5}$.

Station 6 Solutions

Inspect $\displaystyle 16 x^{2} - 16=0$. Identify the structural clue that suggests a fast solving method, then solve.

Solution:

This is a difference of squares: $(4x-4)(4x+4)=0$, so $x=\pm\frac{4}{4}$.

For $f(x)=2 x^{2} - 4 x + 3$ in standard form, identify the $y$-intercept directly from the form.

Solution:

The $y$-intercept is $(0,3)$.

Station 6 Solutions

Rewrite $$x^2-4x+3$$ in factored form. Then state what feature becomes easier to read from the new form.

Solution:

$(x-1)(x-3)$. The zeros/x-intercepts are easier to read from factored form.

A problem asks for the maximum or minimum value and where it occurs. Which quadratic form would you prefer to use first, and why?

Solution:

Vertex Form, because the vertex is visible.
