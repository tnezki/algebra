Unit 7 · Section 4 · Activity 1
Think, Trade, Agree
Comparing Function Families
Source: practice_set_7_4_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: One whiteboard per student
Structure Indy → Group comparison → Consensus
Setup Groups of 4. Each person has a whiteboard
Directions
  1. Teacher presents problem
  2. Each student solves the problem individually
  3. When all, students done, exchange boards
  4. Discuss differences and justify reasoning
  5. Come to an agreement or ask teacher to help
  6. Repeat exchange with someone different
Goal: Accountability + justification
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Each student solves first on their own board before any exchange.
  • Students trade boards and discuss what differs in the reasoning.
  • Groups revise until they can state an agreed answer or ask a specific question.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students trade blank boards or begin by copying a neighbor.
  • Differences are erased without explaining why one solution is stronger.
  • The group asks for help before comparing its own reasoning.

Solution 7
Mastery · DOK 2

Write a factored function for a parabola with x-intercepts $(2,0)$ and $(7,0)$ and y-intercept $(0,-8)$.

Solution: With intercepts $2$ and $7$, write $y=a(x-2)(x-7)$. Use y-intercept $(0,-8)$: $-8=a(-2)(-7)=14a$, so $a=-\frac{4}{7}$. Thus $y=-\frac{4}{7}(x-2)(x-7)$.

Solution: Factor the shared binomial: $8n(3n-5)+12(3n-5)=(3n-5)(8n+12)$. Then factor $4$: $(3n-5)4(2n+3)=4(3n-5)(2n+3)$, so the rewrite is reasonable.

Solution: For $6x^2-x-2$, $ac=-12$ and the needed sum is $-1$. Use $3$ and $-4$: $6x^2+3x-4x-2=3x(2x+1)-2(2x+1)=(3x-2)(2x+1)$. Check by multiplying.

Solution: To complete $x^2+bx$, add $(b/2)^2$. For $b=-14$, add $(-7)^2=49$, so $x^2-14x+49=(x-7)^2$.
