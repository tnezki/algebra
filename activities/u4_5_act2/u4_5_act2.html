Polynomial Structure and Quadratic Graphs
Source: practice_set_4_5_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4. Copies of puzzle
Materials: Reusable Tarsia generator + copies of puzzle
StructureMatching puzzle (self-checking)
SetupGroups of 4. Copies of puzzle
Directions
  1. Students match expressions, answers, or steps.
  2. Pieces form a complete shape (triangle/square).
  3. If correct, puzzle fits perfectly.
Goal: Error detection + pattern recognition
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Students justify each match by connecting a question to an answer or feature.
  • Pieces are rearranged when a local match creates a global shape conflict.
  • The group checks the completed puzzle shape as a self-check before asking the teacher.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Students match by guessing shape edges instead of math.
  • One student assembles the puzzle while others watch.
  • The group stops at the first partial fit without checking all matches.

1.Current · DOK 1–2

For $y=-(x+2)^2+7$, state the vertex and opening direction.

2.Current · DOK 1–2

Use factors to find the $x$-intercepts of $y=(x-5)(x+1)$.

3.Last · DOK 1–2

Classify a system of parallel lines.

4.Mastery · DOK 1–2

Find the constant rate in the table: $(0,2),(2,8),(4,14)$.

Key:

  1. Solution: Vertex $(-2,7)$ and opening direction down.

  2. Solution: Set each factor equal to zero: $x-5=0$ gives $x=5$, and $x+1=0$ gives $x=-1$. The $x$-intercepts are $(5,0)$ and $(-1,0)$.

  3. Solution: Parallel lines have no solution.

  4. Solution: Slope $=\frac{8-2}{2-0}=3$ and $\frac{14-8}{4-2}=3$, so the constant rate is $3$.
