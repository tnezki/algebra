Problem 1

Use the graph to describe the feasible region of the system. Give one point that appears feasible.

Answer:

The feasible region is the overlap of both shaded half-planes. For example, $(1,4)$ lies in the overlap.

Problem 2

A group has a limit of 28 total items split between notebooks and pens, and it needs at least 5 pens. Let $x$ be the number of notebooks and $y$ the number of pens. Write a system of inequalities, including nonnegativity.

Answer:

$x+y\le28$, $y\ge5$, $x\ge0$, and $y\ge0$.

Problem 3

The point $(5,10)$ satisfies $y\ge -x+5$ but does not satisfy $y\le x+4$. Can it belong to the feasible region of the system? Explain using the meaning of overlap.

Answer:

No. A feasible point must satisfy every inequality in the system, so a point outside either solution region is outside their overlap.
