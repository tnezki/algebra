Problem 1

A workshop can make at most 18 stools and tables in total and must make at least 4 tables. Let $x$ be stools and $y$ be tables. Write a system of inequalities, including nonnegativity.

Answer:

$x+y\le 18$, $y\ge 4$, $x\ge0$, and $y\ge0$.

Problem 2

How can substitution verify that a point selected from a graph is truly feasible?

Answer:

Substitute the coordinates into every inequality and confirm that every statement is true.

Problem 3

Use the graph to describe the feasible region of the system. Give one point that appears feasible.

Answer:

The feasible region is the overlap of both shaded half-planes. For example, $(3,4)$ lies in the overlap.

Problem 4

Determine whether $(0,2)$ is feasible for $y\le x+4$ and $y\ge -x$. Show both checks.

Answer:

$2\le 4$ is true and $2\ge 0$ is true; the point is feasible.

Problem 8

What is the difference between a solution to one inequality and a feasible solution to a system of inequalities?

Answer:

A solution to one inequality meets one constraint; a feasible system solution meets all constraints simultaneously.

Problem 13

A workshop can make at most 26 basic packages and premium packages in total and must make at least 7 premium packages. Let $x$ be basic packages and $y$ be premium packages. Write a system of inequalities, including nonnegativity.

Answer:

$x+y\le 26$, $y\ge 7$, $x\ge0$, and $y\ge0$.

Problem 14

Why is a point that satisfies only one inequality not a solution of the whole system?

Answer:

A system requires every inequality to be true at the same point; satisfying only one constraint is not enough.
