Problem 19

Which form makes the shared factor easiest to see: $32x^2+48x$ or $16x(2x+3)$? State the shared factor.

The product form; shared factor $16x$.

Problem 21

Factor $48x^3-72x^2+24x$ completely.

$24x(2x^2-3x+1)$

Problem 22

Does $20x(2x+7)$ multiply to $40x^2+140x$? Justify by distribution.

Yes; distributing produces exactly those two terms.

Problem 23

A group has $24x$ red tiles and $36x$ blue tiles. Express the total as a product showing the greatest shared group size.

$12x(2+3)$

Problem 24

Two proposed rewrites are $A=24x(2x+3)$ and $B=12(4x^2+6x)$ for $48x^2+72x$. Which is completely factored by a GCF?

$A$; $B$ still has a common factor $2x$ inside the parentheses.
