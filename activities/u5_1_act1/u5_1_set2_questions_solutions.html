A student says the greatest common factor of $24x^2+36x$ is $6x$. Name a larger common factor and explain in one sentence why it is larger.

Solution:

A larger common factor is $12x$ because $24x^2\div 12x=2x$ and $36x\div 12x=3$.

So $24x^2+36x=12x(2x+3)$, and $12x$ is larger than $6x$.

Factor $12x^2+30x$ in two different first steps: one using a common factor of $6x$ and one using a common factor of $3x$. Compare the results and explain why one final answer is considered completely factored.

Solution:

Using $6x$: $12x^2+30x=6x(2x+5)$.

Using $3x$: $12x^2+30x=3x(4x+10)=3x\cdot2(2x+5)=6x(2x+5)$.

The completely factored form is $6x(2x+5)$ because $2x+5$ has no common factor.

Solution 12
Mastery · DOK 4

Create a system of two linear equations that has solution $(4,3)$ and is efficient to solve by substitution because one equation is already solved for $y$.

  1. Write the system.
  2. Show a substitution solution path.
  3. Explain why your structure makes substitution efficient.
Solution:

Example system: $y=x-1$ and $2x+y=11$.

Substitute $x-1$ for $y$: $2x+(x-1)=11$, so $3x-1=11$, $3x=12$, and $x=4$.

Then $y=4-1=3$. The solution is $(4,3)$, and substitution is efficient because one equation is already solved for $y$.

Solution:

One model is $y=2(x-2)^2-3$.
