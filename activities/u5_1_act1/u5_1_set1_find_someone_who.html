Problem 19

Which form makes the shared factor easiest to see: $24x^2+36x$ or $12x(2x+3)$? State the shared factor.

The product form; shared factor $12x$.

Answer:

Expanded: $16x^2+12x$; common factor $4x$.

Problem 24

Two proposed rewrites are $A=18x(2x+3)$ and $B=9(4x^2+6x)$ for $36x^2+54x$. Which is completely factored by a GCF?

Answer:

$A$; $B$ still has a common factor $2x$ inside the parentheses.
