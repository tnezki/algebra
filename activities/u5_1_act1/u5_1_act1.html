Unit 5 · Section 1 · Activity 1
Mathematical Hot Seat
GCF and Structure
Source: practice_set_5_1_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups
Materials: Whiteboards + markers
StructureVerbal description only → Blind solving → Role rotation
SetupGroups. One whiteboard and one marker per member.
Directions
  1. One student per team sits facing away from the screen (the Hot Seat)
  2. Teacher projects a problem — only teammates can see it
  3. Teammates describe the problem using words only — no gestures, no writing
  4. Hot Seat student solves based solely on the verbal description
  5. After solving, the student turns around and compares to the original problem
  6. Rotate the Hot Seat for each round
Goal: Mathematical vocabulary + precise communication
✓ Looks Like Success
  • The Hot Seat student faces away before teammates begin describing the problem.
  • Teammates use precise vocabulary such as factor, zero, coefficient, intercept, or vertex instead of pointing.
  • The solver compares their written result to the projected problem before the role rotates.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Teammates gesture, point, or write symbols for the Hot Seat student.
  • The solver turns around before finishing their own attempt.
  • The same student stays in the Hot Seat for multiple rounds.

Current · DOK 1

For each pair, decide whether the expressions are equivalent by naming the common factor that connects them.

  1. $8x+12$ and $4(2x+3)$
  2. $5y^2-15y$ and $5y(y-3)$
  3. $3a+12b$ and $6(a+2b)$
Solution:
  1. Equivalent: $4(2x+3)=8x+12$; common factor $4$.
  2. Equivalent: $5y(y-3)=5y^2-15y$; common factor $5y$.
  3. Not equivalent: $6(a+2b)=6a+12b$, not $3a+12b$; $3$ is common to the original expression.

Current · DOK 2

Two students factored $12p^2-30p$.

Student A: $6p(2p-5)$

Student B: $3p(4p-10)$

Which expression is factored completely? Support your answer by identifying any remaining common factor.

Solution:

Student A is completely factored: $6p(2p-5)$.

Student B has a remaining common factor in the parentheses: $4p-10=2(2p-5)$, so $3p(4p-10)=6p(2p-5)$.

Solution:

The model shows $(2x+3)(x-5)$, so the cell products are $2x^2$, $-10x$, $3x$, and $-15$.
