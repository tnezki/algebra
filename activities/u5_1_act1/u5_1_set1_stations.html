Problem 21

Problem 22

Does $15x(2x+7)$ multiply to $30x^2+105x$? Justify by distribution.

Yes; distributing produces exactly those two terms.

Problem 23

A group has $18x$ red tiles and $27x$ blue tiles. Express the total as a product showing the greatest shared group size.

$9x(2+3)$

Problem 24

Two proposed rewrites are $A=18x(2x+3)$ and $B=9(4x^2+6x)$ for $36x^2+54x$. Which is completely factored by a GCF?

$A$; $B$ still has a common factor $2x$ inside the parentheses.
