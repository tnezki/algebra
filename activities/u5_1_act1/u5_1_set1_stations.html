Question 1

For each pair, decide whether the expressions are equivalent by naming the common factor that connects them.

  1. $8x+12$ and $4(2x+3)$
  2. $5y^2-15y$ and $5y(y-3)$
  3. $3a+12b$ and $6(a+2b)$
Solution:
  1. Equivalent: $4(2x+3)=8x+12$; common factor $4$.
  2. Equivalent: $5y(y-3)=5y^2-15y$; common factor $5y$.
  3. Not equivalent: $6(a+2b)=6a+12b$, not $3a+12b$; $3$ is common to the original expression.

Question 2

Complete the coefficient-and-variable-power table for $18x^3-12x^2+6x$.

Term$18x^3$$-12x^2$$6x$
Coefficient_______________
Power of $x$_______________
Solution:

Coefficients: $18$, $-12$, $6$. Powers of $x$: $3$, $2$, $1$.

The GCF would use the coefficient GCF $6$ and the smallest power $x^1$, so it is $6x$.

Question 3

Two students factored $12p^2-30p$.

Student A: $6p(2p-5)$

Student B: $3p(4p-10)$

Which expression is factored completely? Support your answer by identifying any remaining common factor.

Solution:

Student A is completely factored: $6p(2p-5)$.

Student B has a remaining common factor in the parentheses: $4p-10=2(2p-5)$, so $3p(4p-10)=6p(2p-5)$.

Question 4

The expression $2x^2-7x+15$ and the area model are supposed to represent the same product. Find the mismatch, explain how you know, and revise the incorrect cell.

Solution:

The model shows $(2x+3)(x-5)$, so the cell products are $2x^2$, $-10x$, $3x$, and $-15$.

The displayed model cell $15$ should be $-15$. Then the total is $2x^2-7x-15$, not $2x^2-7x+15$.

Question 5

The equations $y=4x-3$ and $y=4x+2$ have the same slope and different $y$-intercepts. Classify the solution type.

Solution:

Same slope and different $y$-intercepts means the lines are parallel. Parallel different lines have no solution.

Question 6

The equations $y=-2x+5$ and $2y+4x=10$ represent the same line. Classify the solution type.

Solution:

Rewrite $2y+4x=10$ as $2y=-4x+10$, so $y=-2x+5$.

Both equations are the same line, so the system has infinitely many solutions.

Question 13

A table multiplies by $3$ each time the input increases by $1$. Name the most likely family and the evidence.

Solution:

The family is exponential because equal input steps multiply outputs by the same factor, here $3$.

Question 14

An equation has the form $y=m x+b$ with $m\ne 0$. Name the family and one graph feature to expect.

Solution:

The family is linear. A graph feature is a straight line with slope $m$ and $y$-intercept $b$.

Question 15

Classify the family for the table and explain what happens to first and second differences.

$x$0123
$y$14916
Solution:

Outputs $1,4,9,16$ have first differences $3,5,7$ and second differences $2,2$.

Constant second differences support a quadratic family.
