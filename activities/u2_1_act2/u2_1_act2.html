Unit 2 · Section 1 · Activity 2
Speed Dating Math
Rate of Change and Slope
Source: practice_set_2_1_3.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Partners
Materials: Whiteboard or blank paper for each person
Structure Paired practice → Timed rotation → Strategy sharing
Setup Partners. One whiteboard or blank paper for each person
Directions
  1. Work with a partner on the projected problem — both students write on their own paper
  2. Compare strategies and methods with your partner before rotating
  3. After 3–4 minutes, one row shifts one seat to create a new pairing
  4. Bring your previous work and share your strategy with your new partner
  5. Repeat across multiple problems and partner pairings
Goal: Multiple representations + collaborative problem solving
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Both partners solve on their own paper before comparing.
  • Each rotation begins with a quick explanation of the previous strategy.
  • Students carry prior work so new partners can compare methods.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • Partners only trade final answers with no strategy talk.
  • One row rotates before both students have written anything.
  • Students restart from scratch instead of learning from prior work.

Unit 2.1 · Activity 2 · Speed Dating Math Solution 1
Solution 1
Current · DOK 1

A vertical line passes through $x=5$. Describe what happens to $\Delta x$ between any two points on that line, then state the slope category.

Solution:
  1. All points on a vertical line have the same $x$-value.
  2. So $\Delta x=0$.
  3. Because slope divides by $\Delta x$, the slope is undefined.

Solution:

  2. The slope is $-6$ gallons per minute.
  3. The negative sign means the amount of water is decreasing.

4.Mastery · DOK 1–2

Evaluate $3(5-2)^2$.

Key:
  1. Rate $=\frac{1-9}{6-2}=-2$.
  2. One possible rule is $y=-3x+7$.
  3. $g(-4)=(-4)^2=16$.
  4. $3(5-2)^2=3\cdot 3^2=27$.
