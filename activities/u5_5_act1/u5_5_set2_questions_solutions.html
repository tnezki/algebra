Solution 12
Mastery · DOK 4

Create a system of two linear equations that has solution $(4,3)$ and is efficient to solve by substitution because one equation is already solved for $y$.

  1. Write the system.
  2. Show a substitution solution path.
  3. Explain why your structure makes substitution efficient.
Solution:

Example system: $y=x-1$ and $2x+y=11$. Substitute $x-1$ for $y$: $2x+(x-1)=11$, so $3x-1=11$, $3x=12$, and $x=4$. Then $y=4-1=3$. The solution is $(4,3)$, and substitution is efficient because one equation is already solved for $y$.
