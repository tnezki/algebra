Problem 13

You are solving $f(x)=0$ for a quadratic and all three equivalent forms are available. Which form usually gives the solutions most directly?

Factored form, because each factor can be set equal to zero.

Problem 14

You need the vertex and axis of symmetry of a quadratic. Which form usually makes both features easiest to read?

Vertex form; $a(x-h)^2+k$ shows the vertex $(h,k)$ and axis of symmetry $x=h$.

Problem 15

You need the $y$-intercept of a quadratic without doing any substitution. Which form usually makes it easiest to read?

Standard form $ax^2+bx+c$, because the $y$-intercept is $(0,c)$.

Problem 16

A student says factored form is always the best form. Give one task for which vertex form is more efficient.

Finding a maximum or minimum, the vertex, or the axis of symmetry is generally more efficient in vertex form.

Problem 21

For $y=(x-4)^2-9$, which visible feature would take more work to obtain: the vertex or the zeros?

The zeros take more work; the vertex is already visible.

Problem 22

A fountain's path is modeled by a quadratic, and the question asks for its greatest height and where it occurs. Which form is most useful first? Explain.

Vertex form, because the vertex gives the greatest height and the horizontal location where it occurs.

Problem 23

A projectile model asks when the object is on the ground, so you need the zeros. Would factored form or vertex form usually be more useful first? Explain.

Factored form, because the ground times are the inputs that make a factor—and therefore the height—zero.

Problem 24

Rewrite $y=(x+5)^2-25$ in factored form by recognizing a difference of squares.

$y=x(x+10)$.
