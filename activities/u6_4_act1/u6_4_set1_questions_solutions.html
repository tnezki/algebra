The graph shows a quadratic. Estimate the zeros from the graph, then solve $y=x^2-10x+24$ algebraically and decide which representation is more precise.

Solution:

From the graph, the zeros are about $4$ and $6$.

Algebraically, $x^2-10x+24=(x-4)(x-6)$, so the exact zeros are $x=4$ and $x=6$.

The algebraic representation is more precise because it gives exact intercepts.

A classmate says $y=(x-4)^2+6$ moves $y=x^2$ left $4$ and up $6$ because the equation contains $x-4$. Explain why that translation is not reasonable and give the correct movement.

Solution:

So the graph moves right $4$ and up $6$, not left $4$.
