Station 1 Answers

Question 1

Complete each square pattern.

  1. $x^2+12x+\_\_=(x+6)^2$
  2. $y^2-14y+\_\_=(y-7)^2$
  3. $4x^2+\_\_+9=(2x+3)^2$
Solution:
  1. $x^2+12x+36=(x+6)^2$.
  2. $y^2-14y+49=(y-7)^2$.
  3. $4x^2+12x+9=(2x+3)^2$.

Question 2

Write the conjugate factor pair for each difference of squares.

  1. $x^2-49=(x+\_\_)(x-\_\_)$
  2. $25p^2-36=(\_\_+\_\_)(\_\_-\_\_)$
Solution:
  1. $x^2-49=(x+7)(x-7)$.
  2. $25p^2-36=(5p+6)(5p-6)$.

Question 3

For each expression, decide the most efficient pattern and factor it.

  1. $25r^2-81$
  2. $16p^2+40p+25$
  3. $3x^2-27$
Solution:
  1. $25r^2-81=(5r+9)(5r-9)$, difference of squares.
  2. $16p^2+40p+25=(4p+5)^2$, perfect-square trinomial.
  3. $3x^2-27=3(x^2-9)=3(x+3)(x-3)$, GCF then difference of squares.

Question 4

Factor $36x^2-49$ using the difference-of-squares pattern. Then factor it again by thinking of it as a product of conjugates. Compare the two explanations.

Solution:

$36x^2-49=(6x)^2-7^2=(6x+7)(6x-7)$.

Using conjugates, $(6x+7)(6x-7)$ has middle terms $-42x$ and $+42x$ that cancel, leaving $36x^2-49$.

Question 5

When substitution gives $-2=5$, what solution type should be reported?

Solution:

A false statement such as $-2=5$ means the equations are inconsistent, so the system has no solution.

Question 6

For $y=-4x+2$, what expression should replace $y$ in another equation?

Solution:

Replace $y$ with $-4x+2$ in the other equation.

Question 7

A jar contains nickels and quarters. There are 44 coins worth \$6.80. Write and solve a system to find the number of nickels and quarters.

Solution:

Let $n$ be nickels and $q$ be quarters. Then $n+q=44$ and $5n+25q=680$.

Substitute $n=44-q$: $5(44-q)+25q=680$, so $220+20q=680$, $q=23$, and $n=21$.

Question 8

Two water tanks are draining. Tank A has 80 gallons and loses 5 gallons per minute. Tank B has 50 gallons and loses 2 gallons per minute. Write and solve a system to find when they contain the same amount.

Solution:

Tank A: $A=80-5t$. Tank B: $B=50-2t$.

Set equal: $80-5t=50-2t$, so $30=3t$ and $t=10$ minutes. Both tanks have $30$ gallons.

A streaming service charges \$8 per month plus a \$20 setup fee. Another charges \$12 per month with no setup fee. Write and solve a system to find the break-even month.

Solution:

Set equal: $8m+20=12m$, so $20=4m$ and $m=5$ months.

Question 11

A food truck can carry at most 60 meals. It needs at least 20 vegetarian meals. Let $v$ be vegetarian meals and $r$ be regular meals.

Before solving or graphing, estimate whether $(18,30)$, $(22,45)$, and $(30,25)$ are feasible. Justify each conclusion using the constraints.

Solution:

A model is $a+o\le 12$ with $a\ge0$ and $o\ge0$ for context.

The boundary $a+o=12$ represents a full tray.

Feasible whole-number points include $(6,6)$ and $(0,12)$. A non-feasible point is $(8,7)$ because $8+7=15\gt12$.

Question 15

Use the blank coordinate plane to graph $h(x)=(x+1)^2-4$. Mark the translated vertex and at least two matching points.

Solution:

The vertex is $(-1,-4)$. Matching points include $(0,-3)$ and $(-2,-3)$, and also $(1,0)$ and $(-3,0)$.

Question 16

Two students graph $y=-\frac{1}{2}(x-2)^2+4$. One transforms the vertex first; the other transforms a table of parent points first. Explain both methods and why they should produce the same graph.

Solution:

Vertex-first method: start at $(2,4)$, then use the scale/reflection to plot symmetric points.

Table method: transform parent points such as $(0,0),(1,1),(-1,1)$ by shifting right $2$, up $4$, and multiplying outputs from the vertex by $-\frac{1}{2}$.

Both methods use the same transformation, so they produce the same graph.
