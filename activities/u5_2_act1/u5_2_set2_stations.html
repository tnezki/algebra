Problem 23

Compare $(x+5)(x+7)$ and $(x+4)(x+8)$. Do they have the same constant term? Do they have the same middle coefficient?

They have different constant terms because the products differ, but their sums are equal, so the middle coefficients are the same.

Problem 24

A factor pair for a trinomial must have product -30 and sum 1. Can both integers be positive? Explain.

No. A negative product requires opposite signs.
