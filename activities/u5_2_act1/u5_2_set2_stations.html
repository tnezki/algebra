Problem 19

A student factors $x^2+13x+40$ as $(x+5)(x+8)$. Multiply the factors and decide whether the claim is correct.

Answer:

The factors expand to $x^2+13x+40$, so the claim is correct.

Problem 20

A rectangle has side lengths $(x+5)$ and $(x+8)$. Write the area in standard form and factored form.

Answer:

Factored: $(x+5)(x+8)$; standard: $x^2+13x+40$.

Problem 21

Answer:

$(x-4)(x-9)$

Problem 22

Factor $x^{2} + 2x - 35$.

Answer:

$(x-5)(x+7)$

Problem 23

Compare $(x+5)(x+7)$ and $(x+4)(x+8)$. Do they have the same constant term? Do they have the same middle coefficient?

Answer:

They have different constant terms because the products differ, but their sums are equal, so the middle coefficients are the same.

Problem 24

A factor pair for a trinomial must have product -30 and sum 1. Can both integers be positive? Explain.

Answer:

No. A negative product requires opposite signs.
