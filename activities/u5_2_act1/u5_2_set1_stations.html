Problem 19

A student factors $x^2+11x+28$ as $(x+4)(x+7)$. Multiply the factors and decide whether the claim is correct.

Answer:

The factors expand to $x^2+11x+28$, so the claim is correct.

Problem 20

A rectangle has side lengths $(x+4)$ and $(x+7)$. Write the area in standard form and factored form.

Answer:

Factored: $(x+4)(x+7)$; standard: $x^2+11x+28$.

Problem 21

Factor $x^{2} - 11x + 24$.

Answer:

$(x-3)(x-8)$

Problem 22

Factor $x^{2} + 2x - 24$.

Answer:

$(x-4)(x+6)$

Problem 23

Compare $(x+4)(x+6)$ and $(x+3)(x+7)$. Do they have the same constant term? Do they have the same middle coefficient?

Answer:

They have different constant terms because the products differ, but their sums are equal, so the middle coefficients are the same.

Problem 24

A factor pair for a trinomial must have product -20 and sum 1. Can both integers be positive? Explain.

Answer:

No. A negative product requires opposite signs.
