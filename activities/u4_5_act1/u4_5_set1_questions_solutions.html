Problem 22

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=1.5d+20$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=6$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

Algebra 1 • Unit 4 • Section 4.5Activity Set 1
Solution 22

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=1.5d+20$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=6$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

$M=1.5(6)+20=29$, so the model predicts \$2900. The slope means \$150 per day and the intercept means \$2000 at day 0.
