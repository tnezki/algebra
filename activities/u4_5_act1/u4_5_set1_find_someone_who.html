Problem 1

Use the graph to identify the vertex, axis of symmetry, direction of opening, and any visible x-intercepts.

Answer:

Vertex $(-2,4)$, axis $x=-2$, opens down; x-intercepts are $(-4,0)$ and $(0,0)$.

Problem 2

A student claims that the constant term of a quadratic always gives the vertex. Critique the claim using $y=x^2 - 6x + 6$.

Answer:

The constant term 6 gives the y-intercept $(0,6)$, not the vertex. This quadratic has vertex $(3,-3)$.

Problem 3

For $y=(x+2)(x-1)$, predict the x-intercepts and axis of symmetry before graphing.

Answer:

The x-intercepts are $x=-2$ and $x=1$; the axis of symmetry is $x=-0.5$.

Problem 4

For $y=x^2 + 4x + 6$, identify the y-intercept and the direction of opening.

Answer:

The y-intercept is $(0,6)$, and the parabola opens upward because the leading coefficient is 1.

Problem 17

A student says the constant term of $y=x^2 - 6x + 7$ is the vertex. Critique the claim.

Answer:

The constant term 7 gives the y-intercept $(0,7)$. Completing the square gives vertex $(3,-2)$.

Problem 18

For $y=(x-1)(x-6)$, predict the x-intercepts and axis of symmetry before graphing.

Answer:

The x-intercepts are $x=1$ and $x=6$; the axis of symmetry is $x=3.5$.

Problem 19

Adult tickets cost \$8 and student tickets cost \$5. A school sold 8 tickets for \$55. Write and solve a system to determine how many of each type were sold.

Answer:

Let $a+s=8$ and $8a+5s=55$. Solving gives 5 adult tickets and 3 student tickets.

Problem 21

Problem 22

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=1.5d+20$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=6$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

Answer:

$M=1.5(6)+20=29$, so the model predicts \$2900. The slope means \$150 per day and the intercept means \$2000 at day 0.

Problem 23

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=4d+8$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=7$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

Answer:

$M=4(7)+8=36$, so the model predicts \$3600. The slope means \$400 per day and the intercept means \$800 at day 0.

Problem 24

Answer:

$M=2(8)+5=21$, so the model predicts \$2100. The slope means \$200 per day and the intercept means \$500 at day 0.
