Question 1

For $y=x^2-6x+8$, identify whether the graph opens up or down.

Solution: The coefficient of $x^2$ is positive, so the graph opens up.

Question 2

For $y=2(x-1)^2-7$, identify the axis of symmetry.

Solution: In $y=2(x-1)^2-7$, the vertex is $(1,-7)$, so the axis of symmetry is $x=1$.

Question 3

Use the table to identify the vertex and write a possible vertex-form equation.

$x$-10123
$y$94101

Solution: The smallest output is $0$ at $x=2$, so the vertex is $(2,0)$. The symmetric values around $x=2$ match a basic quadratic, so one possible equation is $y=(x-2)^2$.

Question 4

A model predicts a ball height with vertex $(4,80)$ and zeros at $0$ and $8$. Explain why those features are reasonable together, then identify what would be unreasonable about zeros at $0$ and $6$ with the same vertex.

Solution: Zeros at $0$ and $8$ are symmetric around the vertex input $4$, so they are reasonable with vertex $(4,80)$. Zeros at $0$ and $6$ would have midpoint $3$, not $4$, so they would not be symmetric around the same vertex; that would be unreasonable for a parabola with vertex at $x=4$.

Question 5

If two lines have the same slope but different intercepts, state whether they intersect, are parallel, or are the same line.

Solution: Two lines with the same slope and different intercepts are parallel, so they do not intersect.

Question 6

If two relationships have the same intercept but different slopes, what feature is shared at $x=0$?

Solution: They share the same value at $x=0$, which means they have the same $y$-intercept.

Question 7

A line passes through $A(-3,-2)$ and $B(2,1)$. Does it also pass through $C(5,3)$? Justify by comparing slopes.

Solution: Slope from $A$ to $B$ is $\frac{1-(-2)}{2-(-3)}=\frac{3}{5}$. Slope from $B$ to $C$ is $\frac{3-1}{5-2}=\frac{2}{3}$. Since $\frac{3}{5} e\frac{2}{3}$, point $C$ is not on the same line.

Question 8

Without graphing, find the slope of each line:

  1. Through $(4,1)$ and $(2,5)$
  2. Through $(0,0)$ and $(10,5)$
  3. A vertical line through $(6,-5)$
  4. Through $(1,6)$ and $(10,6)$

Solution: Slopes: through $(4,1)$ and $(2,5)$, $m=\frac{5-1}{2-4}=-2$; through $(0,0)$ and $(10,5)$, $m=\frac{5}{10}=\frac{1}{2}$; a vertical line has undefined slope; through $(1,6)$ and $(10,6)$, $m=0$.

Question 9

Use the graph to calculate the slope. Then write a sentence interpreting whether the relationship is increasing or decreasing.

Solution: From the graph, two clear points are $(0,3)$ and $(6,0)$. The slope is $\frac{0-3}{6-0}=-\frac{3}{6}=-\frac{1}{2}$. The relationship is decreasing because the slope is negative.

Question 10

You are given a graph with no labeled intercept but two clear points: $(-2,9)$ and $(4,-3)$. Construct a strategy to write the equation accurately and explain why your strategy avoids guessing from the picture.

Solution: Use the two clear points instead of guessing. Slope $m=\frac{-3-9}{4-(-2)}=\frac{-12}{6}=-2$. Substitute $(-2,9)$ into $y=mx+b$: $9=-2(-2)+b=4+b$, so $b=5$. The equation is $y=-2x+5$. This avoids guessing because it uses exact coordinates and slope.

Question 11

Analyze $2y=6x+10$ and $y=3x+5$. Decide whether they represent the same linear relationship. Then describe which form makes the slope and intercept easier to see.

Solution: Divide $2y=6x+10$ by $2$ to get $y=3x+5$. This is the same as the second equation, so they represent the same linear relationship. The form $y=3x+5$ makes both the slope and intercept easier to see.

Question 12

A relationship has slope $\frac{3}{5}$ and contains the point $(10,11)$. Build three matching representations: a verbal rate statement, a table with at least four rows, and a slope-intercept equation.

Solution: A slope of $\frac{3}{5}$ means the output increases $3$ for every $5$ input units. Using $(10,11)$, $11=\frac{3}{5}(10)+b=6+b$, so $b=5$. The equation is $y=\frac{3}{5}x+5$.

Question 13

A solution $(40,80)$ is found for variables $x$ and $y$. Which coordinate gives the value of $x$?

Solution: The first coordinate gives $x$, so $x=40$.

Question 14

Why should variables be defined before writing a system from a situation?

Solution: Variables should be defined first so each equation has a clear meaning and each coordinate of the solution can be interpreted correctly.

Question 15

Substitute $y=5-x$ into $4x+2y=18$ and solve for $x$. Then find $y$.

Solution: Substitute: $4x+2(5-x)=18$. Then $4x+10-2x=18$, so $2x=8$ and $x=4$. Then $y=5-4=1$.

Question 16

A food truck can carry at most 60 meals. It needs at least 20 vegetarian meals. Let $v$ be vegetarian meals and $r$ be regular meals.

Before solving or graphing, estimate whether $(18,30)$, $(22,45)$, and $(30,25)$ are feasible. Justify each conclusion using the constraints.

Solution: Constraints: $v+r\le 60$ and $v\ge 20$. For $(18,30)$, $v=18\lt20$, so not feasible. For $(22,45)$, $22+45=67\gt60$, so not feasible. For $(30,25)$, $30+25=55\le 60$ and $30\ge 20$, so feasible.
