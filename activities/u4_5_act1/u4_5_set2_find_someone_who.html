Problem 1

A student claims the axis of symmetry of $y=x^2+6x+5$ is $x=6$ because the middle coefficient is 6. Critique the claim.

Answer:

The middle coefficient alone is not the axis. Using $x=-b/(2a)$ gives $x=-6/2=-3$, so the axis of symmetry is $x=-3$.

Problem 2

For $y=(x-1)(x-5)$, predict the x-intercepts and axis of symmetry before graphing.

Answer:

x-intercepts $(1,0)$ and $(5,0)$; axis of symmetry $x=3$.

Problem 3

For $y=x^2 - x - 6$, identify the y-intercept and the direction of opening.

Answer:

y-intercept $(0,-6)$; it opens upward because the leading coefficient is positive.

Problem 11

A student says $y=(x-2)(x+4)$ has x-intercepts 2 and 4. Critique the claim.

The zeros are $x=2$ and $x=-4$, so the x-intercepts are $(2,0)$ and $(-4,0)$. The corrected feature is what should be predicted before graphing.

Problem 22

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=1.5d+20$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=7$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

Answer:

$M=1.5(7)+20=30.5$, so the model predicts \$3050. The slope means \$150 per day and the intercept means \$2000 at day 0.

Problem 23

A linear model for a school fundraiser is $M=4d+8$, where $d$ is days and $M$ is hundreds of dollars. Predict $M$ at $d=8$ and interpret the slope and intercept.

Answer:

$M=4(8)+8=40$, so the model predicts \$4000. The slope means \$400 per day and the intercept means \$800 at day 0.

Answer:
