Problem 1

Before solving $x+y=21$ and $8x+5y=126$ for large candles and small candles, explain what each coordinate of the ordered-pair solution will represent.

Answer:

The first coordinate is the number of large candles; the second is the number of small candles. Both coordinates must satisfy the count and value constraints.

Problem 2

The table lists combinations of large candles and small candles that meet one total-item constraint. Describe the relationship and write the count equation.

x = large candlesy = small candles
034
826
1717

Answer:

Each increase of 1 in $x$ requires a decrease of 1 in $y$ to keep the total at 34; $x+y=34$.

Problem 3

A group sells large candles for 8 dollars each and small candles for 5 dollars each. It sold 9 items for 54 dollars total. A candidate solution is $(3,6)$. What checks confirm that this solution is reasonable in context?

Answer:

Check both constraints: $3+6=9$ and $8(3)+5(6)=54$. The values are also nonnegative whole numbers, so they fit an item-count context.

Problem 4

A group sells premium passes for 12 dollars each and regular passes for 7 dollars each. They sell 7 items for 64 dollars. Define variables and write a system that models the situation.

Answer:

Let $x$ be the number of premium passes and $y$ the number of regular passes. A valid system is $x+y=7$ and $12x+7y=64$.
