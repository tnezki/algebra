Answer:

Problem 2

A model for counts of meal vouchers and drink vouchers produces the candidate $(12,8)$. Explain what must be checked before accepting it as a reasonable contextual solution.

Answer:

Verify the candidate in both original constraints and confirm both coordinates are nonnegative whole numbers because they count meal vouchers and drink vouchers.

Problem 3

A fundraiser sells large boxes for 7 dollars each and small boxes for 3 dollars each. A total of 15 items brings in 69 dollars. Define variables and write a system.

Answer:

Let $x$ be large boxes and $y$ be small boxes. Then $x+y=15$ and $7x+3y=69$.

Problem 4

Using $x$ for large boxes and $y$ for small boxes, solve $x+y=15$ and $7x+3y=69$. Interpret both coordinates in context.

Answer:

$x=6$ and $y=9$. The coordinates give the numbers of large boxes and small boxes, respectively.
