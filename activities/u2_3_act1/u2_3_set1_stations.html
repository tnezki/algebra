Question 1

A line has slope $5$ and $y$-intercept $-2$. Write the equation.

Solution:
  1. Slope-intercept form is $y=mx+b$.
  2. Use $m=5$ and $b=-2$.
  3. The equation is $y=5x-2$.

Question 2

A context starts at $40$ and decreases by $3$ each step. Identify the slope and initial value.

Solution:
  1. The starting value is the amount at step $0$: $40$.
  2. Decreases by $3$ means slope $-3$.
