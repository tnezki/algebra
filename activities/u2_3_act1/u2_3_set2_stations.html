Problem 13

For $y=\frac{1}{2}x+5$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=\frac{1}{2}$ is the constant rate of change and $b=5$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(2,6)$ lies on the line.

Problem 14

For $y=5x-4$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=5$ is the constant rate of change and $b=-4$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(2,6)$ lies on the line.

Problem 15

For $y=-3x+9$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=-3$ is the constant rate of change and $b=9$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(2,3)$ lies on the line.

Problem 16

A linear relationship is represented by $y=4x-5$. Write a different equation that is equivalent to it after moving all terms to one side, and verify that $(3,7)$ satisfies both forms.

Answer:

One equivalent form is $4x-y-5=0$. Substituting $x=3$ and $y=7$ makes both equations true.
