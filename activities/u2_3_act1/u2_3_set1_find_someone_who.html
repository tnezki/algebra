Problem 1

Write a linear equation in slope-intercept form for the line through $(-4,6)$ and $(2,0)$.

Answer:

$m=-1$ and $b=2$, so $y=-x+2$.

Problem 2

Write a linear equation in slope-intercept form for the line through $(-2,1)$ and $(2,9)$.

Answer:

$m=2$ and $b=5$, so $y=2x+5$.

Problem 3

Write a linear equation in slope-intercept form for the line through $(-3,-1)$ and $(3,2)$.

Answer:

$m=\frac{1}{2}$ and $b=\frac{1}{2}$, so $y=\frac{1}{2}x+\frac{1}{2}$.

Problem 4

Write an equation in slope-intercept form for the relationship in the table.

$x$$y$
$-2$$7$
$0$$5$
$2$$3$
$4$$1$
Answer:

The constant rate is $-1$ and the value at $x=0$ is $5$, so $y=-x+5$.

Problem 9

Write an equation in slope-intercept form for the graphed line. Use graph features as evidence.

Answer:

The graph has slope $\frac{4}{3}$ and $y$-intercept $-3$, so $y=\frac{4}{3}x-3$.

Problem 10

A tank contains 70 liters and drains 5 liters per minute. Let $t$ be minutes and $V$ be liters remaining. Write $V$ as a function of $t$. Identify the per-minute change and the initial volume.

Answer:

$V=-5t+70$. The slope $-5$ is the change in V per unit of t; the intercept $70$ is the starting value.

Problem 11

A candle is 18 cm tall and burns down 1.5 cm each hour. Let $h$ be hours and $H$ be height. Write $H$ as a function of $h$. Identify the hourly change and the initial height.

Answer:

$H=-\frac{3}{2}h+18$. The slope $-\frac{3}{2}$ is the change in H per unit of h; the intercept $18$ is the starting value.

Problem 12

A tank contains 96 liters and drains 6 liters per minute. Let $t$ be minutes and $V$ be volume. Write $V$ as a function of $t$. Identify the per-minute change and the initial volume.

Answer:

$V=-6t+96$. The slope $-6$ is the per-minute change; the intercept $96$ is the initial volume.

Problem 13

For $y=-\frac{3}{2}x+10$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=-\frac{3}{2}$ is the constant rate of change and $b=10$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(2,7)$ lies on the line.

Problem 14

For $y=3x-3$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=3$ is the constant rate of change and $b=-3$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(5,12)$ lies on the line.

Problem 15

For $y=3x+1$, explain the roles of $m$ and $b$. Then give one ordered pair that must lie on the line.

Answer:

$m=3$ is the constant rate of change and $b=1$ is the value when $x=0$. For example, $(5,16)$ lies on the line.

Problem 16

A linear relationship is represented by $y=\frac{1}{2}x+5$. Write a different equation that is equivalent to it after moving all terms to one side, and verify that $(9,\frac{19}{2})$ satisfies both forms.

Answer:

One equivalent form is $\frac{1}{2}x-y+5=0$. Substituting $x=9$ and $y=\frac{19}{2}$ makes both equations true.

Problem 17

A linear relationship is represented by $y=-x+4$. Write a different equation that is equivalent to it after moving all terms to one side, and verify that $(3,1)$ satisfies both forms.

Answer:

One equivalent form is $-x-y+4=0$. Substituting $x=3$ and $y=1$ makes both equations true.

Problem 18

A linear relationship is represented by $y=-3x+7$. Write a different equation that is equivalent to it after moving all terms to one side, and verify that $(2,1)$ satisfies both forms.

Answer:

One equivalent form is $-3x-y+7=0$. Substituting $x=2$ and $y=1$ makes both equations true.

Problem 19

Rewrite $4(x+1)$ as an equivalent expression, then verify equivalence at $x=2$.

Answer:

The equivalent expression is $4x+4$. At $x=2$, both forms equal $12$.

Problem 20

Answer:

$-3(x-4)=-3x+12$. At $x=3$, both forms equal $3$.
