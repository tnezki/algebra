A tank contains 70 liters and drains 5 liters per minute. Let $t$ be minutes and $V$ be liters remaining. Write $V$ as a function of $t$. Identify the per-minute change and the initial volume.

Solution:

$V=-5t+70$. The slope $-5$ is the change in V per unit of t; the intercept $70$ is the starting value.
