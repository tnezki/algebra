Rally Coach
Writing Linear Equations
Source: practice_set_2_3_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Partners
Materials: One whiteboard and one marker per person
Structure Partner explanation + alternating roles
Setup Sit with your partner. Each person needs one whiteboard and one marker.
Directions
  1. Work with a partner
  2. Partner A explains, Partner B writes
  3. Switch roles
  4. Check answers with teacher
  5. Rotate partners
Goal: Verbal reasoning + procedural accuracy
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Partner A explains each step while Partner B writes exactly what is described.
  • Partners switch roles after each problem or round.
  • Both partners check the result before asking the teacher.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • The writer solves silently while the coach waits.
  • Partners keep the same roles the whole time.
  • The pair asks for a check before agreeing on their own work.

Unit 2.3 · Activity 1 · Rally Coach Solution 1
Solution 1
Current · DOK 1

A line has slope $5$ and $y$-intercept $-2$. Write the equation.

Solution:
  1. Slope-intercept form is $y=mx+b$.
  2. Use $m=5$ and $b=-2$.
  3. The equation is $y=5x-2$.
