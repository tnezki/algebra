Find the slope of the line through $(2,1)$ and $(8,4)$.

Answer:

$m=\dfrac{3}{6}=\frac{1}{2}$.

Find the slope of the line through $(1,1)$ and $(4,10)$.

$m=3$.

Problem 3

Find the slope of the line through $(1,4)$ and $(7,-5)$.

Answer:

$m=-\dfrac{9}{6}=-\frac{3}{2}$.

Problem 4

$x$$y$
$0$$9$
$2$$6$
$4$$3$
$6$$0$
Answer:

The rate of change is $-\frac{3}{2}$ because $y$ changes by $-3$ when $x$ changes by 2.

Classify the slope of a line through $(1,2)$ and $(5,10)$.

Answer:

The slope is positive because $y$ increases as $x$ increases.

Answer:

The slope is $-5$ liters per minute because the volume is decreasing.
