Question 1

A vertical line passes through $x=5$. Describe what happens to $\Delta x$ between any two points on that line, then state the slope category.

Solution:
  1. All points on a vertical line have the same $x$-value.
  2. So $\Delta x=0$.
  3. Because slope divides by $\Delta x$, the slope is undefined.

Question 2

A water tank has $48$ gallons and drains at $6$ gallons per minute. Write the slope with units and explain what the negative rate means in the situation.

Solution:
  1. The amount changes by $-6$ gallons each minute.
  2. The slope is $-6$ gallons per minute.
  3. The negative sign means the amount of water is decreasing.
