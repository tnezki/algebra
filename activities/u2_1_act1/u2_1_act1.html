Showdown
Rate of Change and Slope
Source: practice_set_2_1_2.html
Time: 20–30 min
Groups: Groups of 4
Materials: Whiteboards + markers
Structure Indy → Group comparison → Consensus
Setup Groups of 4. Each person has a whiteboard. Rotate the Captain each round.
Directions
  1. Each student solves the problem individually
  2. When prompted, students Showdown (reveal answers at the same time)
  3. Discuss differences and justify reasoning
  4. Group agrees on a correct final answer
  5. Repeat
Goal: Accountability + justification
✓ Looks Like Success
  • Every student writes a full attempt before any reveal.
  • The Captain waits until all boards are ready before calling Showdown.
  • Students explain differences before the group chooses one final answer.
✗ Doesn't Look Like
  • One student announces an answer before boards are revealed.
  • Students erase or change work after seeing another board.
  • The group accepts an answer without asking for evidence.

Unit 2.1 · Activity 1 Exit Ticket Answer Key
Exit Ticket
1.Current · DOK 1–2

A line drops from $y=10$ to $y=4$ while $x$ increases from 1 to 4. Find the slope.

2.Current · DOK 1–2

A runner's distance changes from 12 meters to 30 meters in 3 seconds. What is the average rate?

3.Last · DOK 1–2

Identify the family suggested by outputs $2,5,8,11$.

4.Mastery · DOK 1–2

Combine like terms: $7a-3a+5$.

Key:
  1. Slope $=\frac{4-10}{4-1}=-2$.
  2. Average rate $=\frac{30-12}{3}=6$ meters per second.
  3. The outputs increase by $3$ each time, so the family is linear.
  4. $7a-3a+5=4a+5$.
