Problem 17

Two points are $(2,3)$ and $(6,11)$. A student uses $\frac{11-3}{2-6}=-2$. Identify and repair the subtraction-order error.

Answer:

The point order is inconsistent. Using the same order gives $\frac{11-3}{6-2}=2$.

Problem 18

A cyclist travels 18 miles in 1.5 hours. Jordan reports the rate as $12$ hours per mile. Correct the unit interpretation.

Answer:

The rate for distance versus time is $12$ miles per hour; the units were reversed.
