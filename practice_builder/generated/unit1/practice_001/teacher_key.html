Coordinate-Plane Graphs · One-Off Practice Packet

Teacher Key. Answers and concise solutions follow each item.

1.I Can: Plot and read ordered pairs.

Plot the point \(R(5,-7)\) on the coordinate plane. Label the point R.

Answer: R is at (5, -7).
Solution: Move 5 units right and 7 units down, then label the point R.
2.I Can: Plot and read ordered pairs.

Start at the origin. Move 4 units left and then 7 units down. Write the ordered pair for the ending point.

Answer: (-4, -7)
Solution: Left makes the x-coordinate -4; down makes the y-coordinate -7.
3.I Can: Plot and read ordered pairs.

A student says, “I moved 5 units right and 4 units down from the origin, so the point is \((-5,-4)\).” Explain the error and write the correct ordered pair.

Answer: (5, -4); the x-coordinate must be positive.
Solution: Moving right gives a positive x-coordinate, so the correct point is (5, -4).
4.I Can: Plot and read ordered pairs.

Plot the point \(S(0,-6)\) on the coordinate plane. Label the point S.

Answer: S is at (0, -6).
Solution: Because x=0, the point lies on the y-axis; move 6 units down and label S.
5.I Can: Use the x- and y-axes correctly.

For \(T(-3,5)\), state which axis controls the -3 movement and which axis controls the 5 movement. Then plot T.

Answer: x-axis: 3 left; y-axis: 5 up; T=(-3,5).
Solution: The first coordinate controls horizontal movement on the x-axis; the second controls vertical movement on the y-axis.
6.I Can: Use the x- and y-axes correctly.

A graph will show how the amount of water in a tank changes as minutes pass. Which quantity belongs on the x-axis and which belongs on the y-axis? Give a one-sentence reason.

Answer: Minutes on the x-axis; amount of water on the y-axis.
Solution: Time is the input that progresses, and the amount of water depends on that time.
7.I Can: Use the x- and y-axes correctly.

A student plots \(W(4,-2)\) by moving 4 units down and 2 units right. Explain the axis mistake and describe the correct moves.

Answer: Move 4 right, then 2 down.
Solution: The x-coordinate 4 controls horizontal movement; the y-coordinate -2 controls vertical movement.
8.I Can: Use the x- and y-axes correctly.

A graph will show total money collected as the number of tickets sold increases. Which quantity belongs on each axis? Explain briefly.

Answer: Tickets sold on x-axis; money collected on y-axis.
Solution: The number of tickets sold is the input; the money collected depends on the number sold.
9.I Can: Read a graph using its scale.

Use the scale on the graph to write the ordered pair for point A.

Answer: (15, 60)
Solution: The x-axis counts by 5 and the y-axis counts by 20. A is at x=15 and y=60.
10.I Can: Read a graph using its scale.

Use the graph scale to read the y-values of A and B. By how much is B’s y-value greater than A’s?

Answer: 50
Solution: A has y=40 and B has y=90, so 90-40=50.
11.I Can: Read a graph using its scale.

Point P is shown on the graph. A student says P has y-coordinate 3 because it is three gridlines above 0. Explain the error and give P’s correct y-coordinate.

Answer: 75
Solution: The y-axis increases by 25 per gridline, so three gridlines above 0 is 75.
12.I Can: Read a graph using its scale.

Point R is shown on the graph. A student says its x-coordinate is 3 because it is on the third vertical gridline to the right of 0. Explain the error and give the correct x-coordinate.

Answer: 150
Solution: The x-axis increases by 50 per gridline, so the third gridline is x=150.
13.I Can: Identify and interpret intercepts when they are shown.

Identify the x-intercept and the y-intercept of the line shown.

Answer: x-intercept (6,0); y-intercept (0,-3).
Solution: The x-intercept is where y=0; the y-intercept is where x=0.
14.I Can: Identify and interpret intercepts when they are shown.

The graph shows water remaining in a tank while it drains. Interpret the x-intercept \((6,0)\) in this situation.

Answer: After 6 minutes, 0 liters remain; the tank is empty.
Solution: At an x-intercept, the y-value is 0. Here x is time and y is water remaining.
15.I Can: Identify and interpret intercepts when they are shown.

A student says, “The point \((0,4)\) is the x-intercept because its x-coordinate is 0.” Explain the error. Then identify both intercepts of the line.

Answer: (0,4) is the y-intercept; x-intercept (-2,0), y-intercept (0,4).
Solution: An x-intercept has y=0. A y-intercept has x=0.
