I Can: Plot and read ordered pairs.
1.Plot a Given Ordered Pair

Plot the point \(T(-8,4)\) on the coordinate plane. Label the point T.

Answer: T is at (-8, 4).
Solution: Move 8 units left from the origin and 4 units up; place and label T.
2.Translate Movement to an Ordered Pair

Start at the origin. Move 9 units up, then 4 units left. Write the ordered pair for the ending point.

Answer: (-4, 9)
Solution: Left makes the x-coordinate -4. Up makes the y-coordinate 9.
3.Diagnose a Coordinate Sign Error

A student says, “From the origin I moved 7 units right and 4 units down, so the point is \((-7,-4)\).” Explain the error and write the correct ordered pair.

Answer: The x-coordinate should be positive. The point is (7, -4).
Solution: Moving right gives a positive x-coordinate; moving down gives a negative y-coordinate.
4.Plot a Given Ordered Pair

Plot the point \(U(6,0)\) on the coordinate plane. Label the point U.

Answer: U is at (6, 0) on the x-axis.
Solution: Move 6 units right and 0 units vertically; place U on the x-axis.
5.Translate Movement to an Ordered Pair

Start at the origin. Move 5 units up, then 9 units right. Write the ordered pair for the ending point.

Answer: (9, 5)
Solution: Right gives x=9; up gives y=5.
6.Diagnose a Coordinate Sign Error

A student says, “Two units left and 6 units down ends at \((-2,6)\).” Explain the error and write the correct ordered pair.

Answer: The y-coordinate should be negative. The point is (-2, -6).
Solution: Left gives a negative x-coordinate and down gives a negative y-coordinate.
I Can: Use the x- and y-axes correctly.
7.Axis-First Coordinate Move

For the point \(V(-7,3)\), state which axis controls each movement. Then plot and label V.

Answer: x-axis: 7 left; y-axis: 3 up; V=(-7,3).
Solution: The first coordinate controls horizontal x movement; the second controls vertical y movement.
8.Assign Variables to Axes

A graph will show the number of photos uploaded as time passes. Which quantity belongs on the x-axis, and which belongs on the y-axis? Explain why.

Answer: Time on the x-axis; number of photos uploaded on the y-axis.
Solution: Time is the input that progresses; the upload count is measured for each time.
9.Diagnose an Axis Swap

To plot \(W(4,-8)\), Jordan moves 8 units right and 4 units down. What did Jordan reverse? State the correct movements.

Answer: Jordan reversed the coordinates. Move 4 right, then 8 down.
Solution: The first coordinate, 4, controls x; the second, -8, controls y.
10.Axis-First Coordinate Move

For the point \(X(0,-9)\), describe the x-movement and y-movement. Then plot and label X.

Answer: x-movement: 0; y-movement: 9 down; X=(0,-9).
Solution: Because x=0 there is no horizontal movement. The y-coordinate -9 places X on the negative y-axis.
11.Assign Variables to Axes

A student wants to graph the temperature of a cooling drink as time passes. The student puts temperature on the x-axis and time on the y-axis. Is that assignment sensible? If not, correct it and explain.

Answer: No. Time should be on the x-axis and temperature on the y-axis.
Solution: Time is the input/independent quantity; temperature is recorded as the output for each time.
12.Diagnose an Axis Swap

To plot \(Y(-5,2)\), Alex moves 5 units up and then 2 units left. Explain the axis mistake and state the correct movements.

Answer: Alex swapped the axis roles. Move 5 left, then 2 up.
Solution: The x-coordinate -5 controls horizontal movement; y=2 controls vertical movement.
I Can: Identify and interpret intercepts when they are shown.
13.Identify Both Intercepts

Identify the x-intercept and y-intercept of the line.

Answer: x-intercept (6,0); y-intercept (0,-4).
Solution: At the x-intercept y=0; at the y-intercept x=0.
14.Interpret an Intercept in Context

The graph shows the percent charge remaining in a device as streaming time passes. What does the y-intercept mean in this situation? Include units.

Answer: At 0 hours, the device has 90% charge.
Solution: The y-intercept occurs when time is 0, so it represents the starting charge: 90%.
15.Distinguish x- from y-Intercept

A student says, “\((4,0)\) is the y-intercept because it lies on an axis.” Correct the statement and explain how you know.

Answer: (4,0) is the x-intercept; the y-intercept is (0,8).
Solution: An x-intercept has y=0. A y-intercept has x=0.
16.Identify Both Intercepts

Identify the x-intercept and y-intercept of the line.

Answer: x-intercept (-2,0); y-intercept (0,6).
Solution: The x-intercept is where y=0; the y-intercept is where x=0.
17.Interpret an Intercept in Context

The graph shows the amount of water left in a tank as it drains. What does the x-intercept mean in this situation? Include units.

Answer: The tank is empty after 8 minutes.
Solution: The x-intercept is (8,0), meaning 0 liters remain at 8 minutes.
18.Distinguish x- from y-Intercept

A student calls \((0,-6)\) the x-intercept. Correct the statement, identify both intercepts, and give a rule that distinguishes them.

I Can: Find and interpret rate of change from a context.
19.Compute and Interpret Context Rate

A tank contains 120 liters of water after 3 minutes and 192 liters after 7 minutes. Find the rate of change and interpret it in context.

Answer: 18 liters per minute; the amount of water increases by 18 liters each minute.
Solution: Rate = (192-120)/(7-3) = 72/4 = 18 liters per minute.
20.Read Rate from a Context Graph

The graph shows water remaining in a reservoir as time passes. Find the rate of change using two readable points and explain what the sign means.

Answer: -18 liters per minute; the reservoir loses 18 liters each minute.
Solution: For example, from (1,180) to (5,108): (108-180)/(5-1) = -72/4 = -18 L/min. The negative sign means the amount decreases.
21.Distinguish Rate from Initial Value

A drone’s height is modeled by \(H=250-8t\), where H is height in feet and t is time in seconds. A student says, “The rate is 250 feet per second.” Explain the error and state the actual rate.

Answer: The rate is -8 feet per second; 250 feet is the initial height.
Solution: The coefficient of t gives change per second. The constant is the value at t=0.
22.Compute and Interpret Context Rate

A cup of tea is 82°F after 1 minute and 61°F after 8 minutes. Find the rate of change and interpret it in context.

Answer: -3°F per minute; the temperature decreases by 3°F each minute.
Solution: Rate = (61-82)/(8-1) = -21/7 = -3°F per minute.
23.Read Rate from a Context Graph

The graph shows the number of pages read as time passes. Find the rate of change and explain what it means.

Answer: 4 pages per minute; the reader gains 4 pages for each additional minute.
Solution: Using (4,18) and (10,42): (42-18)/(10-4) = 24/6 = 4 pages per minute.
24.Distinguish Rate from Initial Value

A scooter rental app gives the table below. A student says, “The rate is $4 per minute because $4 is the first cost shown.” Explain the error and state the actual rate.

MinutesTotal cost
0$4.00
5$11.50
10$19.00
Answer: The rate is $1.50 per minute; $4 is the starting fee.
Solution: The cost rises $7.50 over 5 minutes, so rate = 7.50/5 = $1.50 per minute. At 0 minutes the cost is $4, so $4 is the initial value.
