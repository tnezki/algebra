Algebra 1 · Section 5.2 Investigation Teacher Guide

Sum-Product to Rectangle

Factoring Trinomials

Why This Fits

The create-and-trade structure makes factoring the inverse of multiplication and keeps sum/product reasoning explicit.

TimeAbout 20–30 minutes
Groupingpairs, then trade
Core ideaStudents will factor quadratic trinomials using patterns, products, sums, and structure.

Materials

sum-product cards, area-model grids, pencils

Before Class

Prepare the listed materials and, when a generated representation is used, verify the local figure opens. Keep the task optional: students should still receive required core instruction elsewhere.

Suggested Flow

  1. For x²+7x+12, find two numbers with product 12 and sum 7.
  2. Use those numbers as side lengths in an area/box model to verify (x+3)(x+4).
  3. Repeat with a negative constant and track sign logic.
  4. Create a factorable monic trinomial from chosen integer factors.
  5. Trade only the trinomial; the partner reconstructs the factors and checks by multiplication.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • A uses 3 and 4: (x+3)(x+4).
  • B uses -4 and 3: (x-4)(x+3), since product -12 and sum -1.
  • For a created monic trinomial, middle coefficient is p+q and constant is pq.

Watch For

  • Using factor pairs that multiply correctly but do not produce the required middle term.
  • Stopping after finding one factor without verifying the full product.

Strong Synthesis

Target: Expanding (x+p)(x+q) gives x²+(p+q)x+pq, so their sum becomes the x-coefficient and product becomes the constant.

Synthesis — student prompt

Why do the two side numbers in a sum-product structure control both the middle and constant terms of a monic trinomial?

Suggested / acceptable response: Expanding (x+p)(x+q) gives x²+(p+q)x+pq, so their sum becomes the x-coefficient and product becomes the constant.
What to listen for: Students should generalize from the investigation evidence, not repeat a procedure. Teacher press:
  • Check by expansion, not by pattern memory alone.
  • What sign pattern is forced by a negative constant?
