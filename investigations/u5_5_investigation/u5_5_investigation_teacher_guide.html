Algebra 1 · Section 5.5 Investigation Teacher Guide

Three Forms, One Parabola

Comparing Quadratic Forms

Why This Fits

Students compare forms by usefulness rather than treating conversion as an end in itself.

TimeAbout 20–30 minutes
Groupinggroups of 3
Core ideaStudents will compare standard, vertex, and factored forms of quadratic functions and determine which form best reveals key features.

Materials

form cards, feature cards, graph panel, organizer

Before Class

Prepare the listed materials and, when a generated representation is used, verify the local figure opens. Keep the task optional: students should still receive required core instruction elsewhere.

Suggested Flow

  1. Match three equivalent equation forms to the same graph.
  2. For each form, name the feature that is easiest to read: zeros, vertex, or coefficient structure/y-intercept.
  3. Use the graph to verify features claimed by the equations.
  4. Convert or expand one form enough to prove the equations are equivalent.
  5. Choose the most useful form for three different questions and justify each choice.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • All three are equivalent.
  • Factored form: zeros 1 and 5.
  • Vertex form: vertex (3,-4), axis x=3.
  • Standard form: y-intercept 5 and leading coefficient 1.

Watch For

  • Assuming one quadratic form is always best for every question.
  • Reading zeros, vertex, or intercepts from the wrong structural feature.

Strong Synthesis

Target: Use the form that exposes the needed feature with the least extra work, while recognizing all forms describe the same function.

Synthesis — student prompt

How should the question you are answering influence which quadratic form you choose to use?

Suggested / acceptable response: Use the form that exposes the needed feature with the least extra work, while recognizing all forms describe the same function.
What to listen for: Students should generalize from the investigation evidence, not repeat a procedure. Teacher press:
  • Which form answers “where is the maximum/minimum?” fastest?
  • Which conversion would prove equivalence?
