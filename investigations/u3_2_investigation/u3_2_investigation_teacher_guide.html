Algebra 1 · Section 3.2 Investigation Teacher Guide

Substitution Route Clinic

Solving Systems by Substitution

Why This Fits

Critiquing routes and errors makes substitution a strategic replacement process rather than a fixed sequence.

TimeAbout 20–30 minutes
Groupingpairs
Core ideaStudents will solve systems using substitution and choose efficient forms for solving.

Materials

student-work cards, highlighters, recording page

Before Class

Prepare the listed materials and, when a generated representation is used, verify the local figure opens. Keep the task optional: students should still receive required core instruction elsewhere.

Suggested Flow

  1. Rank three systems from easiest to hardest for substitution and justify the ranking.
  2. For each, circle the equation/variable you would isolate or substitute first.
  3. Audit two worked solutions for partial-substitution and sign errors.
  4. Repair one solution and finish it to an ordered pair.
  5. Substitute the ordered pair into both original equations as the final verification.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • System 1: substitute y=3x-2 to get 5x-2=13, x=3, y=7.
  • System 2: substitute x=4y+1 into the second equation.
  • A substitution replaces the variable with the entire equivalent expression everywhere that variable occurs; partial replacement is invalid.

Watch For

  • Substituting into only part of an expression or equation.
  • Failing to interpret the ordered pair in the original system.

Strong Synthesis

Target: Use an already-isolated variable or a variable with coefficient ±1 when possible, minimizing extra algebra while preserving equivalence.
