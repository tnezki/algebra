Directions
  • Work with a partner unless your teacher assigns a different grouping.
  • Show the evidence you use, not only the final answer.
  • When a representation changes, keep the meaning of the quantities attached to your work.
  • Revise an answer if later evidence shows that your first claim was incomplete.

Part 2 · Solve by Substitution

Solve $y=2x+1$ and $x+y=10$. Show replacement, solve the one-variable equation, then back-substitute.

Part 3 · Efficiency Rewrite

For $2x+y=8$ and $x-3y=1$, decide which variable you would isolate first. Rewrite one equation and explain your choice.

Synthesis

Write a rule for recognizing when substitution is likely to be efficient.
