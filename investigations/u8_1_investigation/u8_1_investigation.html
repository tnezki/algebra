Selecting Appropriate Models

Time
20–25 minutes
Format
Model classification + revision
Name
________________
Learning Target: Students will determine whether a situation is best modeled by a linear, quadratic, or exponential function.
Directions
  • Work with a partner unless your teacher assigns a different grouping.
  • Show the evidence you use, not only the final answer.
  • When a representation changes, keep the meaning of the quantities attached to your work.
  • Revise an answer if later evidence shows that your first claim was incomplete.

Part 2 · Ambiguous Start

Data begins 10, 13, 17, 22. Explain why a few early values may not be enough to settle the family. What additional data or evidence would you request?

Part 3 · Revise a Verdict

A new point breaks the pattern supporting your first choice. Describe what you would recheck before choosing a new model.

Synthesis

Write a hierarchy of evidence you would use to choose among linear, quadratic, and exponential models.
