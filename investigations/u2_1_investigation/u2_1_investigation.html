Part 3 · Sign and Meaning

Write one possible context for each slope: $m=3$, $m=-2$, $m=0$, and undefined. Your context must make the sign or special case meaningful.

Synthesis

A student says, “A slope of $-4$ is always steeper than a slope of $3$ because 4 is larger than 3.” Critique the claim. Separate direction from steepness.
