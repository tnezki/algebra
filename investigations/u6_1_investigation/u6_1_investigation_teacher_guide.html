Algebra 1 · Section 6.1 Investigation Teacher Guide

Factor, Solve, Graph-Check

Solving Quadratics by Factoring

Why This Fits

Graph verification gives solutions geometric meaning and exposes factoring/sign errors without replacing the algebraic method.

TimeAbout 20–30 minutes
Groupingpairs
Core ideaStudents will solve quadratic equations using factored form and interpret solutions.

Materials

quadratic cards, graph paper, reveal graphs

Before Class

Prepare the listed materials and, when a generated representation is used, verify the local figure opens. Keep the task optional: students should still receive required core instruction elsewhere.

Suggested Flow

  1. Factor a quadratic equation after moving all terms to one side.
  2. Use the zero-product property to find both solutions.
  3. Predict where the related parabola crosses the x-axis.
  4. Compare with the teacher reveal graph and diagnose any mismatch.
  5. Create one equation with two integer solutions and one with a repeated solution.

Answer / Evidence Guide

  • A factors (x-2)(x-3)=0 → x=2,3.
  • B factors (x+4)(x-2)=0 → x=-4,2.
  • Repeated root (x-3)²=0 → x=3; graph is tangent at the x-axis.

Watch For

  • Applying the zero product property before the equation is set equal to zero.
  • Keeping factor values instead of solving each factor equation for the variable.

Strong Synthesis

Target: Solutions of f(x)=0 should be exactly the x-coordinates where y=f(x) meets the x-axis. A mismatch signals algebra, graph, or equation setup error.
