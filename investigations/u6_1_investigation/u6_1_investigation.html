Solving Quadratics by Factoring

Time
18–22 minutes

Factoring + solution interpretation

Learning Target: Students will solve quadratic equations using factored form and interpret solutions.
Directions
  • Work with a partner unless your teacher assigns a different grouping.
  • Show the evidence you use, not only the final answer.
  • When a representation changes, keep the meaning of the quantities attached to your work.
  • Revise an answer if later evidence shows that your first claim was incomplete.
Part 1 · Zero Product Property

Solve each equation and state the solution set.

$(x-4)(x+2)=0$

$3x(x-5)=0$

$(2x+1)(x-3)=0$

Part 2 · Factor First

Solve $x^2-7x+12=0$ by factoring. Show the factorization before applying the zero product property.

Part 3 · Context Filter

A model for height reaches zero at $t=-1$ and $t=5$. If $t$ is time after launch, which solution is meaningful and why?

Synthesis

Why can the zero product property be used only after one side of the equation equals zero?
