Part 1 · Translate the Constraints

A club can spend at most \$120 on notebooks (\$6 each) and posters (\$10 each). It needs at least 14 total items.

Let $x$ = notebooks and $y$ = posters. Write two inequalities plus the nonnegative restrictions.

Part 2 · Build the Feasible Region

Graph the constraints on the provided plane. Label each boundary and shade only the overlap that satisfies every restriction.
