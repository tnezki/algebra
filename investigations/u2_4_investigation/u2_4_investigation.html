Part 2 · Decision Questions

Without making a full table for every input, decide which plan is smaller at $x=1$, which is smaller at $x=10$, and whether they ever have equal outputs. Show evidence.

Part 3 · Claim Check

Claim: “Plan A is always larger because its starting value is larger.” Decide whether the claim is valid. If not, explain when the comparison changes.
