Part 3 · Point Test

For $y\lt -2x+5$, determine whether $(0,0)$, $(2,1)$, and $(3,0)$ are solutions. Show substitution evidence.

Synthesis

Explain how the inequality symbol controls both the boundary style and the meaning of the shaded half-plane.
