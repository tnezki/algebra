How Fast Can You Write?

5

Use your models

  1. Predict the number of letters you could write in 90 seconds with your dominant hand.
  2. Predict the number of letters you could write in 90 seconds with your non-dominant hand.
  3. Which hand has the greater writing rate?
6

Discuss

  1. Which hand was faster? How do you know?
  2. What does the rate mean in this activity?
  3. Why are the two rates different?
  4. Did your points make a perfect line? Why or why not?
  5. Would your model work well for 5 minutes? Explain.
  6. What does the graph show that is harder to see in the table?
