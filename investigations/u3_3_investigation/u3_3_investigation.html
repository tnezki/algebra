Writing and Solving Systems from Situations

Time
20–25 minutes
Format
Context modeling + solution interpretation
Name
________________
Learning Target: Students will create and solve systems that model real-world situations.
Directions
  • Work with a partner unless your teacher assigns a different grouping.
  • Show the evidence you use, not only the final answer.
  • When a representation changes, keep the meaning of the quantities attached to your work.
  • Revise an answer if later evidence shows that your first claim was incomplete.
Part 1 · Define the Variables

A theater sold 140 tickets. Adult tickets cost \$9 and student tickets cost \$6. Revenue was \$1080.

Define two variables with units.

Write one equation for total tickets and one equation for total revenue.

Part 2 · Solve and Interpret

Solve your system using a method that fits its structure. Then write a complete sentence interpreting both coordinates.

Part 3 · Model Audit

A student uses $a+s=1080$ and $9a+6s=140$. Explain what each incorrect equation is actually adding and repair the system.
