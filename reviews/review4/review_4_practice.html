Algebra 1 — Unit 4 Review Practice

Practice the skills and understandings from your Unit 4 Mastery Goals.

Rate of Change & Reasonableness

1. A delivery route is 18 miles longer after 3 hours of driving. Determine the constant rate of change in miles per hour and explain what the sign would mean if the distance were measured from the destination instead.

2. The table shows $x=0,2,4,6$ and $y=11,17,23,29$. Find the slope, describe the constant-change pattern, and explain why the rate is reasonable for the table.

3. A line passes through $(2,9)$ and $(7,-1)$. Compute the slope two different ways by reversing the point order, and explain why the value stays the same.

4. A student says a tank filling at 4 liters per minute has slope $ rac14$ liters per minute. Evaluate the claim and correct the rate with an explanation.

Graphing & Writing Linear Equations

5. Write the equation of the line with slope $3$ and y-intercept $-5$. Then give two points you would plot to graph it.

6. The table contains $(0,7),(2,11),(4,15),(6,19)$. Write a linear equation and verify it with one nonzero input from the table.

7. A line passes through $(-3,8)$ and $(1,0)$. Find its slope, write an equation in slope-intercept form, and state the y-intercept.

8. A taxi costs $6$ to start plus $2.50$ per mile. Write a linear equation, interpret both parameters, and describe how the graph would show them.

Comparing Linear Relationships

9. Relationship A is $y=4x-3$. Relationship B contains $(0,5),(2,11),(4,17)$. Compare their slopes and y-intercepts and state which grows faster.

10. Plan A costs $12+5h$ dollars and Plan B costs $24+3h$ dollars. Compare starting costs and rates, then determine when the two plans have equal cost.

11. Two graphs use different vertical scales. Line A appears steeper, but its slope is $1.5$ while Line B has slope $2$. Explain which relationship changes faster and why visual steepness alone can mislead.

12. Relationship A starts at 10 and decreases 2 units per input. Relationship B is $y=-x+4$. Compare their rates and starting values, then determine which has the larger output at $x=8$.

Linear Models & Predictions

13. A club has 42 members at week 0 and 54 members at week 3. Build a linear model, predict membership at week 5, and explain why the prediction is reasonable if the trend continues.

14. A line of fit for a scatter plot is $y=1.8x+12$. Interpret the slope and intercept in a possible data context and make an in-range prediction at $x=10$.

15. A linear model fits data well for inputs from 0 to 20. Explain why a prediction at $x=18$ is usually more defensible than one at $x=200$, even when the equation can compute both.

16. A scatter plot has a clear upward trend but several points are far from the fitted line. Describe two pieces of evidence you would examine before deciding whether the linear model is useful for prediction.
