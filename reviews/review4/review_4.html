Rules & structures

Keep these structures ready

System solution

$y=m_1x+b_1$ and $y=m_2x+b_2$

A solution satisfies both equations.

Inequality boundary

$y\;\square\;mx+b$

Boundary type and shading must match the inequality.

Feasible region

The solution to a system of inequalities is the overlap that satisfies every constraint.

Review Focus 1

Graphing and Elimination

Back to top
Review Target

Students will solve systems graphically and by elimination while interpreting the meaning of different solution types.

I can...

  • I can construct graphical representations of systems and connect intersection points to solutions.
  • I can use elimination to solve systems and interpret the solution type.
  • I can determine whether a system has one solution, no solution, or infinitely many solutions from algebraic and graphical evidence.

What to remember

  • Connect the algebraic method to the graphical or contextual meaning.
  • Check the result in the original system or constraint.
  • Use precise boundary, solution, and variable language.
Key words:system of equationsintersectioneliminationone/no/infinitely many solutions

Common traps

Common trap
