Algebra 1 — Unit 8 Review Practice

Practice the skills and understandings from your Unit 8 Mastery Goals.

Factoring, Zeros & Meaning

1.

Solve $(x-5)(x+2)=0$. State the zeros and the x-intercepts of the related parabola.

2.

A quadratic has zeros $-4$ and $3$ and leading coefficient 1. Write a factored equation and explain how the zeros appear on its graph.

3.

Solve $x^2-7x+10=0$ by factoring. Check each solution in the original equation.

4.

A height model is $h(t)=-2(t-1)(t-6)$. Find the modeled ground-level times and state which values are meaningful if $t\ge0$.

Completing the Square

5.

Complete the square to solve $x^2+6x-7=0$.

6.

Rewrite $x^2-8x+5$ in vertex form by completing the square. State the vertex.

7.

Solve $2x^2+12x+10=0$ by completing the square. Show how equality is preserved.

8.

A parabola is $y=x^2+4x-1$. Complete the square and use the result to state the axis of symmetry and vertex.

Formula & Discriminant

9.

Use the quadratic formula to solve $2x^2-3x-4=0$. Give exact solutions.

10.

For $3x^2+2x+5=0$, compute the discriminant and predict the number and type of real solutions.

11.

Solve $x^2-10x+21=0$ with the quadratic formula, then compare the result to what factoring would give.

12.

A quadratic has $a=1$, $b=-6$, and $c=13$. Organize the coefficients, evaluate the discriminant, and interpret what it says about x-intercepts.

Choose the Best Method

13.

Choose an efficient method to solve $x^2-9x+20=0$. Solve it and justify why your method fits the equation.

14.

Choose an efficient method to solve $(x-4)^2=11$. Solve and defend your choice.

15.

A student uses the quadratic formula on $x^2-16=0$. Critique the choice for efficiency and give a better method.

16.

Compare factoring, completing the square, and the quadratic formula for $x^2+5x+1=0$. Select one method and explain why it is the best fit.

Quadratic Models & Meaning

17.

A ball is modeled by $h(t)=-4(t-3)^2+36$ for $0\le t\le6$. State the maximum height and when it occurs, then find and interpret the zeros.

18.

A rectangular garden has area $A(x)=-x^2+18x$. Find the maximum area and the value of $x$ that produces it. Explain the meaning.

19.

A profit model is $P(x)=-2x^2+40x-96$. Find the break-even values and the maximum profit. State any domain restriction needed for the context.

20.

A quadratic model has vertex $(5,42)$ and zeros $1$ and $9$. Describe what those features could mean in a context and explain how you would test whether the model is reasonable.
