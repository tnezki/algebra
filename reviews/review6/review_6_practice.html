Algebra 1 — Unit 6 Review Practice

Practice the skills and understandings from your Unit 6 Mastery Goals.

U6-MG01: Identify common function families using evidence from graphs, equations, tables, contexts, and key features.

1.

A table has constant first differences. Which familiar family is supported most strongly?

2.

A table has a constant multiplicative ratio but not constant first differences. Which familiar family is supported?

3.

Use the graph shown. Which familiar function family is the best match? Justify with one graph feature.

4.

A table has constant second differences. Which familiar family is supported, and why is “the graph is curved” weaker evidence?

U6-MG02: Represent translations, stretches, compressions, and reflections of parent functions across graphs, equations, tables, and key points.

5.

Describe the translation from $y=x^2$ to $y=(x-4)^2+3$.

6.

Write an equation for $y=x^2$ shifted left 2 and down 5.

7.

The parent point $(1,1)$ is shifted right 3 and up 2. Give the new point.

8.

Explain why $y=(x+6)^2$ shifts the graph left rather than right.

U6-MG03: Add, subtract, and multiply polynomial expressions using structure, like terms, equivalent forms, and area-based representations.

9.

Simplify $(3x^2+2x-5)+(x^2-7x+4)$.

10.

Simplify $(5x^2-x+2)-(2x^2+4x-3)$.

11.

Multiply $(x+4)(x-3)$.

12.

A rectangle has side lengths $x+2$ and $x+5$. Write its area as a polynomial.

U6-MG04: Connect polynomial and quadratic expression structure to graph features such as vertex, intercepts, symmetry, and direction of opening.

13.

For $f(x)=(x-2)(x+6)$, state the $x$-intercepts and direction of opening.

14.

A student claims $g(x)=-(x+1)^2+4$ opens upward. Critique the claim.

15.

Which feature is most visible in $h(x)=3(x-5)^2-2$? State it.

16.

Connect $p(x)=(x+4)(x-3)$ to two graph features.
