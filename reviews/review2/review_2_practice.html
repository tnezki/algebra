Section 1.5 — Equations as Models

1.

A taxi charges a 5-dollar starting fee and 3 dollars per mile. Let $m$ be miles and $C$ be cost. Write an equation that models the situation.

2.

A tank starts with 60 liters and drains 4 liters each minute. Let $t$ be minutes and $V$ be volume. Write an equation that models the situation.

3.

A student has 20 dollars and adds 6 dollars each week. Let $w$ be weeks and $S$ be savings. Write an equation that models the situation.

4.

A candle is 24 cm tall and burns down 3 cm per hour. Let $h$ be hours and $H$ be height. Write an equation that models the situation.
