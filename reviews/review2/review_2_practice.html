Algebra 1 · Unit 0B

Review Practice Problems

0B.1 Coordinate Plane and Graph Interpretation
1.

For the point $(-4,3)$, identify the $x$-coordinate, the $y$-coordinate, and the quadrant. Explain how the coordinate signs determine the quadrant.

2.

A graph has a $y$-intercept at $(0,6)$ and an $x$-intercept at $(9,0)$. State what each intercept means mathematically and describe one possible context interpretation.

3.

A line passes through $(0,2)$, $(3,8)$, and $(6,14)$. Describe the trend and explain how the points support your interpretation.

4.

Two students look at the same graph. One says the graph is increasing because it moves upward from left to right; the other says the context does not allow negative inputs. Explain how both graph features and context restrictions should be used when interpreting the model.

0B.2 Patterns and Input-Output Rules
5.

The table has inputs $0,1,2,3$ and outputs $5,8,11,14$. Write the rule in words and symbols.

6.

Use the rule $y=4x+1$ to find the outputs for $x=0,2,5$. Organize the results as ordered pairs.

7.

A table starts with output $12$ at input $0$ and decreases by $3$ for each increase of $1$ in the input. Write the first five rows of the table and a symbolic rule.

8.

A student claims the table $x:0,1,2,3$ and $y:4,7,10,14$ follows the rule “start at 4 and add 3.” Evaluate the claim and revise the table or rule so they agree.

0B.3 Linear Patterns and Rate of Change
9.

For $x=0,2,4,6$, the outputs are $3,9,15,21$. Calculate the rate of change and include units of output per input step.

10.

A relationship decreases from $18$ to $14$ when the input increases from $2$ to $4$. Determine the rate of change and explain the sign.

11.

Compare the tables $A: (0,2),(1,5),(2,8)$ and $B:(0,7),(1,9),(2,11)$. Which has the greater constant rate of change? Show evidence.

12.

A student says any table whose outputs keep increasing is linear. Critique the claim using the output pattern $2,5,10,17,26$ for equal input steps.

0B.4 Writing Linear Relationships
13.

A linear situation starts at $7$ when $x=0$ and increases by $4$ for each unit of $x$. Write an equation in the form $y=mx+b$.

14.

The table contains $(0,10),(2,16),(4,22)$. Find the rate and starting value, then write a linear equation.

15.

A line has slope $-2$ and $y$-intercept $5$. Find the outputs when $x=0,1,3$ and explain how the table reflects the equation.

16.

A parking plan costs $6$ to enter plus $3$ per hour. Write an equation, predict the cost for $5$ hours, and explain what the two parameters mean in context.

0B.5 Comparing Representations
17.

Relationship A is $y=2x+8$. Relationship B is $y=5x+1$. Compare their starting values and rates of change.

18.

Plan A starts at $20$ and increases by $3$ per month. Plan B starts at $8$ and increases by $6$ per month. Determine which is larger at month $0$ and which changes faster.

19.

A table shows a start of $4$ and repeated change $+2$. A graph shows a $y$-intercept of $4$ and rises $4$ units for every $2$ units right. Decide whether the representations match and justify with two features.

20.

A student says the line that looks steeper on a printed page must have the larger rate of change. Explain why axis scales and numerical evidence are needed before accepting that conclusion.
