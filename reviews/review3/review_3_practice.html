Algebra 1 — Unit 3 Review Practice

Practice the skills and understandings from your Unit 3 Mastery Goals.

Representations & Evidence

1.

A table has points $(0,2),(1,5),(2,8)$. Write a linear rule and cite one feature that confirms the match.

2.

A graph and equation both have y-intercept 4. What additional evidence would you use to decide whether they represent the same relationship?

3.

Create a table with three input-output pairs for $y=-2x+7$.

4.

Two representations have the same rate of change but different starting values. Explain why they do not represent the same relationship.

Functions & Family Evidence

5.

For $f(x)=3x-5$, find $f(4)$ and state the ordered pair.

6.

A relation assigns outputs $2,5,8$ to inputs $0,1,2$. Describe a rule that fits the pattern.

7.

A table doubles its output each time the input increases by 1. Which common function family is suggested, and what evidence supports the choice?

8.

Explain how you can tell whether a rule gives exactly one output for each input.

Expression Structure

9.

Rewrite $4(x+3)-2x$ as an equivalent simplified expression.

10.

Determine whether $3(x-2)+6$ and $3x$ are equivalent. Justify.

11.

Substitute $x=-2$ into $2x^2-3x+1$ and evaluate.

12.

Explain how grouping can change the appearance of an expression without changing its value.

Equations & Context

13.

Write an equation for: a fee of 12 dollars plus 4 dollars per hour totals 36 dollars.

14.

Solve $5x-7=28$ and check the solution.

15.

A solution to a ticket equation is $x=-3$. Explain why the value may be unreasonable in context.

16.

A rectangle has perimeter 34 and length $x+4$ with width $x-1$. Write and solve an equation for $x$.
