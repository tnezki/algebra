Unit 4 Review — Practice Problems

Function Families and Transformations · Open Response

4.1: Recognizing Function Families

1.

Classify $y=3(2)^x$ as linear, quadratic, exponential, or absolute value. Give one equation feature that supports your choice.

2.

A table has first differences $2,5,8$ and constant second differences. Which function family is most plausible? Explain.

3.

Compare $y=2x+1$ and $y=x^2+1$. Name the family of each and one feature that distinguishes them.

4.

A graph is V-shaped with vertex $(1,-2)$. Identify the likely family and justify from the graph feature.

4.2: Translations

5.

Describe the translation from $y=x^2$ to $y=(x-3)^2+2$.

6.

Write an equation for $y=|x|$ shifted left 4 and down 1.

7.

The point $(2,4)$ lies on a parent graph. Where does it move after a translation right 3 and up 5?

8.

A student says $y=(x+2)^2$ shifts $y=x^2$ right 2. Critique the claim.

4.3: Transformations

9.

Describe the transformations from $y=x^2$ to $y=-2x^2$.

10.

For $y=\qquad frac12|x|$, explain how the graph compares with $y=|x|$.

11.

Transform the point $(3,9)$ under a reflection across the $x$-axis.

12.

Decide whether $y=-3(x-1)^2+4$ includes a reflection, stretch/compression, and translations. Explain each.

4.4: Polynomial Operations

13.

Simplify $(3x^2+2x-5)+(x^2-7x+4)$.

14.

Simplify $(5x^2-x+3)-(2x^2+4x-6)$.

15.

Multiply $(x+4)(x-3)$.

16.

A rectangle has side lengths $x+2$ and $x+5$. Write and simplify an expression for its area.

4.5: Polynomial Structure and Quadratic Graphs

17.

For $y=(x-2)(x+5)$, identify the zeros and $x$-intercepts.

18.

For $y=(x-3)^2-4$, identify the vertex and axis of symmetry.

19.

Explain which form of a quadratic best reveals zeros and which best reveals the vertex.

20.
