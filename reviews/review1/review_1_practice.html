Algebra 1 — Unit 1 Review Practice

Practice the skills and understandings from your Unit 1 Mastery Goals.

Mastery Goal 1: Rational Numbers & Expressions

1. Fill the two side numbers so their product matches the top and their sum matches the bottom.

2. Fill the two side numbers so their product matches the top and their sum matches the bottom.

3. Evaluate $3x+5$ when $x=-2$.

4. Evaluate $2(a-4)^2$ when $a=7$.

Mastery Goal 2: Equivalent Expressions

5. Simplify $3x+5+2x-1$.

6. Expand $4(x-3)$.

7. Factor $10x+15$.

8. Rewrite $4(2x-3)+5x$ as an equivalent expression with no parentheses. Show how the distributive property preserves equivalence.

Mastery Goal 3: Solving Equations

9. Solve $x-8=15$.

10. Solve $4x+3=27$.

11. Solve $5x-2=2x+16$.

12. Solve $5(x-2)+7=22$. Show equivalent steps, then substitute your solution into the original equation to verify it.

Mastery Goal 4: Solving Inequalities

13. Solve $x+3\gt8$ and describe the number-line graph.

14. Solve $-2x\le 10$.

15. A theater has room for at most 120 people. If 87 are seated, write and solve an inequality for additional people $p$.

16. A club can spend at most \$180 on shirts after a \$36 setup fee. Shirts cost \$12 each. Write and solve an inequality for the number of shirts $s$, represent the solution set on a number line, and interpret the boundary.
