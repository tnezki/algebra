Algebra 1 — Unit 1 Review Practice

One problem for each current Unit 1 “I can” statement.

Coordinate-Plane Graphs

1. Plot and read ordered pairs.

On the coordinate plane, plot $A(-3,2)$. Point $B$ is already shown. Write the ordered pair for $B$.

2. Use the x- and y-axes correctly.

A graph shows minutes practiced and number of correct free throws. Which quantity should go on the x-axis and which should go on the y-axis? Explain your choice in one sentence.

3. Read a graph using its scale.

The y-axis below increases by 5 each grid step. What y-value is represented by point $P$?

4. Identify and interpret intercepts when they are shown.

A line crosses the y-axis at $(0,6)$ and the x-axis at $(9,0)$. State both intercepts. Then explain what the x-intercept would mean if $x$ were time in minutes and $y$ were liters of water remaining in a tank.

Slope in Four Views

5. Find slope from a graph.

7. Find slope from an equation.

Find the slope of $y=-3x+7$. Explain how you located the slope in the equation.

8. Find and interpret rate of change from a context.

A water tank contains 240 liters at 2:00 p.m. and 192 liters at 2:06 p.m. Find the rate of change in liters per minute and explain what the sign of the rate means.

Evaluate Expressions

9. Substitute a value for a variable.

Evaluate $3x-7$ when $x=-4$. Show the substitution step before simplifying.

10. Use order of operations correctly.

Evaluate $18-3(2^2)+8\div4$.

11. Evaluate expressions with positive and negative numbers.

Evaluate $2a^2-3b$ when $a=-2$ and $b=5$.

12. Check whether an answer is reasonable.

A student evaluates $5n+2$ for $n=6$ and reports $302$. Without redoing every step first, explain why $302$ is not reasonable. Then find the correct value.
