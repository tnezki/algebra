Model the Options

You must write a method-selection guide for \(x^2-16=0\), \(x^2+7x+10=0\), \(x^2+6x-7=0\), and \(2x^2+3x-4=0\).
Sample standard: both alternatives are modeled on comparable quantities/units before the student chooses.
The recommendation must follow from a breakpoint, feasible region, maximum/minimum, or other relevant feature—not preference alone.
A strong response states a limitation or condition under which the recommendation could change.
Section-specific sample: A strong guide chooses square-root reasoning or factoring for \(x^2-16=0\), factoring for \(x^2+7x+10=0\), completing the square or formula for \(x^2+6x-7=0\), and usually the quadratic formula for \(2x^2+3x-4=0\).
