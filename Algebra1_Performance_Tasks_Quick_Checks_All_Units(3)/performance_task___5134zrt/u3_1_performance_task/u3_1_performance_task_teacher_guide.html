Audit the Evidence

Read the evidence packet below. Identify the exact mismatch or unsupported claim. Record what is valid before you change anything.
The system \(y=2x+1\) and \(y=-x+7\) is graphed with intersection labeled \((3,7)\). An elimination page also claims the solution is \((2,5)\).
Look for: an explicitly named mismatch, not just “this is wrong.”
The repair must satisfy the original representation/constraint and be checked with substitution, points, or another representation.
A strong defense cites section evidence tied to: I can construct graphical representations of systems and connect intersection points to solutions.
Section-specific sample: The correct intersection is \((2,5)\): both \(y=2x+1\) and \(y=-x+7\) give 5 when \(x=2\). The labeled \((3,7)\) is the mismatch.
