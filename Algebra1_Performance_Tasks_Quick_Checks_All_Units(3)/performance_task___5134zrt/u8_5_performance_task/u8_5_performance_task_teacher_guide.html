Model the Options

A growing service can be modeled over an observed interval by \(L(t)=12+5t\) or \(E(t)=12(1.18)^t\). The team must choose a model for short-range scheduling and long-range capacity planning.
Sample standard: both alternatives are modeled on comparable quantities/units before the student chooses.
The recommendation must follow from a breakpoint, feasible region, maximum/minimum, or other relevant feature—not preference alone.
A strong response states a limitation or condition under which the recommendation could change.
Section-specific sample: For short intervals the linear and exponential models may be similar; long-run behavior diverges. A strong recommendation explicitly separates short-range fit from long-range capacity risk and supports the choice with a comparison table/graph.
