Writing Exponential Equations

Section 7.2
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can build exponential equations using an initial value and a growth or decay factor.
  2. I can use exponential equations to predict values from tables, graphs, and situations.
  3. I can formulate exponential equations that represent repeated percent increase or decrease.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

A quantity begins at 100 and changes by 10% each period (increase). Write an exponential equation in the form \(y=a(b)^x\).

2

Use your equation to predict the value after 3 periods.

3

State the initial value and growth/decay factor separately, and explain how the 10% change becomes the factor.
