Completing the Square

Section 6.2
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can build perfect-square trinomials by adding the needed constant term.
  2. I can use completing the square to solve quadratic equations and write equivalent vertex-form expressions.
  3. I can predict how completing the square reveals the vertex and symmetry of a quadratic function.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Complete the square for \(x^2+4x\) by identifying the constant that creates a perfect-square trinomial.

2

Solve \(x^2+4x=1\) by completing the square. Show the same addition on both sides.

3

Rewrite \(y=x^2+4x+1\) in vertex form and state the vertex revealed by the completed square.
