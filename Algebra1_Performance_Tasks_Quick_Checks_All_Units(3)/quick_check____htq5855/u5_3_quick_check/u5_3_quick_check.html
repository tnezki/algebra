Special Factoring Patterns

Section 5.3
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can design a strategy for factoring special polynomial patterns.
  2. I can apply difference-of-squares and perfect-square-trinomial patterns.
  3. I can assess whether a polynomial fits a special factoring pattern before rewriting it.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Factor the difference of squares \(x^2-4\).

2

Determine whether \(x^2+4x+4\) is a perfect-square trinomial. If so, factor it and identify the repeated binomial.

3

Explain why \(x^2+4\) is a look-alike but not a difference-of-squares pattern over the real numbers.
