Polynomial Operations

Section 4.4
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can formulate polynomial sums, differences, and products from area, perimeter, and expression contexts.
  2. I can use structure and like terms to add, subtract, and multiply polynomial expressions.
  3. I can extend polynomial operations to equivalent expressions written in different forms.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Add and simplify: \((2x^2+3x+1)+(1x^2-2x+4)\).

2

Multiply \(2x(1x+2)\) using distribution and combine like terms.

3

Use an area/generic rectangle model to multiply \((x+3)(x+2)\). Write the equivalent polynomial.
