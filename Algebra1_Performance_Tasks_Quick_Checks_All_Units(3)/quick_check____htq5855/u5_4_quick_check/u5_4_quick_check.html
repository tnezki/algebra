Factored Form and Zeros

Section 5.4
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can formulate quadratic functions in factored form from zeros or intercepts.
  2. I can use factored form to predict zeros and x-intercepts of quadratic graphs.
  3. I can extend connections among factors, zeros, intercepts, and graph behavior.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

For \(y=(x+1)(x-2)\), identify the zeros and x-intercepts.

2

A quadratic has x-intercepts \((-1,0)\) and \((2,0)\) and passes through \((0,6)\). Write a factored model with a scale factor and determine that factor.

3

Explain how the zero-product property connects the factors to graph crossings.
