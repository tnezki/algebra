Function Synthesis and Capstone Analysis

Section 8.5
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can construct a solution pathway that connects equations, graphs, tables, and contexts across function families.
  2. I can use multiple representations to solve complex function problems and interpret results.
  3. I can assess competing solution strategies and choose the representation that best supports the conclusion.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

A context has a table with constant first differences for early inputs but a graph that begins to curve upward later. State what additional evidence you would use before choosing a function family.

2

Compare the models \(L(x)=4x+8\) and \(E(x)=8(1.25)^x\) at \(x=0,4,8\). Use a table or calculations to decide which grows faster by the end of the interval.

3

Two students reach different model conclusions from the same data: one emphasizes early values, the other long-run behavior. Assess the competing strategies and choose the representation that best supports your final conclusion.
