Algebra 1 • Quick Check

Choosing and Defending Solution Methods

Section 6.4
Name:
Date:
Evidence Focus
  1. I can formulate a solution-method plan based on the structure of a quadratic equation.
  2. I can use factoring, completing the square, or the quadratic formula when the equation structure supports the method.
  3. I can critique a selected solution method for efficiency, accuracy, and fit to the equation.

Work independently. Show enough mathematics, representations, and interpretation to make your thinking judgeable.

1

Choose the most efficient method for \(x^2-9=0\) and explain what structural feature drives your choice.

2

Choose and use a method to solve \(x^2+7x+12=0\).

3

A classmate uses the quadratic formula for \(x^2+6x-2=0\). Critique whether that is valid and whether another method is more efficient.
