Teacher Key

Portfolio Baseline Exit Tickets

Keys below are for the local Version 1 source sets. Versions 2–4 are direct parallels/variations of these evidence roles.

Portfolio Baseline 1

  1. Anchor: Solve $6x=42$. Show enough work to make your reasoning clear.
    Answer: $x=7$.
  2. Parallel A: Compute $\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{3}{5}$ and simplify.
    Answer: $\frac25$.
  3. Parallel B: Point $A=(-3,2)$. State its $x$-coordinate, $y$-coordinate, and quadrant.
    Answer: $x=-3$, $y=2$, Quadrant II.
  4. Variation: Two numbers have product 24 and sum 10. Find the numbers.
    Answer: 4 and 6.

Portfolio Baseline 2

  1. Anchor: A cube pattern has 6 cubes at Stage 0 and adds 3 cubes each stage. How many cubes are in Stage 5? Explain your reasoning.
    Answer: 21 cubes.
  2. Parallel A: Solve $x+7=19$.
    Answer: $x=12$.
  3. Parallel B: Compute $\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{2}{5}$ and simplify.
    Answer: $\frac{3}{10}$.
  4. Variation: Point $B=(3,-4)$. Explain how to move from the origin to plot $B$.
    Answer: 3 right, 4 down.

Portfolio Baseline 3

  1. Anchor: Plot $P=(-4,3)$ on the coordinate plane and label it.
    Answer: Point at $(-4,3)$, Quadrant II.
  2. Parallel A: Solve $\frac{x}{5}=7$.
    Answer: $x=35$.
  3. Parallel B: Compute $\frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{3}{5}$ and simplify.
    Answer: $\frac25$.
  4. Variation: If you are confused by an exit-ticket question and cannot finish it, what should you write instead of leaving it blank?
    Answer: Do not leave it blank. Show what you understand, identify where you became stuck, and explain what you would try next or what you need clarified.
