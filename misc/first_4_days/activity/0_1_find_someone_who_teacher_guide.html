Teacher Guide

Find Someone Who — Get Acquainted

Teacher: Keep the emphasis on explaining and meeting classmates, not racing. Ask students to compare methods before initials are added.
Students: Solve, explain, listen, compare, and move to a new partner.
1. Compute $\frac{3}{4}\cdot\frac{2}{9}$.
Answer: $\frac16$
2. Compute $\frac{5}{6}+\frac{1}{3}$.
Answer: $\frac76$ or $1\frac16$
3. Solve $x+9=22$.
Answer: $x=13$
4. Solve $8x=56$.
Answer: $x=7$
5. State the quadrant containing $(-2,5)$.
Answer: Quadrant II
6. State the quadrant containing $(4,-3)$.
Answer: Quadrant IV
7. Two numbers have product 20 and sum 9. Find them.
Answer: 4 and 5
8. Two numbers have product 32 and sum 12. Find them.
Answer: 4 and 8
9. Compute $\frac{7}{10}\cdot\frac{5}{14}$.
Answer: $\frac14$
10. Compute $\frac{3}{8}+\frac{1}{4}$.
Answer: $\frac58$
11. Solve $x-6=-11$.
Answer: $x=-5$
12. Solve $\frac{x}{3}=8$.
Answer: $x=24$
13. Give an ordered pair in Quadrant III.
Answer: many answers, e.g. $(-2,-3)$
14. Give an ordered pair on the $y$-axis.
Answer: many answers, e.g. $(0,4)$
15. Two numbers have product 42 and sum 13. Find them.
Answer: 6 and 7
16. Two numbers have product 16 and sum 8. Find them.
Answer: 4 and 4
