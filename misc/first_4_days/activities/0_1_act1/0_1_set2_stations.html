Problem 1

As class begins, what should you start working on when the bell rings?

Answer: Begin the posted warm-up right away and get the day's materials ready.

Problem 2

A typical section is organized across how many class days?

Answer: Three class days.

Problem 3

Describe the main job of Day 1, Day 2, and Day 3 in our usual section routine.

Answer: Three class days: Day 1 focuses on notes and initial practice; Day 2 uses an investigation with continued practice; Day 3 uses an activity/continued practice and gathers evidence such as an exit ticket.

Problem 4

State the learning target we used during our first week of Algebra 1.

Answer: Access, organize, and use Algebra 1 course materials while practicing the complete class routine with familiar mathematics.

Problem 5

Suppose you are stuck on a question. What can you write so your answer still shows useful thinking?

Answer: Do not leave it blank. Show what you understand, identify where you became stuck, and explain what you would try next or what you need clarified.
