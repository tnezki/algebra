Teacher Guide

What's To Come — Quadratic Function Preview

A deliberately difficult long-range preview designed to open the door to mathematical discourse, not to preteach quadratics.

Teacher moves: Give quiet notice time first. Ask “What words do you recognize?”, “What might maximum mean here?”, “What does the notation seem to be doing?”, and “What evidence supports that guess?” Revoice useful partial ideas without formalizing procedures. Invite students to compare interpretations and revise language.
Student moves: Notice, predict, explain, restate a classmate’s idea, compare interpretations, mark unfamiliar vocabulary, and ask questions.

Function

$$f(x)=-x^2+6x-5$$

Teacher reference

  • Maximum value: $4$ at $x=3$.
  • $x$-intercepts: $(1,0)$ and $(5,0)$.
  • $y$-intercept: $(0,-5)$.
  • Domain: all real numbers.
  • Range: $y\le 4$.
  • Increasing for $x<3$; decreasing for $x>3$.
  • $f(x)=3$ at $x=2$ and $x=4$.
Do not turn this into a lesson. The success criterion is that students recognize some words, make sense of some notation, hear classmates reason, and leave with questions they will later be able to answer precisely.
