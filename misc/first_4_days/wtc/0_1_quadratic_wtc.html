Quadratic Function Preview

This is supposed to look harder than today’s work. Do not worry about finishing every part. Notice the notation and vocabulary you recognize, make reasonable attempts, and record questions.

Consider the function

$$f(x)=-x^2+6x-5$$

  1. Does this function have a maximum or a minimum? What do you think that word is asking you to find?
  2. Find the x-intercepts and the y-intercept, or describe what you think those terms mean if you are not sure how to calculate them.
  3. State the domain and range, or write what you think those words might describe.
  4. Where is the function increasing? Where is it decreasing?
  5. Solve $f(x)=3$. What do you think the notation $f(x)$ means?
It is okay not to know yet. Circle vocabulary you recognize. Put a ? beside language or notation you want to understand by the end of Algebra 1.
